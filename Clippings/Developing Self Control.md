Mastering your emotional nature is not an option. It is a necessity. And mastering emotions does not mean controlling emotion.  
It means understanding them so that you can master them and use them to create the conditions of self-control. You learn how to work using emotions to create the mindset that is controlled, patient, and aware. Awareness is key. Come learn how to use awareness to stop emotions from taking over.
