In this video I discuss the "twitter model" from the MMXM trader. In this model I talk about looking for an important liquidity level - PDH / PDL and framing a trade to an hourly fair value gap (FVG). This is trading ERL to IRL, or external liquidity to internal liquidity. The setup includes a m15 set and mss for an entry model. This model is fractal and I explain that in the video with an example starting at the weekly chart instead of the daily chart. I hope you enjoy
