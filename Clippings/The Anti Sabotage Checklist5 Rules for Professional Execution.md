Self-sabotage isn't a willpower problem.  
It's a PATTERN problem.  
  
Patterns don't need willpower to break.  
They need INTERRUPTION.  
  
5 rules that create friction between impulse and action:  
  
1\. OBSERVER DELAY (10-min no-touch rule)  
Open terminal → Don't touch mouse for 10 min  
Dopamine wave fades → Hunter mode → Observer mode  
First 30 seconds ≠ After 10 minutes  
  
2\. PRE-MORTEM (Assume failure before entry)  
"This trade stopped me out. Why was I wrong?"  
No shock when it happens = No revenge trading  
Normalize losing BEFORE you enter  
  
3\. PHYSICAL ANCHOR (Break autopilot loop)  
Before trade → Stand up / deep breath / hand slap  
Autopilot: See → Feel → Click  
NEW: See → Stand → Pause → Decide  
  
4\. ONE BULLET THEORY (Trade like it's your last shot)  
Stop acting like you have infinite chances  
Sniper with one bullet = Only perfect setups  
Safety net removed = Reckless behavior stops  
  
5\. AUDITOR TEST (Would you take this live?)  
"Could I explain this trade to 1000 people watching?"  
If answer = NO → Don't take it  
Can't explain to others = Don't understand it yourself
