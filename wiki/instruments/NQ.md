# NQ

The full-size Nasdaq-100 futures contract. In this vault it is the main review instrument for the 2025 lecture-series clips, and it is the parent market for the MNQ execution notes ([[sources/nq-review-april-24-2025]] @ 0:20, [[sources/nq-review-april-28-2025]] @ 0:28, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 0:34).

## ICT doctrine applied to NQ

NQ uses the same ICT framework as other index futures: [[concepts/fair-value-gap|FVGs]], [[models/silver-bullet|Silver Bullet]], [[concepts/liquidity|liquidity]], [[concepts/new-week-opening-gap|new week opening gaps]], [[timing/macros|macro windows]], and premium/discount framing all show up repeatedly in the review clips ([[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 6:21, [[sources/nq-ny-lunch-macro-02242025]] @ 0:32, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 1:43).

## Recurring source-set themes

- Daily charts often anchor to a **premium wick consequent encroachment** and a **daily volume imbalance** as the higher-time-frame draw ([[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 1:43, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 1:43).
- The market frequently uses the **first presented FVG** inside a defined session window as the working entry array. The clearest ICT-direct statement is the May 18 rule: use the first valid 1-minute FVG between 9:31 and 10:00 for the AM session, and repeat the same idea for the 1:30–2:00 PM session ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-when-930am-et-is-1st-presented-fvg-may-18-2025]] @ 8:29, @ 12:01, @ 43:48, @ 47:42).
- **New day opening gaps** and **new week opening gaps** are reused across the same session, often alongside opening-range consequent encroachment and the first-presented-FVG logic ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-presession-trade-may-15-2025]] @ 30:40, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-may-29-2025]] @ 4:54, @ 6:49, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-when-930am-et-is-1st-presented-fvg-may-18-2025]] @ 12:46, @ 14:03).
- When initial bias is unclear, ICT says to wait for the first hour of trading, mark the high and low from the 9:30 open, and work the upper half if bullish / lower half if the market rolls over ([[sources/how-do-i-engage-markets-when-i-dont-have-an-initial-bias]] @ 6:08, @ 6:41, @ 7:15, @ 12:22).
- **Sell-side liquidity sweeps** of London lows, prior-day lows, or obvious local lows frequently precede continuation or validation of the next array ([[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 5:56, [[sources/nq-heavy-manipulation-review]] @ 13:09, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 5:56).
- Macro timing matters: 10:00 news, 10:50–11:10 lunch macro, and 2:50–3:10 / 3:15–3:45 execution windows repeatedly frame the tradeable move ([[sources/nq-ny-lunch-macro-02242025]] @ 0:32, [[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 7:02, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 10:51).
