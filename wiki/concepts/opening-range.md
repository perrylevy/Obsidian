# Opening Range

The **opening range** is the price span from the **opening price** to the **high of the day** (bearish context) or from the **opening price** to the **low of the day** (bullish context). It defines where **premium arrays** (bearish) or **discount arrays** (bullish) form in the early session.

## Definition

**Bearish opening range**:
- Range = High of day − Opening price.
- Subtract this range from the opening price to find the **optimal short entry zone**.
- All premium PD arrays (FVGs, order blocks, breaker blocks) reside within this range.

**Bullish opening range** (reversed):
- Range = Opening price − Low of day.
- Add this range to the opening price to find the **optimal long entry zone**.
- All discount PD arrays reside within this range.

## Key principle

> "Every single one of them will reside and form in the range between the open and its high" — ICT, 2022 Mentorship Episode 10

The opening range is not a prediction tool; it is a **placement framework** for identifying where institutional arrays are likely to form after the open.

## Relationship to daily bias

The opening range refines the daily bias execution:
- **Bearish daily bias** → opening range defines the premium zone where short entries form.
- **Bullish daily bias** → opening range defines the discount zone where long entries form.
- The opening range helps the trader **anticipate** where the FVG / order block will print, not chase it after it forms.

## Relationship to Power of 3

The opening range can sit inside a PO3 read, but it is not the same thing as PO3.

In a bearish day, the opening range often describes the area price uses to build short exposure before the manipulation leg returns to that area and the session expands lower. In a bullish day, the same idea flips.

Use PO3 to understand the delivery scaffold; use the opening range to locate the practical price band where arrays may form.

## Timeframe context

The opening range concept applies to:
- **Daily chart** — the range from daily open to daily high/low.
- **Session opens** — 8:30 AM, 9:30 AM, 1:30 PM.
- **Any opening price** — midnight open, 8:30 open, 7:00 AM open.

The 8:30 opening price is particularly significant because it follows the economic calendar news embargo and often sets the morning's directional thesis.
