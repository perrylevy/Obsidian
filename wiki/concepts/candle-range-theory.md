# Candle Range Theory

Candle range theory is the read of a single candlestick as its own price range from high to low. In the survey source, it is described as focusing on the price range of one candle rather than on a multi-candle structure ([[sources/every-ict-trading-strategy-explained-in-13-minutes]] @ 6:57).

## Core idea

The candle itself becomes the working range:

- high to low is the relevant span,
- the candle can be read as a compact execution frame,
- and the range can be used as a small-scale reference for delivery, rejection, or continuation.

## Why it matters

CRT gives a shorthand for candle-level microstructure when the broader swing or session context is already established.

It sits naturally beside [[concepts/candle-science]] and the previous-candle pages as a way to read one bar as a meaningful range.

## Practical use

Use candle range theory when:

- the candle itself is the main unit of analysis,
- you care about the candle’s full high-to-low span,
- and you want a compact framework for a local move or reversal.

The 9 AM CRT source applies the same logic in a session frame: wait for the 8:00 a.m. candle to close, then use the 15-minute purge / Turtle Soup plus lower-timeframe confirmation to refine the entry ([[sources/why-the-9-am-crt-model-could-change-your-life]] @ 1:35, @ 3:11, @ 4:48, @ 7:45).
