# 8:30 Opening Price

The **8:30 opening price** is the 8:30 a.m. New York time open used in several derivative ICT-style models as a premium / discount anchor and a filter for whether the market is expensive or cheap relative to the morning session.

## Core idea

The 8:30 open is not the same thing as the midnight open.

Episode 16 uses it explicitly as one of the two opening-price references that matter for manipulation and bias confirmation.
Episode 17 applies it in a forex model by comparing the 8:30 open against the midnight open inside a bearish / bullish Power of Three framework, with the forum summary noting that exits should often be staged a few pips before round-number institutional levels.

It is a later opening reference, often used to judge:

- whether price is in deep premium or discount,
- whether shorts or longs are favored in a given model,
- and whether the market has already reset liquidity before the 9:30 cash open.

Lecture 4 of the 2024 mentorship adds a timing nuance: even when the report is not especially dramatic, 8:30 is still a real spooling checkpoint, and the side with the stacked NDOG/NWOG inefficiencies becomes the more likely draw ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-4]]). The 2022 mentorship Episode 10 adds the **economic calendar context**: 8:30 AM ET is when the news embargo lifts, and multiple high-impact events (red on forexfactory.com) enter the marketplace simultaneously, creating the displacement that sets the morning's directional thesis ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-10]]).

## Why it matters

In the derivative material, the 8:30 open often sits next to the midnight open as part of a simple opening-price checklist.

Common uses:

- above 8:30 open = expensive / premium in some models,
- below 8:30 open = cheap / discount in some models,
- and 8:30 can act as an opposing-run / Judas-swing anchor before the main session expands.

## Relationship to midnight opening price

Use the midnight opening price and 8:30 opening price together when a model explicitly uses both anchors.

The typical logic is:

- above both = deep premium,
- beneath both = deep discount,
- between them = transitional area where confirmation matters more.

The same clip distinguishes between a tier 1 and tier 2 long framework using the 8:30 and midnight opening prices: beneath both opening prices is the deep-discount / tier 2 pocket, while between them is the more transitional tier 1 zone ([[sources/nq-trade-breakdown-how-to-pick-the-right-first-presented-fvg-ict-concepts]]).
