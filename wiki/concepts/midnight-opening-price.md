# Midnight Opening Price

The **midnight opening price** is the price at 00:00 New York time. It is a separate concept from the midnight opening range, but it is usually used together with that range.

## Core idea

The midnight opening range is a 30-minute window. The midnight opening price is the exact open that starts that window.

Episode 16 treats the midnight open as a distinct reference point alongside the 8:30 open.
Episode 17 uses the midnight open as the companion anchor for the 8:30 open in a forex application, where the relative position of those two opens helps determine whether the session is in a bullish or bearish Power of Three setup.

That distinction matters because the open itself is often used as:

- a premium / discount reference,
- a bias filter,
- a runner target,
- or a support / resistance marker for the session.

The same clip uses MOP as part of a deeper-discount / runner-target structure: beneath both the midnight and 8:30 opens is the tier-2-style pocket, and the midnight open becomes the target/runner reference after the reclaim ([[sources/nq-trade-breakdown-how-to-pick-the-right-first-presented-fvg-ict-concepts]]).

In practice, MOP is a separate concept from the range, but the two are tightly linked.

## Relationship to midnight opening range

Use the midnight opening range to measure the 00:00–00:30 window.

Use the midnight opening price to anchor the window and judge whether price is trading in premium or discount relative to that anchor.
