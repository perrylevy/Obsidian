# New Week Opening Gap

The **new week opening gap** is the gap / displacement at the start of a new trading week that becomes an active reference level for the rest of that week and often the next session. In the May 15 / May 29 reviews it sits beside the new day opening gap and the first presented FVG as part of the opening scaffold ([[sources/nq-review-april-28-2025]] @ 5:39, @ 8:35, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 16:10, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 23:08, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-presession-trade-may-15-2025]] @ 30:40, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-may-29-2025]] @ 4:54, @ 6:49). In the February 03, 2025 Monday review, the NWOG is explicitly overlaid with the opening-range gap so the two gaps can be traded as one confluence scaffold instead of two separate visuals. The January 27, 2025 GBP/USD lecture shows the same weekly scaffold on Forex, where NWOG + inversion FVG + CE become the 10:00 Silver Bullet short frame ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-smc-new-york-nwog-with-forex-01272025]] @ 0:30, @ 1:02, @ 3:15, @ 6:25, @ 6:58).

The 12/01/2024 new-week commentary shows the weekly gap as the live weekly frame: price can work the upper or lower quadrant, use consequent encroachment as the reaction point, and then flip into inversion-FVG / relative-equal-high-low logic for the rest of the week ([[sources/new-week-commentary-for-nq-futures-december-01-2024]]).

The 02/23/2026 SMC lecture folds NWOG into the live Monday opening scaffold: the 9:30 open and 30-minute opening range are expected to probe buy side, then sell side, before the real move reveals itself ([[sources/ict-2026-smart-money-concepts-lecture-february-23-2026]] @ 16:20, @ 19:13, @ 21:49).

Episode 34 of the 2022 mentorship sharpens the same weekly-gapping idea: the Sunday gap opening is extended through the entire week, price can run away from it for long stretches, and the level still matters as a dynamic support/resistance scaffold when the market returns to it ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-34-using-the-sunday-gap-opening]]).

## How it behaves in the NQ reviews

- It can act like a **draw on liquidity** or "black hole" that pulls price back to itself after the session has moved away ([[sources/nq-review-april-28-2025]] @ 8:35, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 16:10).
- The 12/01/2024 commentary shows the weekly gap working as an actual weekly map: upper quadrant, lower quadrant, consequent encroachment, and inversion-FVG continuation/reversal all live off the same scaffold ([[sources/new-week-commentary-for-nq-futures-december-01-2024]]).
- Price can revisit it multiple times in the same day, using it first as a target, then as resistance, then as a support / retest level ([[sources/nq-review-april-28-2025]] @ 7:01, @ 15:24, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 23:08).
- It is most useful when paired with consequent encroachment, the new day opening gap, first presented FVGs, old opening-range gaps, and the daily premium/discount frame ([[sources/nq-review-april-28-2025]] @ 7:01, @ 8:35, [[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 13:19, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-when-930am-et-is-1st-presented-fvg-may-18-2025]] @ 43:05).

## Practical use

The NWOG is not merely a gap-fill curiosity; in these clips it becomes a live execution reference that can:

- define the day’s directional bias,
- mark a precision target,
- and provide a retest level once the market returns from a macro move.

Lecture 6 of the 2024 mentorship adds a Monday-specific rule: if Friday’s TGIF-style retracement did not materialize and Monday opens above the weekly gap with the NWOG / older NDOGs sitting below price, Monday can become the deferred retracement session into those opening gaps instead ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-6]]).
Lecture 16 of the 2024 mentorship adds a further live filter: NWOGs older than about 60 days are treated as low-interest for this specific opening-gap routine, and when a NWOG sits in close proximity to a NDOG the midpoint between them becomes the event-horizon draw ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-16]] @ 32:27, @ 32:48, [[deferred/concepts/event-horizon]]).

The January 27, 2025 GBP/USD NWOG lecture is a direct live example: the weekly gap becomes the working frame, the 10:00 Silver Bullet window provides the execution pocket, and the remaining half of the NWOG is treated as the live imbalance after the lower half has already been worked ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-smc-new-york-nwog-with-forex-01272025]]).
The 09/01/2024 top-down review sharpens the practical definition: the weekly open defines the NWOG on a one-minute chart, while the weekly chart only supplies bias. In that session the market opens in the lower half of the weekly inefficiency, trades down to the order-block opening price / consequent encroachment, and then rebounds higher after the gap has been used as the live reference ([[sources/top-down-trade-review-september-01-2024]] @ 12:06, @ 25:52, @ 33:43).
