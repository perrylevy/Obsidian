# Discount / Premium

**Premium** is the upper half of a reference range; **discount** is the lower half. The midpoint is the equilibrium / consequent-encroachment line used to separate the two halves ([[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 6:21, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 3:00, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 20:21).

## Core rule

- When price is **above** the 50% line, the market is trading in **premium** ([[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 6:21, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 3:00).
- When price is **below** the 50% line, the market is trading in **discount** ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 20:41, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 6:16).
- The reference range can be a daily wick, a dealing range, an opening range, or a broader PD array matrix frame ([[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 4:31, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 1:43, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 20:21).

## How NQ reviews use it

- In the April 30 and May 1 reviews, the market is framed as buying from **discount** and selling from **premium wick** / daily imbalance extremes ([[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 6:16, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 6:16).
- A bullish IFVG or [[deferred/concepts/gray-pool|gray-pool]] entry is only attractive when it still sits inside the lower half of the working frame, not after price has already run too far into premium ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 11:59, @ 20:41).
- Consequent encroachment is the midpoint / equilibrium marker that operationalizes the discount-premium split on a specific wick or range ([[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 6:21, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 3:00).
