# Low-Resistance Liquidity

Low-resistance liquidity is the liquidity pool that price can reach with the least friction — the unswept side of the market that forms a cleaner draw on liquidity. In the wiki’s current usage, it is closely tied to a **failure swing**: a swing high followed by a lower high, or a swing low followed by a higher low, where the original extreme was never swept ([[concepts/liquidity]], [[concepts/draw-on-liquidity]], [[sources/hrlr-vs-lrlr]] @ 0:37, @ 1:00, [[sources/hrlr-vs-lrlr]] @ 37–40).

## Core idea

If high-resistance liquidity is the post-sweep / more obstructed side, low-resistance liquidity is the side price is trying to target because it has not yet been consumed.

The pair is complementary:
- [[concepts/high-resistance-liquidity]] names the already-swept / obstructed side,
- this page names the cleaner side price is trying to reach.

The derivative-teacher framing is explicit:
- low-resistance liquidity is a failure swing,
- target low-resistance liquidity,
- and use high-resistance liquidity as the stop side / the side of the curve that is already post-sweep ([[sources/hrlr-vs-lrlr]] @ 1:42, [[sources/hrlr-vs-lrlr]] @ 103–105).

The 10/28/2024 mentorship lecture adds the session-selection nuance: on a quiet Monday, the premarket can be the lower-resistance opportunity while the 9:30 open becomes the higher-resistance side to avoid forcing ([[sources/ict-2024-mentorship-high-resistance-low-resistance-conditions-october-28-2024]] @ 6:34, @ 8:53, @ 12:38, @ 14:52).

## How it behaves

Low-resistance liquidity often shows up as:
- a clean unswept swing extreme,
- a series of stacked failure swings on the path to the target,
- or a higher-time-frame pool that aligns with the path of least resistance ([[concepts/low-resistance-liquidity-run]], [[concepts/failure-swing]], [[sources/hrlr-vs-lrlr]] @ 51–57, @ 89–95).

When the path to the pool is unobstructed, the market tends to deliver through it in a [[concepts/low-resistance-liquidity-run|low-resistance run]].

## Practical use

Use low-resistance liquidity to:
- identify the preferred target side of the curve,
- distinguish the clean target from the post-sweep side,
- and frame bias with a specific pool instead of a vague directional guess.

This is why the liquidity hub and DOL page treat it as a higher-quality target when the path is clear ([[concepts/liquidity]], [[concepts/draw-on-liquidity]], [[sources/internal-external-liquidity]] @ 8:06, [[sources/hrlr-vs-lrlr]] @ 1:42).
