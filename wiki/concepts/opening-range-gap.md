# Opening Range Gap

The **opening range gap** is the gap between the prior regular-trading-hours settlement and the current session's 9:30 a.m. open. In the 2025 lecture-series intro, ICT treats it as an active frame rather than a passive visual artifact: price can be graded against it with quadrants, consequent encroachment, and later macro windows ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-making-money-with-smc-concepts]] @ 18:21, @ 23:24, @ 24:37, @ 57:03; [[sources/2025-lecture-series-algorithmic-timing-and-journaling-03222025]] @ 42:01, @ 48:46, @ 54:11; [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-when-930am-et-is-1st-presented-fvg-may-18-2025]] @ 10:06, @ 12:46, @ 13:16, @ 14:03; [[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 5:50, @ 6:21, @ 7:02; [[sources/2025-lecture-review-february-03-2025]] @ 0:44–3:31; [[sources/smc-opening-range-gaps]], [[sources/smc-trading-opening-range-gaps]], [[sources/ict-gems-opening-range-gap-secrets]], [[sources/ict-the-first-presented-fvg-in-the-opening-range]]).

## Core idea

The opening range gap gives the session its first measurable separation between yesterday's settlement and today's open. The broader time-price lecture `[[sources/understanding-time-price-the-only-thing-you-need]]` frames that same morning as a session-range ladder: PM range, London range, AM opening / opening-range-gap, and then the lunch pulse.

The 10/01/2025 NQ market review adds a layered live example: the opening-range-gap is read together with premium wick levels, a 9:31 first-presented FVG, inversion FVGs, and relative equal highs so the session can be handled as one integrated map rather than as isolated patterns ([[sources/ict-nq-futures-market-review-october-1-2025]] @ 0:44, @ 1:34, @ 4:20, @ 5:09, @ 5:45, @ 12:58, @ 13:08).

The 10/03/2024 NQ market review reinforces the same opening-frame discipline in an NFP week: the RTH opening range gap sits inside a broader weekly imbalance map, and the tape is better handled by waiting for the session to resolve instead of forcing an early read ([[sources/october-03-2024-nq-market-review]]).

The 02/13/2025 Telegram execution adds the tighter morning version of the same rule: the opening-range first-presented FVG becomes the working array, while the PPI move and consequent-encroachment management keep the trade anchored to the opening frame instead of the raw volatility burst ([[sources/nq-live-execution-using-analysis-i-shared-in-telegram-02132025]] @ 6:12, @ 9:03, @ 10:06, @ 14:43, @ 15:30).

The 03/28/2026 first-hour lesson sharpens the opening-range-gap rule by saying there is a 70% likelihood the market will try to reach the midpoint / consequent encroachment of the opening-range gap during the first 30 minutes, so the CE is a live early objective rather than a passive annotation ([[sources/ict-2026-1st-hour-dealing-range-march-28-2026]] @ 38:24).

The 03/11/2026 lunch algorithmic-theory clip carries that logic forward from the other side of the day: it uses yesterday's regular-trading-hours opening-range gap plus consequent encroachment as the practical reference for the next session, with the inefficiency right before the lunch liquidity raid acting as the carry-forward anchor ([[sources/ict-2026-new-york-lunch-algorithmic-theory]] @ 10:08, @ 11:01, @ 27:12).

The 03/22/2025 timing-and-journaling lecture makes the same idea practical: the operator should log the session open, opening range, first presented FVG, macro window, and the exact time the move completed, because those time signatures are what repeat over and over ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-algorithmic-timing-and-journaling-03222025]] @ 5:25, @ 7:42, @ 11:22, @ 12:33).

The 03/08/2026 market commentary gives a clean live NQ example of the same opening frame: the 9:30 RTH print becomes the gap anchor, a print above prior settlement is read as a premium ORG high, and the later morning reference is still judged by whether bodies remain in the correct half of the gap rather than whether wicks overshot it ([[sources/ict-2026-market-commentary-march-08-2026]] @ 10:02, @ 13:19–13:43, @ 16:05–16:42, @ 17:29–18:26).

The 03/10/2026 market commentary sharpens the carry-forward rule: the 9:30 open above prior settlement makes the ORG a premium reference, while the prior-week ORG can still act as the working discount array after price has already traded above it; ICT then reads the bodies against the upper half and uses the 7-minute-after-10 candle as the sellside discriminator ([[sources/ict-2026-market-commentary-march-10-2026]] @ 10:02, @ 13:19–13:43, @ 18:34–19:06, @ 23:33–23:54, @ 25:45–26:36).

The 09/27/2024 high-resistance-liquidity lecture makes the Friday open concrete: the 9:30 candle is still useful for context, but the model should wait for the 9:31 candle before seeking the FVG, and it treats the opening-range gap as the active frame while TGIF / relative-equal-lows define the sellside draw ([[sources/ict-2024-mentorship-how-to-identify-high-resistance-liquidity-conditions-september-27-2024]] @ 7:11, @ 8:31, @ 12:46, @ 23:21).

The 10/04/2025 forex/futures review reinforces the same rule from a cross-market angle: the prior day's RTH opening-range gap can be recreated on a CFD by using the 4:14 p.m. close and the next 9:30 a.m. open, so the gap logic is portable even when the chart feed changes ([[sources/ict-forex-futures-market-review-october-4-2025]]).

The 02/05/2025 Forex & NQ review makes the same opening-gap structure explicit on a discount open: when the market opens below prior RTH settlement, the 9:30 open is the gap low, the first move often raids sell-side before continuing higher, and the midpoint / consequent encroachment becomes the first reaction checkpoint ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-forex-nq-review-02052025]] @ 23:29, @ 25:02, @ 25:51, @ 26:21, @ 27:18, @ 38:40, @ 39:56, @ 44:27).

The 04/11/2026 weekend review makes the first-hour bias rule explicit: the opening-range gap's half-gap / consequent encroachment is the first draw inside the opening hour, and ICT says the first 30 minutes are enough to tell whether the opening gap is steering buy side or sell side. If the gap is below price, look sell side; if it is above price, look buy side; and if you do not know that half-gap level, you are guessing ([[sources/ict-2026-futures-weekend-review-april-11-2026]] @ 33:00, @ 33:46, @ 54:49, @ 55:42, @ 1:23:07).

Lecture 16 of the 2024 mentorship uses the opening-range gap together with the first-presented FVG and the event-horizon / NDOG-NWOG stack as a live NQ delivery map: when the range is large enough, the gap can be split into upper / lower quadrants and the market may continue in the gap direction first, revisit the gap later as a draw or target, or flip it into an inversion-style frame once price has delivered through it ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-16]] @ 34:07, @ 34:28, @ 35:19, @ 35:51). The September 4 tutelage clip reinforces the same morning scaffold by treating the opening-range midpoint and upper quadrant as active management levels after the first 9:31+ FVG reclaim, especially on a post-holiday / large-range day ([[sources/2024-mentorship-tutelage-september-04-2024]] @ 7:58, @ 9:31, @ 10:01, @ 15:59).

## Why it matters

This gap is useful because it sits right next to the first presented FVG logic:

- it helps define the first usable RTH frame,
- it can become the immediate target when the market wants to rebalance,
- and it gives the trader a structured way to judge whether the move is still extending or already turning.
