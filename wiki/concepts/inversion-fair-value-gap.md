# Inversion Fair Value Gap (IFVG)

The **post-violation state** of a [[concepts/fair-value-gap|FVG]] — the polarity flip.

The January 27, 2025 GBP/USD NWOG lecture gives a clean live example: a 5-minute IFVG is read on a 1-minute chart, bodies stay in the lower half / near CE, and the gap then resolves lower into the next draw on liquidity ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-smc-new-york-nwog-with-forex-01272025]] @ 3:15, @ 5:15, @ 6:25, @ 6:58, @ 10:52). The Trump-volatility NQ trade breakdown uses the same reclaim logic in a bullish AM context: a daily discount-wick / upper-half frame, a 9:30 Judas swing lower, then a reclaimed IFVG back toward buy-side liquidity ([[sources/nq-trade-breakdown-reclaimed-ifvg-entry-with-trump-volatility-live-account-ict-concepts]]).

The January 24, 2026 IFVG clip sharpens the execution filter further: trade the inversion only after HTF swing context is clear, candle two shows a small wick that supports expansion, and the market closes cleanly through the gap; if the chart is consolidating or the opposing run is too large, wait for the next HTF candle or skip the setup ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 1:07, @ 1:32, @ 2:03, @ 4:00, @ 5:19, @ 6:45, @ 12:13).

The "Forever Model" clip uses the same logic in a cleaner sequence: manipulate first, then use the inversion through the FVG as the trigger that aligns with the draw-on-liquidity and the eventual change in state of delivery ([[sources/my-forever-model-the-strategy-that-has-made-me-over-half-a-million-insane-value]]).

The 10/01/2025 NQ market review adds a live wick-and-CE example: the market uses the consequent encroachment of the longest wick, an inversion FVG, and later relative equal highs as a full-day delivery map instead of a single support/resistance line ([[sources/ict-nq-futures-market-review-october-1-2025]] @ 4:20, @ 4:49, @ 10:28, @ 12:58, @ 13:08).

The May 27, 2025 Chermane Trades review adds a live tape-reading variant: after a 9:30 Judas swing into bullish support factors, the 5-minute IFVG becomes the long trigger only after close-above / pullback behavior, while a separate aggressive failure-short variant appears only when price is already above midnight and 8:30 opens and buy side has been run ([[sources/nq-live-tape-reading-am-review]] @ 6:27, @ 7:28, @ 9:16, @ 10:38, @ 11:09, @ 26:32, @ 27:28).

The "This Stupid Simple Strategy Works Everyday" clip compresses the same family into a 1H/4H-to-5m routine: once the higher-time-frame FVG is tapped, the 5-minute IFVG becomes the confirmation gap, the swing that manipulated into it defines the stop, breakeven is often moved to the internal high / low, and the next significant liquidity pool becomes the target. That is a low-R, high-win-rate way to use IFVGs as the final trigger rather than the whole thesis ([[sources/this-stupid-simple-strategy-works-everyday-stupid-simple-and-proven]]).

## Definition

When an existing FVG is "not respected and swiftly closed over" by an expansion candle, it becomes an IFVG. Polarity flips: former resistance → support; former support → resistance ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 0:38).

## Use

Anticipate **continuation** in the prevailing trend. An IFVG can be used "in place of" a [[concepts/change-in-state-of-delivery|CISD]] as the continuation trigger ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 4:00, @ 7:22).

## NQ review-clips usage

In the 2025 NQ review clips, the first presented FVG after the open or after news is often treated as the candidate IFVG, but only after price sweeps a nearby liquidity pool and then holds above / below the relevant half of the gap ([[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 8:56, [[sources/nq-review-april-30-2025]] @ 7:24, @ 9:04, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 7:24, @ 9:04).

The direct 02/24/2025 lunch-macro execution gives the same structure in a cleaner live form: price rebalances immediately, stays away from the lower half of the inversion FVG, and uses the 10:00 opening-range high as the target ([[sources/nq-ny-lunch-macro-02242025]] @ 1:58, @ 4:50, @ 7:42).

The 11/14/2025 general market commentary gives a non-NQ bridge example: ICT says he had already marked the dollar index inversion FVG before it formed, then uses that level as the backdrop for the later bullish move and the reclaim of the opening price ([[sources/ict-general-market-commentary-november-14-2025]] @ 13:42, @ 1:41:21, @ 1:42:27).

The May 1 review adds an execution nuance: even when the IFVG is the doctrinal anchor, the actual fill can be taken from a lower **discount wick / [[deferred/concepts/gray-pool|gray pool]]** inside the same broader bullish context, as long as price has not already run too far into premium ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 11:59, @ 12:28, @ 13:00).

## Entry framework

From [[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]]:

1. HTF expansion / bias established.
2. On a lower TF, the sweep candle ("candle 2" in the fractal framing) sweeps a key level with a **small wick** ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 1:32, @ 2:03).
3. LTF inversion — close through the FVG (or [[concepts/fair-value-gap|stacked FVGs]] aggregated as one) ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 5:08, @ 10:02).
4. Enter on the close-through or on the open of the next HTF candle ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 6:58, @ 10:33).
5. Stop above / below swing or HTF wick.
6. Target 2R or other side of range.

Optional confluence: [[concepts/smt|SMT]] between candle 1's high and candle 2's high (or equivalent lows) ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 2:15, @ 12:13).

## Large opposing run rule

When the opposing candle has a large wick / large opposing run, expansion typically happens on **candle 3** — defer entry to the open of the next HTF candle ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 6:45, @ 7:38).

## Live-tape entry variants (Chermane Trades)

Two distinct IFVG techniques observed during live AM tape reading on NQ ([[sources/nq-live-tape-reading-am-review]]). _Attribution: Chermane Trades — derivative teacher; treat as supplementary technique, not ICT-direct doctrine._

1. **IFVG long entry (waiting variant)** — close above the FVG → next candle pulls back into the inverted FVG → **buy on the pullback** ([[sources/nq-live-tape-reading-am-review]] @ 10:38).

2. **IFVG failure short (no-wait variant)** — when buyside is already cleared **and** price is above midnight / 8:30 opens, **short inside the FVG without waiting for return** to the inversion level. The FVG itself is the entry level on first violation ([[sources/nq-live-tape-reading-am-review]] @ 26:32, @ 27:28).

The no-wait variant is the more aggressive technique — it sacrifices the confirmation pull-back in exchange for entering before any displacement away from the inversion.

## V-shape reversal

Ideal reversal shape accompanying a valid inversion. Absence (consolidation instead of V) means avoid or wait for another sweep ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 5:19, @ 9:32, @ 12:13).

## HTF alignment required

IFVGs traded as a raw pattern without HTF context is the primary failure mode — they must be taken with [[concepts/time-frame-alignment|HTF alignment]] ([[sources/how-to-trade-ifvg]] @ 1:07, @ 1:32).
