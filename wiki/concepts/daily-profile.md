# Daily Profile

Daily profile is the shape of the day’s session behavior: whether price trends cleanly, broadens and reverts, or spends the day oscillating around a center of value.

The practical lesson is that the daily profile tells you what kind of day you may be in before you over-commit to an entry model. A seek-and-destroy / broadening day behaves very differently from a clean expansion day.

The 06/10/2024 lunch-macro clip shows the same thing intraday: once the morning drive is set, the midday pulse often becomes a repair / continuation window around a local liquidity raid rather than a brand-new directional start.

The economic-calendar workflow is the same idea at the weekly scale: high-impact news can turn the profile into a manipulation / distribution sequence, so the trader filters the week first and then uses the post-news reaction as part of the daily-profile read ([[sources/how-to-use-the-economic-calendar-for-trading]]).

The 03/22/2025 timing-and-journaling lecture makes the same thing explicit: the operator should log the session open, the opening range, the first presented FVG, the macro window, and the exact time the move completed so the repeated timing signature becomes visible over a sample ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-algorithmic-timing-and-journaling-03222025]] @ 5:25, @ 7:42, @ 11:22, @ 12:33).
The AM Trades weekly-narrative clip pushes the same idea up a frame: the economic calendar, daily chart, and weekly profile are used together to decide whether the week is a trend, reversal, or consolidation week before any entry is considered ([[sources/am-trades-blending-the-economic-calendar-daily-chart-weekly-profiles]] @ 4:04, @ 5:55, @ 12:23, @ 15:32).

## Core idea

When London already does most of the directional work, New York often becomes the repair or reversal session rather than a fresh trend day. In those cases the profile matters as much as the entry trigger.

The new-york-reversal clip frames this explicitly: London can fail to reach a higher-time-frame level, New York can finish the run, and CISD then confirms the reversal. That is a daily-profile read, not just a pattern name.

## Common variants

- seek-and-destroy: both sides get taken and price keeps rotating back into value
- new-york-reversal: New York finishes the higher-time-frame run and then reverses
- broadening / indecision profiles: the range expands but does not cleanly trend
