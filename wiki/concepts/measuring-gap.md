# Measuring Gap

A **measuring gap** is the FVG or imbalance that sits at the **50% / equilibrium** level of a dealing range and acts as the midpoint reference for the move.

## Core idea

In the EP8 daily-range framing, the 50 DRT level is the equilibrium of the dealing range. When an inverse fair value gap or imbalance sits at that midpoint, it is treated more specifically as the **measuring gap** rather than just a generic FVG. The April 1 NQ review applies the same midpoint logic inside the opening frame, where the first presented FVG becomes the measuring reference after the open ([[sources/ep8-the-daily-range]] @ 4:04, [[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 10:06, @ 13:19).


## Why it matters

The measuring gap is the place where price often reveals whether the move is only rebalancing back to equilibrium or whether the market intends to continue through the range.

In practice it functions as:
- the equilibrium marker of the dealing range,
- a midpoint confluence array,
- and a reaction level that often overlaps with other PD arrays ([[sources/ep8-the-daily-range]] @ 4:04, @ 11:57).

## Relationship to DRT

- **25 DRT / 75 DRT** tend to host the [[concepts/breakaway-gap|breakaway gap]] or terminus-gap logic.
- **50 DRT** is the measuring / equilibrium level.

So the measuring gap is the gap expression of the range midpoint, not the beginning or end of the move.
