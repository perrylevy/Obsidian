# Weekly Open

The weekly open is the opening reference for the new trading week. It is one of the price references that can be read inside a weekly PO3 framework, but it should not be treated as synonymous with PO3 itself.

## Core idea

The weekly open is not just a line on the chart; it is a weekly reference price used when reading the week’s directional context.

In the source, extended price action below the weekly open is read as long accumulation before a move higher ([[sources/simple-power-of-three]] @ 7:05).

The 2022 mentorship Episode 2 establishes the weekend analysis routine: analyze the weekly candle before the week opens, determine directional bias (higher or lower), and use that as the foundation for all subsequent daily and intraday reads. This is the earliest ICT-direct source for weekly-open-as-accumulation-anchor ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-2]] @ 13:49).

## Why it matters

It helps anchor weekly bias by showing where price is relative to the open and whether the week is building above or below that reference.

## Practical use

Use the weekly open as:

- a weekly reference line for bias,
- a contextual marker for whether price is accepting above or below the open,
- and a frame for reading whether the current week is trending, rotating, or stalling.
