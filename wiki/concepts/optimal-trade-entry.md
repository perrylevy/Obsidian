# Optimal Trade Entry

Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) is the older Fibonacci-style entry concept that the wiki now treats as superseded by FVG-based entry logic. In the Silver Bullet material, FVG is explicitly said to have "taken the throne" from OTE as ICT's flagship entry pattern ([[sources/2023-mentorship-silver-bullet]] @ 5:31, [[concepts/fair-value-gap]]).

ICT's Fibonacci levels (from 2022 mentorship Episode 12) are: -1.618, -1.272, -1 (extensions), 0.618, 0.5, 0.382 (retracements), and 0 (anchor). These remain a **targeting tool** (projecting targets from structure points), not an entry tool — FVG has taken the entry role ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-12]]).

## Core idea

OTE is still worth knowing as a historical reference and as a term that appears in older ICT material, but in the current wiki it is not the primary entry doctrine.

The modern framing is:

- identify the draw on liquidity,
- use the FVG / PD array as the entry engine,
- and treat OTE as secondary or replaced.

## Why it matters

Keeping OTE in the wiki prevents old material from becoming confusing while preserving the current doctrine hierarchy.

## Practical use

If you encounter OTE in legacy content, read it as a prior entry framework that is now usually subordinated to FVG entry logic.
