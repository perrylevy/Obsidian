# New Day Opening Gap

The **new day opening gap** is the gap between the prior regular-trading-hours settlement / close and the new trading day's regular-trading-hours open. In the NQ review clips it is not just a curiosity — it becomes an active reference level for the session and can be revisited more than once ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-presession-trade-may-15-2025]] @ 30:40, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-may-29-2025]] @ 4:54, @ 6:49, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-when-930am-et-is-1st-presented-fvg-may-18-2025]] @ 10:06). Lecture 3 of the 2024 mentorship makes that even more explicit: the 6:00 p.m. reopen is the reference print, the midpoint is consequent encroachment, and clustered NDOGs above/below price act like a black-hole draw rather than a one-off gap-fill event ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-3]]). Lecture 5 adds the Asian-session rule: once the 6:00 p.m. reopen has printed the NDOG, price should move away, set initial short-term liquidity pools, and only then return to the gap as a breakaway / launch structure if the session is going to expand ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-5]]).

The 08/27/2024 premarket NQ clip shows the same NDOG as the live draw inside an order-block / rejection-block short: the trade waits for the opening delivery shift, then expects price to work back toward the new day opening gap rather than treating the premarket move as complete ([[sources/premarket-nq-ob-to-discount-ndog-august-27-2024]]).


## How it behaves in the NQ reviews

- It is used as a live reference level at the open, especially when the morning session is blending RTH and ETH structure ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-presession-trade-may-15-2025]] @ 12:01, @ 14:03, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-when-930am-et-is-1st-presented-fvg-may-18-2025]] @ 10:39).
- The market can trade back to the NDOG after the first move, then use it again as support / resistance once the opening drive is underway ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-presession-trade-may-15-2025]] @ 30:40, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-may-29-2025]] @ 6:17, @ 8:25).
- It sits naturally beside the first presented FVG and the new week opening gap as part of the same opening-gap toolkit ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-when-930am-et-is-1st-presented-fvg-may-18-2025]] @ 12:46, @ 14:03, @ 43:05, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-may-29-2025]] @ 4:54).

## Practical use

The NDOG gives the chart a session anchor before the rest of the opening range resolves. In these clips it functions as a target, a retest level, and sometimes the scaffold that helps decide which first-presented FVG deserves attention.
