# Market Structure Shift

A **market structure shift** is the lower-time-frame structural confirmation that price has stopped delivering in the prior direction and is beginning to deliver the other way.

## Working definition

- A liquidity raid or stop run by itself is **not enough**.
- The useful shift comes when price sweeps liquidity, then **trades away** from that sweep with enough structure to validate the reversal / continuation idea ([[sources/internal-external-liquidity]] @ 1:56, @ 14:23, [[sources/hrlr-vs-lrlr]] @ 1:42).

**Market structure hierarchy** (from 2022 mentorship Episode 12): ICT distinguishes three structural levels — **long-term highs/lows** (daily chart, institutional reference), **intermediate-term highs/lows** (created when an imbalance rebalances at that swing; must have lower short-term highs on both sides), and **short-term highs/lows** (individual swing points). The intermediate-term level is the structural pivot: its **violation** is a red flag that the directional thesis is wrong ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-12]]).
Episode 17 extends the same idea into a EURUSD daily-chart application, where MSS is combined with midnight/opening-price logic and an analysis of missing structure shifts to frame the short-side execution narrative ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-17]]).
Episode 18 adds a practical bearish sequence: price raids prior-day liquidity, displaces inside the New York session, then confirms the short with a lower-timeframe internal shift before the OB retest ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-18]]).
- Episode 36 gives a compact intraday version of the same structure: the market drops into discount, runs back into premium, consolidates, and then clears sell-side liquidity on the final expansion leg ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-36]]).
- Episode 37 gives the same sequence in a daily / news-week framing: the market works inside the FVG, rallies to the short-term high, and then the shift in market structure confirms the bullish leg after the stop-run style grab ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-37]] @ 0:24, @ 7:18, @ 9:10, @ 23:09).
- A direct example: the Faiz SMC video frames a high-probability MSS as sweep + reaction into FVG + break of structure, with the entry taken after the confirming break rather than on the sweep itself ([[sources/secret-way-to-identify-high-probability-market-structure-shifts]] @ 1:49, @ 5:29, @ 6:24, @ 15:35). The 2022 mentorship Episode 2 frames the same sequence as the core setup pattern: liquidity run → break in market structure → FVG entry ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-2]] @ 18:22, @ 30:05). Episode 3 adds the critical **intraday vs. break distinction**: a market structure shift is an intraday leg that may reverse same-day, while a market structure break is a longer-term move confirming sustained directional change ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-3]]). Episode 4 demonstrates the MSS + FVG sequence in live ES and NQ chart examples, showing how the setup either exists or it doesn't — "you can't force it" ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-4]]). Episode 6 emphasizes that **"every single trading day this pattern forms every single day long and short"** — the MSS is a daily recurring event, but the trader must look for it with the correct process ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-6]]). Episode 7 shows how the MSS on the intraday chart confirms or invalidates the **daily bias**: once daily bias is established (old high or old low target), the intraday MSS is the confirmation that price is delivering in the biased direction ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-7]]). Episode 8 demonstrates the same MSS pattern in **forex markets** (EUR/JPY), showing the structural break on the daily chart followed by lower timeframe FVG entries — the framework is asset-class agnostic ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-8]]). Episode 14 gives a compact live example of the same sequence: upside MSS already in place, a limit entry into the FVG from Friday's close, and continuation expected toward buyside liquidity above the short-term high ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-14]]).
- In SMT framing, market structure is the thing that confirms the crack in correlation — not the other way around ([[sources/intro-smt-divergence]] @ 14:04–14:21).

## How it is used

- In the internal/external-liquidity model, the required sequence is: **killzone + stop raid + market structure shift + fair value gap entry** ([[sources/internal-external-liquidity]] @ 1:56).
- In low-/high-resistance-liquidity framing, once the sweep creates high-resistance liquidity, the MSS is what justifies using the nearby FVG or order block for entry ([[sources/hrlr-vs-lrlr]] @ 1:42).
- The shift is therefore a **confirmation event**, not the original reason for bias.

The April 29, 2024 bias lecture fits this definition cleanly: the daily / weekly bias can be framed from seasonal and macro context, but the trader still needs structure to confirm which side is actually delivering after the event-week manipulation has run its course ([[sources/how-to-read-price-with-or-without-a-bias-april-29-2024]] @ 5:08, @ 16:55, @ 29:59, @ 50:52).
The 03/21/2026 market commentary applies the same logic to the dollar index: once the line-in-the-sand level gives way, the market-structure shift confirms that the bearish narrative is actually being delivered, not merely discussed ([[sources/ict-2026-market-commentary-march-21-2026]]).
The "Keys To Success In Troubled Markets" lecture uses the same sequence on the daily chart: once price breaks the obvious swing low, the market has shifted structure and the trader can stop treating the prior range as live support ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-keys-to-success-in-troubled-markets-june-16-2025]] @ 12:42, @ 13:09).

## Relationship to SMT

SMT is confirmation of the end of manipulation, but it still needs structure to matter. The useful sequence is:

1. anticipate the draw / liquidity event,
2. observe SMT or another confirmation clue,
3. require market structure to validate the directional thesis,
4. enter from the selected PD array.

That is why "market structure confirms SMT" is the right direction of dependency ([[sources/intro-smt-divergence]] @ 14:04–14:21).

## SMC synonyms

The SMC community uses **Break of Structure (BOS)** and **Change of Character (CHoCH)** for what this wiki files under **market structure shift**. Treat these as synonyms that resolve here:

- **BOS** — a break of a prior swing in the direction of the prevailing trend. In ICT framing this is the continuation-side MSS.
- **CHoCH** — the first structural break against the prior trend. In ICT framing this is the reversal-side MSS, typically preceded by a liquidity sweep and confirmed by [[concepts/displacement|displacement]] ([[sources/secret-way-to-identify-high-probability-market-structure-shifts]] @ 1:49).

Canonical term in this wiki: **market-structure-shift**. Do not fork a separate BOS or CHoCH page.

## Relationship to CISD

A MSS is the broader structural event; [[concepts/change-in-state-of-delivery|CISD]] is the more precise price-delivery / order-block level often used to express that shift on the chart. They are related, but not identical.
