# Failure Swing

A swing point that **fails to follow through** to the next expected liquidity target — typically a [[concepts/high-resistance-liquidity-run|HRLR]] that aborts before reaching its post-sweep target ([[sources/hrlr-vs-lrlr]]).

## Practical use

A failure swing is a **reversal signature**:

- A bearish leg that fails to make a new low after sweeping prior sellside → potential long.
- A bullish leg that fails to make a new high after sweeping prior buyside → potential short.

## Distinction from Turtle Soup

- [[models/turtle-soup|Turtle Soup]] is the **specific reversal model** off a liquidity sweep.
- A failure swing is the **structural condition** that often precedes one — the swing failed, then the reversal triggers.

A turtle soup may be the entry; a failure swing is what you observed first.
