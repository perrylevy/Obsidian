# Breakaway Gap

A breakaway gap is a gap that is expected to stay open at the start of a new directional leg. It functions like launch fuel: after the market trades into it, the tape should show immediate speed in the intended direction rather than stall, chop, or fully close the gap right away.

## Core idea

The practical test is simple:
- if the gap is supposed to be bullish, price should not spend much time below it or drift back through the lower half;
- if the gap is supposed to be bearish, price should not spend much time above it or drift back through the upper half.

When the market is behaving correctly, the breakaway gap acts as a departure structure rather than a midpoint balance area.

## Relationship to measuring gap

A breakaway gap is the opposite side of the same family as the [[concepts/measuring-gap|measuring gap]].

- **Breakaway gap** = departure / launch / immediate speed.
- **Measuring gap** = midpoint / equilibrium / continuation projection.

In other words, the measuring gap sits around the center of the move, while the breakaway gap marks the start of the move.

## Practical use

Treat a breakaway gap as valid only while it remains clean enough to keep driving the market away from the origin of the move. Once it is fully mitigated or starts acting like an equilibrium magnet, the gap has stopped behaving like a breakaway.
