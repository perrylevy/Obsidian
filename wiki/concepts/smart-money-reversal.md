# Smart Money Reversal

The **apex** of the [[models/market-maker-buy-model|MMBM]] / [[models/market-maker-sell-model|MMSM]] curve — the precise pivot where accumulation flips to distribution (or distribution to accumulation).

## Signature

ICT's defining description: **bodies stay inside the [[concepts/pd-array|PD array]] at the turn; only wicks exceed it** ([[sources/2023-mentorship-market-maker-models]] @ 43:20). The wick-only sweep marks the apex of the curve.

The 11/14/2025 general market commentary uses the same logic in live tape form: the low is the smart-money-reversal pivot on the dollar index, and once price reclaims the opening price it has shifted into a buy program ([[sources/ict-general-market-commentary-november-14-2025]] @ 28:09, @ 1:41:21).

## Why it matters

The smart money reversal is **not** the same as a [[models/turtle-soup|Turtle Soup]] reversal:

- Turtle Soup is a **discrete reversal trade** off a single liquidity sweep.
- Smart Money Reversal is the **structural inflection** at the top of the entire MM model — the rotation from buy-side delivery to sell-side delivery (or mirror).

The MM model can contain a turtle soup at the apex; the turtle soup is the entry, the SMR is the structural label for the larger pivot.

## PD-array role flip

At the smart money reversal, every PD array on the way up that acted as **support** flips to act as **resistance** on the way down (mirror for bullish models). Same level, opposite role ([[sources/2023-mentorship-market-maker-models]] @ 51:43).
