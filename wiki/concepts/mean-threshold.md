# Mean Threshold

The **midpoint of an [[concepts/order-block|order block]]** (single candle or composite range of consecutive same-color candles). Mean threshold is to OBs what [[concepts/consequent-encroachment|consequent encroachment]] is to FVGs ([[sources/2024-04-18-ob-tape-reading]] @ 1:51:22, @ 1:52:16).

## Use as quality filter

- **Best shorts in a bearish OB form below mean threshold** — entry candle should sit in the lower portion (in discount of the OB's internal range) ([[sources/2024-04-18-ob-tape-reading]] @ 2:17:38).
- **Best longs in a bullish OB form in the upper portion of the OB** (above mean threshold), unless a [[concepts/fair-value-gap|FVG]] in the lower half of the same range pulls price deeper for FVG+OB confluence in discount ([[sources/2024-04-18-ob-tape-reading]] @ 2:20:52, @ 2:21:21).

## Strength signature

Price that **fails to reach mean threshold** before reversing is a **strength signature** for the prevailing direction — the move is too strong to retrace 50% of the originating OB ([[sources/2024-04-18-ob-tape-reading]] @ 1:51:22, @ 1:52:16).
