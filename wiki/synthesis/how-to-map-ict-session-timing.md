## Read it as one stack

- New York time tells you when the market clock starts.
- Session ranges define the structure.
- Macros / SB / CRT define the repeatable windows inside that structure.
- No-initial-bias / time-distortion / FOMC tell you whether to trust the structure yet.
- First-presented-FVG and opening-range-gap tell you which displacement actually matters.
