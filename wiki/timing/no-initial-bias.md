# No Initial Bias

No initial bias is the operating state where you do not yet have a directional thesis at the open. In the source, ICT’s answer is to wait for the first hour of trading, mark the high and low from the open, and let the opening structure tell you which side is likely to matter first ([[concepts/no-trade]], [[sources/how-do-i-engage-markets-when-i-dont-have-an-initial-bias]] @ 6:08, @ 6:41, @ 7:15, @ 12:22).

The June 11, 2025 EURUSD / NQ update adds a broader pre-open bias-building habit: he starts from seasonal tendencies and the larger road map before the Sunday open, but still makes it explicit that when the expected IFVG continuation does not appear and the tape turns into consolidation, the right move is to stop forcing a thesis and let the market reveal its hand ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-eurusd-nq-futures-june-11-2025]] @ 0:51, @ 15:29, @ 16:49, @ 18:07).

The April 29, 2024 bias lecture sharpens the same point: bias is only the highest-probability direction, not a demand to predict the exact high or low. ICT says you can trade without a bias when the tape is not aligned, and on event-heavy weeks like FOMC / NFP he treats the calendar itself as part of the read instead of forcing a thesis ([[sources/how-to-read-price-with-or-without-a-bias-april-29-2024]] @ 5:08, @ 16:55, @ 29:59, @ 50:52, @ 1:01:19–1:02:02).
The Micro-Scalping Without Bias clip shows the same operating mode in a tiny-range live context: volume imbalances, FVGs, and inversion levels are traded without forming a directional thesis first ([[sources/micro-scalping-without-bias-nq-may-06-2024]] @ 0:30, @ 1:25, @ 5:54, @ 9:53).
The 09/06/2024 NFP lecture adds that the opening bias should remain provisional until the initial shock prints and the 15-minute map tells you which side actually survives ([[sources/nonfarm-payroll-september-06-2024]] @ 8:22, @ 9:23, @ 11:52, @ 15:15, @ 22:14).



## Core idea

When bias is unclear:

- let the market build the first useful range,
- use that range as your first structure to grade,
- and avoid forcing a directional opinion before the opening work resolves.

## Why it matters

This is a practical timing rule, not a predictive model.

## Practical use

If the market is one-sided or you do not yet have a clean read:

- wait for the first hour,
- mark the 9:30 open high/low frame,
- and only then decide whether the upper half or lower half is the better focus.

If you miss the first clean trigger inside that frame, do not force a replacement trade; use the missed-entry playbook or wait for the next valid macro boundary instead.
