# New York Local Time

**All ICT timing references are in New York local time, with daylight saving observed** ([[sources/2023-mentorship-silver-bullet]] @ 9:06, [[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-1]]).

Timezones in ICT material are never UTC, never exchange-local-other, never broker-server. Charts and journal entries should be set to NY local.

The 2022 mentorship Episode 5 makes this explicit: **"Set all charts to New York Local Time — this is non-negotiable for proper calibration with all ICT students and methodology"** ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-5]]). Episode 8 reinforces this for forex markets: **"Always set TradingView to New York local time (not your local time)"** — even when trading forex pairs, NY local time is the mandatory calibration ([[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-8]]).

## AM session boundary

The **AM session starts at 7:00 NY** ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-1]]). Pre-market is everything before that. The 9:30 cash open is a separate inflection inside the AM session — it defines the new dealing range / killzone trigger ([[sources/2024-04-18-ob-tape-reading]] @ 1:12:07).

Lecture 3 of the 2024 mentorship sharpens the 7:00 / 8:00 / 9:00 checkpoints into a practical morning liquidity map: if the NDOG sits below and relative equal highs form after 7:00, the market often engineers a sweep first before moving toward the larger draw ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-3]]).

## Opening prices to track

The **midnight open, 8:30 open, and 7:00 open** form a personal checklist for premium / discount filtering during the AM session ([[sources/nq-live-tape-reading-am-review]] @ 35:59). _Attribution: Chermane Trades — derivative teacher; not yet confirmed as ICT-direct doctrine._ The midnight and 8:30 anchors are now broken out as separate concept pages ([[concepts/midnight-opening-price]], [[concepts/830-opening-price]]).

The recurring silver bullet windows are another NY-local-time timing scaffold and now sit in their own page ([[timing/silver-bullet-windows]]).

## Why this matters

A "10:00 entry" only matters if it's 10:00 NY. Trading from a chart on London time, GMT, or exchange time misaligns with every macro / killzone / SB window in this wiki.
