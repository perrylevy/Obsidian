# 9 AM CRT

The 9 AM CRT is a New York local-time execution frame that starts from the 8:00 a.m. hourly CRT range and then uses the 9:00–10:30 window for a lower-timeframe purge, confirmation, and entry ([[sources/why-the-9-am-crt-model-could-change-your-life]], [[sources/tired-of-9-5-learn-the-9-am-crt-model-to-achieve-financial-freedom]]).

## Core idea

The workflow is:

- mark the 8:00 a.m. candle high / low,
- wait for a sweep of that range on the 15-minute chart,
- require a lower-timeframe confirmation such as market structure shift or change in state of delivery,
- and then enter from the confirming order block or breaker block.

The model treats CRT as a session frame, not just a candle-level abstraction.

## Why it matters

The 9 AM CRT model sits between candle range theory and session timing:

- [[concepts/candle-range-theory]] gives the candle-level read,
- [[timing/new-york-local-time]] gives the correct time base,
- [[timing/opening-range]] gives the nearby opening-session structure,
- and this page captures the specific 9:00 a.m. execution sequence.

## Practical use

Use the 9 AM CRT when you want a repeatable morning sequence:

- directional bias from higher-timeframe context,
- a clear 8:00 a.m. CRT reference,
- a 15-minute purge / Turtle Soup event,
- and a confirmation-based entry.
