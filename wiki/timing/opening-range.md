# Opening Range

The opening range is the first structured range after the session open. In the current wiki, the term is used most often for the regular-trading-hours opening work around the New York open, where the market establishes its first meaningful high/low frame before later continuation or reversal ([[sources/how-do-i-engage-markets-when-i-dont-have-an-initial-bias]] @ 6:08, @ 6:41, [[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-review-when-930am-et-is-1st-presented-fvg-may-18-2025]] @ 12:46, @ 13:16, [[sources/nq-futures-review-04012025]] @ 6:21, @ 8:56, [[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-1]] @ 1:51:44–1:52:32, [[sources/ict-opening-range-theory-1st-presented-fvg-logic]], [[sources/smc-opening-range-gaps]], [[sources/ict-gems-opening-range-gap-secrets]], [[sources/understanding-time-price-the-only-thing-you-need]]).

## Core idea

The opening range is the first session structure the market can grade against.

Depending on the source, it may be treated as:

- the first hour of trade,
- the 9:30–10:00 range,
- or the opening work around 9:30 where the first presented FVG becomes the active array.

The 02/23/2026 SMC lecture states the opening range as a 30-minute window and uses the 9:30 open as a probe through buy-side and sell-side liquidity before the real move shows itself ([[sources/ict-2026-smart-money-concepts-lecture-february-23-2026]] @ 19:13).

The shared idea is the same: the market reveals the first usable range after the open, and that range becomes the immediate reference for bias and follow-through.
The 9:30–10:30 first-hour dealing range is a larger scaffold that includes the opening range but is not the same thing ([[timing/first-hour-dealing-range]], [[sources/ict-2026-1st-hour-dealing-range-march-28-2026]]).

The 03/27/2026 market commentary is a clean live example: ICT uses the 9:30 print, the opening-range gap, first presented FVG, and consequent encroachment as one morning map instead of treating them as separate ideas ([[sources/ict-2026-market-commentary-march-27-2026]]).

The 01/13/2026 lecture adds a premarket lead-in: the 7:00–9:30 tape can sit in time distortion/compression, and the 9:30 open becomes the handoff into the 30-minute opening-range probe ([[sources/ict-2026-smart-money-concepts-lecture-january-13-2026]] @ 2:19, @ 4:00, @ 17:17, @ 19:13).

## Why it matters

The opening range is load-bearing because it often interacts with:

- [[concepts/new-day-opening-gap|new day opening gap]]
- [[concepts/opening-range-gap|opening range gap]]
- [[timing/first-presented-fvg|first presented FVG]]
- [[concepts/consequent-encroachment]]
- [[concepts/weekly-open]]
- [[concepts/suspension-block]]

The 2025 timing-and-journaling lecture reiterates the same rule as a practical execution frame: the morning opening range is 9:30–10:00 ET, and the afternoon repetition is 1:30–2:00 ET ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-algorithmic-timing-and-journaling-03222025]] @ 42:01, @ 57:03, @ 1:15:31).
The "NQ May 01, 2025 Review" clip keeps the same open-driven logic alive on a live tape day: the opening-range first-presented FVG, the news pulse, and the inversion validation all sit inside the same morning frame ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 5:35, @ 7:24, @ 9:04).
The 06/02/2025 and 06/05/2025 NQ reviews treat the opening range as the handoff into a larger map: the first-presented FVG, the opening-range gap, and the prior Friday / prior daily inefficiency are all carried together instead of being redrawn as separate ideas ([[sources/2025-storytellers-series-nq-review-june-02-2025]] @ 11:08, @ 12:44; [[sources/2025-storytellers-series-nq-futures-june-05-2025]] @ 2:48, @ 5:17, @ 12:44).

The SMC opening-range-gap lectures further split the family into regular-trading-hours opening-range gaps and the separate midnight opening-range scaffold, so the wiki keeps the 9:30 open and the 00:00 open as related but distinct timing frames ([[sources/smc-opening-range-gaps]], [[sources/smc-trading-opening-range-gaps]], [[sources/smc-midnight-opening-range]]).

The opening range is one of the first places the market shows whether it is likely to continue, invert, or range. On Monday gap-overlap reviews, the opening range can be traded together with the new week opening gap so both frames act as one scaffold rather than two separate drawings. The midnight opening-range family is a separate but related timing scaffold for the 00:00–00:30 session window ([[timing/midnight-opening-range]]).

## Practical use

Use the opening range to ask:

- Has the market established a clear first-hour high / low?
- Is the first presented FVG blending with the opening-range gap?
- Is price respecting the upper half or lower half of that opening structure?

The 04/20/2026 ATH lecture adds a gap-CE test: when the Friday-to-Monday gap forms near ATH, the consequent encroachment of that gap becomes the decision line. Bodies staying in the lower half with no willingness to reach the midpoint = bearish confirmation. This is the "laboratory experiment" approach to the opening range at all-time highs ([[sources/ict-2026-lecture-trading-ath-in-pd-array-matrix-april-20-2026]] @ opening range gap, @ the test).
