# Power of Three (PO3 / AMD)

Power of Three is the wiki’s canonical umbrella for the three-phase ICT delivery scaffold: accumulation, manipulation, distribution.

Use this page as the single home for PO3 logic. The phase pages below are redirects for backlink stability and should not be expanded into separate doctrines:
- [[concepts/accumulation]]
- [[concepts/manipulation]]
- [[concepts/distribution]]

## Core meaning

PO3 describes how price tends to build, raid, and then expand. In bullish form: build around the open, sweep sell-side liquidity, then expand higher. In bearish form: build around the open, sweep buy-side liquidity, then expand lower.

The earliest ICT-direct 2022 mentorship source in this wiki is [[sources/2022-ict-mentorship-episode-9]], which frames the daily pattern as open near one extreme, fake move into the opposite side, then directional expansion back through value. Episode 10 refines the bearish case with the opening-range concept.

## How to read it

1. Find the higher-timeframe bias.
2. Identify the accumulation range or opening reference.
3. Wait for the manipulation sweep.
4. Confirm displacement / market structure shift.
5. Use the resulting FVG or OB for entry.
6. Target the higher-timeframe draw.

## Fractal use

PO3 applies on multiple scales: a candle, a session, a day, a week.

That means the exact same language can be used at different frames, but the interpretation should stay disciplined:
- accumulation = where price builds,
- manipulation = where liquidity is raided,
- distribution = where the move delivers.
