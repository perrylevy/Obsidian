# October 03, 2024 NQ Market Review

The clip's main point is that NFP week can be a poor environment for aggressive trading when the tape is already congested and the market is still sitting above unfilled inefficiency.

ICT frames the week as a top-down read: the weekly chart has an active imbalance, Friday payroll is highly manipulated, and a new trader is better off staying flat on most of the week rather than forcing exposure into the chop.

The review also uses the RTH opening range gap, the midday grind, and a later inversion-FVG continuation to show how the market can chop first and only later reveal the cleaner draw.
