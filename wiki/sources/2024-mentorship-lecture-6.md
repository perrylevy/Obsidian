# 2024 Mentorship — Lecture 6

ICT-direct live NQ walkthrough, August 12, 2024 (pre-CPI / PPI week). Primarily tape-reading flow rather than dense doctrine — fills the Lecture 6 gap in the mentorship series (1, 3, 4, 5, 12, 16, 21 already ingested).

## Summary

- Pre-CPI / PPI week framing: on high-impact data weeks, expect manipulated price into the prints and wait for the market to digest the release before taking the opening-bell read (@ 7:30, @ 12:01).
- **7:00 a.m. reference candle rule.** When price prints a high around 7:00 NY, the first useful move is often the run that takes out that high or the short-term low beneath it; do not annotate the high itself — wait for the short-term low to form, then mark both levels once a sweep is in motion (@ 9:28, @ 10:00).
- **Layered sell-side / buy-side liquidity.** When multiple relative equal lows stack during London close, treat them as a **layered pool** rather than picking one; price often works each one in sequence (@ 11:07, @ 11:31).
- **Inversion FVG invitation rule.** A shaded-orange zone indicates ICT wants price to trade **below** a level, spend time below it, and then revisit from underneath; only after a displacement is the short worth framing with an FVG, institutional-orderflow-entry-drill, or bearish order block (@ 21:42, @ 22:02).
- Protective stop on live trade was **brought up under the structure and taken** before the trade could re-run; lecture framing: do not get emotional, read the signature and re-evaluate (@ 20:29, @ 20:55).
- Continues ICT's "NWOG / NDOG are too good to give away" stance — the reason they are not oversaturated in the public conversation is that interest faded; students who did the work have seen the value (@ 40:33, @ 41:08).

## Key quotes

- **@ 9:28** — "We have a minor cell side liquidity pool resting right below here, and you can see how we're working the initial cell side liquidity."
- **@ 10:00** — "Whenever it's like that, I'm going to wait for the initial run because I know it's probably going to take this high out."
- **@ 21:42** — "If it's shaded orange like this to me, my interest is some facet of seeing price go below it and come back up and then treat it as a means of barrier."
- **@ 22:02** — "If I start watching candlesticks promote that idea, and then I see a displacement, I might entertain the opportunity for them to put me into a short with a fair value gap… or a bearish order block."
