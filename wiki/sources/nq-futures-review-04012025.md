# 2025 Lecture Series - NQ Futures Review 04012025

## Summary

- Distinguishes electronic trading hours from regular trading hours and treats the 9:30 NY open as the meaningful reset for the day.
- Frames the session around the opening-range gap between prior settlement and the new RTH open, then uses the first presented 1-minute FVG after the open / news impulse as the primary intraday array.
- Uses 10:00 NY news as the driver that confirms or invalidates the opening thesis; the first retracement back into the consequential encroachment of the opening gap becomes the actionable short or continuation point.
- Reuses the same logic on the afternoon reversal: after the initial selloff, price can rebalance back into the opening frame and then continue toward the next draw on liquidity.
- Adds a practical body-vs-wick filter: wicks may raid, but if the bodies keep respecting the level, the delivery narrative is still intact.
- Treats prior Asia low, old daily low, and the opening-range gap levels as the main liquidity / target references.
