# 2025 Lecture Series - Algorithmic Timing & Journaling 03/22/2025

## Summary

- ICT frames price delivery as a continuous loop across timeframes, not as a one-shot top-down read.
- The trader should cycle the chart hierarchy at each close: 60-minute, 15-minute, 5-minute, and 1-minute views all matter as the market develops.
- Journaling is not optional decoration; it is the mechanism that turns repeated timing signatures into durable skill.
- The lesson is explicit that the time of the day, the active PD array, and the current delivery leg should be logged together so future repetitions become obvious.
