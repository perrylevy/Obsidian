# 2025 Lecture Series - NQ Review & Extraordinary Volatility | 04/09/2025

## Summary

- ICT frames the day as an unusually volatile, geopolitically driven market where normal real-time commentary would be irresponsible and smaller risk is the correct response.
- On the daily / 15-minute charts, the market is organized around a bullish order block, a daily fair value gap, and the consequent encroachment of the higher-time-frame imbalance rather than around classic support and resistance.
- On the 1-minute chart, the morning session uses a Friday 9:35 first presented FVG as the working reference, with a later 12:10 FVG carried forward as additional context.
- The same logic repeats later in the day: the new week opening gap, the afternoon first presented FVG, and the market-on-close pulse all become part of the same carried-forward delivery map.
- The durable rule here is not to chase every violent swing; it is to keep the model, lower size, and let the active session prove which array is actually valid.
