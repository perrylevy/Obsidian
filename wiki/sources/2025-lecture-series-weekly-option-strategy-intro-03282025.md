# 2025 Lecture Series - Weekly Option Strategy Intro 03/28/2025

## Summary

- ICT uses a weekly perspective to frame option ideas: identify the likely weekly high / low area first, then structure the trade around that delivery path.
- The wick consequent encroachment of the weekly candle becomes the key midpoint reference for the setup, especially when the market is expected to reject a high and revisit a lower objective.
- The lesson stresses that weekly options are a slower, more patient expression of the same ICT logic: predict the move on the higher frame, then use the option vehicle for the expression of that view.
- The clip also warns that the trader must respect risk, because option buyers and writers have very different exposure even when they are looking at the same price path.
