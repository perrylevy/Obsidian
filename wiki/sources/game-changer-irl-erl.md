# The Game Changer to ICT Concepts: IRL & ERL

**Teacher**: The MMXM Trader (derivative teacher, not direct ICT)
**Published**: 2024-03-26

## Summary

- Defines **Internal Range Liquidity (IRL)** explicitly as fair value gaps — "imbalances on your chart" (@ 1:32). No other structure type (order blocks, breakers, voids) is named as IRL in this clipping.
- Defines **External Range Liquidity (ERL)** as old highs and old lows (@ 1:32). Identical to the TTrades ERL definition.
- Core price cycle: **external → internal → external** on repeat. When ERL is swept, the next draw is IRL (an FVG); when IRL is filled, the next draw is ERL (@ 1:32–1:56, @ 4:28–4:56).
- **Premium / discount filter**: when multiple FVGs exist after an ERL sweep, price draws to the one in discount, not the one in premium (@ 10:38–11:07).
- **Time frame alignment**: monthly ERL/IRL → daily structure; weekly ERL/IRL → 4H structure; daily ERL/IRL → 1H structure. Price is fractal (@ 0:28–0:45).
- The framework is presented as the foundation for **daily bias** — knowing ERL/IRL sequencing on monthly/weekly/daily charts frames directional expectation for days or even two months (@ 4:56, @ 7:23).
- **Low-resistance liquidity runs** are catalysed by higher time frame PDAs (monthly FVGs, weekly FVGs, daily FVGs) — not just fragile patterns like trend lines or equal highs/lows (@ 18:12).
