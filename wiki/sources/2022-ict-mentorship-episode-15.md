## Key sections

### Setup and expectation

ICT opens by stating the daily bias is **bullish** and that he is watching for a **swing high breakout** to confirm the bullish market structure. The target is the daily chart level around **14,000**, acknowledged as "a little bit of a stretch" but the directional thesis is clear.

He also notes the economic calendar: there is expected volatility later in the day, so equities traders should finish early.

### Entry logic and risk management

> "When you're entering on like the opening like 9:30 equities opening, you could take a long entry here but you don't know how far it's going to whip below that low and you could be wrong and painfully wrong."

At market open, ICT avoids a hard stop loss. Instead, he keeps **sell stops resting at 13,612** (below the swing low). This allows the trade to survive opening whipsaw rather than being stopped out by a quick sweep that immediately reverses. He is ready to close at market if price trades below the support level.

The limit order targets the **$80 level** for approximately **$1,000+ profit**.

### Key levels

- **Fair value gap at 680 level** — premium market support.
- **Minimum support level: 630** — small fair value gap in the last 3 candles.
- **Sell stops: 13,612** — resting below the swing low.

### Trade results

- **2 trades executed**
- **Gross profit**: $1,255
- **Commissions**: ~$15
- **Net profit**: ~$1,240
- **Maximum drawdown**: ~3 handles (~$60) from entry point
- **Status**: No additional trades taken for the remainder of March.

> "Small little scout out, very little drawdown, about three handles of drawdown if that from the entry."

### Emotional discipline and platform awareness

ICT warns that this is where new traders get nervous and "hit the wrong thing." He uses multiple platforms (ThinkorSwim, TradeStation, TradingView) and notes that different settings require adaptation.
