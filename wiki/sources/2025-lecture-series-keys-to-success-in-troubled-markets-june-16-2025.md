# 2025 Lecture Series - Keys To Success In Troubled Markets June 16, 2025

## Summary

- ICT frames the market as “troubled” when it is hesitant, volatile, and unwilling to trend cleanly, with large gaps and geopolitical risk amplifying the difficulty.
- The solution is not classic support/resistance but reading the chart through quadrants, consequent encroachment, and the active daily/weekly context.
- The clip repeatedly contrasts higher-time-frame structure with lower-time-frame noise and emphasizes that traders should not force a bias while the market is distorted.
- Key practical ideas include using relative equal highs as buyside targets, waiting for market structure shift before leaning into the next leg, and using PD-array / midpoint logic for stop placement.
- The lesson is best treated as a live-trading risk-and-context lecture rather than a single setup model.
