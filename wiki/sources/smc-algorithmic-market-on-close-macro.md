# 2025 Lecture Series - SMC Algorithmic Market On Close Macro

## Summary

- Frames the market-on-close trade as a precise short inside an inversion FVG, with the stop initially protected above the high and deliberately left alone.
- The author emphasizes that the bodies respecting the shaded zone are the important signal; the move is treated as algorithmic script, not retail order-flow noise.
- Builds the target stack from the lower orange level, the volume imbalance, and the minor sellside liquidity pool below.
- Introduces a clean worst-case retest to the rejection block: if price must come back higher, that highest closed candle before rotation is the next defensive line.
- The actual execution window is the 3:45–4:00 PM NY market-on-close macro, where the tape starts ripping toward liquidity and then accelerates lower.
- Uses partials and stop management only after the delivery has confirmed the intended direction.
