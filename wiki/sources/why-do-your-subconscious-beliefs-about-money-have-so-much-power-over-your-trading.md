# Why Do Your Subconscious Beliefs About Money Have So Much Power Over Your Trading?

This clip explains why simulation and live trading feel different: when the money is real, subconscious beliefs about money, power, safety, and loss hijack the rational mind.

The reusable rule is that the emotional brain treats live uncertainty like a survival problem. Fear can freeze you out of valid setups, while opportunity can trigger overtrading and revenge trading. The fix is not more willpower; it is retraining how you respond to uncertainty.

The clip recommends building a new mind rooted in discipline, courage, and calm thinking, and remembering that you can control only performance, not outcome.
