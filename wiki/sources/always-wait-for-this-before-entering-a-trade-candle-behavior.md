# Always Wait For THIS Before Entering a Trade (Candle Behavior)

## Summary

- Candle behavior is defined as how a candle moves through its life cycle in relation to the previous candle close; timing is the key missing piece in many failed entries ([[sources/always-wait-for-this-before-entering-a-trade-candle-behavior]] @ 0:25, @ 0:55).
- In continuation contexts, the first half of the candle is expected to build the wick against the prior close and the second half to confirm the intended direction around the midpoint of the candle ([[sources/always-wait-for-this-before-entering-a-trade-candle-behavior]] @ 2:02, @ 2:24).
- The lesson applies the same idea across hourly, daily, and weekly candles, then refines the entry with lower-timeframe structure around the 30-minute mark of the hour; this is essentially a candle-science / previous-candle-high-low read, not a separate doctrine family ([[sources/always-wait-for-this-before-entering-a-trade-candle-behavior]] @ 3:17, @ 4:53, @ 6:59, @ 7:19).
- The trader explicitly frames candle behavior as a session tool: daily bias can be faded if the session timing suggests the candle should still be building its wick first ([[sources/always-wait-for-this-before-entering-a-trade-candle-behavior]] @ 3:17, @ 4:47).
