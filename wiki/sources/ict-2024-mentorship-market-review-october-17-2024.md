# ICT 2024 Mentorship Market Review October 17, 2024

## Summary

- ICT frames the morning through a daily narrative: settlement, the 9:30 open, the opening-range gap, and the way the first 30 minutes tend to price the gap midpoint before deciding whether the gap will fully fill or hold.
- He introduces a named wick-midpoint PD array, the "Gallow", and treats the body/wick relationship around that area as an actionable reference that can later be reused as an inversion or reclaimed level.
- The clip reinforces a simple open-up / gap-up expectation: if the market opens well above settlement, the first leg can still be bearish into mid-gap before any larger bullish continuation is allowed to show itself.
- The broader lesson is narrative construction, not prediction: identify the draw, grade the gap, then let price validate or invalidate the thesis through time-based delivery.
