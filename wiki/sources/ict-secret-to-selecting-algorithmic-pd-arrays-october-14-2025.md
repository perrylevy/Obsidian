# ICT Secret To Selecting Algorithmic PD Arrays \ October 14, 2025

## Summary

- Frames PD arrays as time-driven signposts: the market is algorithmic, price is delivered through time, and the trader should read which array is actually active instead of treating every level as equal.
- Uses the dollar index and EUR/USD as live examples of how an old inefficiency, suspension block, breaker, or relative-equal-high cluster can become the practical reference for the next move.
- Treats **relative equal highs** as a billboard-style buyside target: the smoother right-side high is the one likely to get run first.
- Recasts an old inefficiency as **immediate rebalance** when price snaps back to the inefficiency's opening price / high and does not need to fully close in to be useful.
- Repeatedly emphasizes that a bullish setup should not be forced into a full closure of the gap if the market is clearly leaving the discount portion open.
- Highlights the first-presented FVG, the new week opening gap, and consequent encroachment as precise target / kickoff references when they align with the broader draw.
- Repeats the anti-retail message: Level 2, DOM, footprint, VWAP, volume profile, and similar tools do not replace time-based OHLC reading.
