# Trading IRL & ERL With Order Blocks! - ICT Concepts

## Summary

- The lesson frames the market as oscillating between external liquidity and internal liquidity, with the relationship repeating as external → internal → external.
- TTrades defines IRL as fair value gaps / imbalances and ERL as obvious swing highs and lows.
- After external liquidity is taken, price is expected to reprices into internal liquidity first; after internal liquidity is tapped, the next objective becomes external liquidity again.
- The practical flowchart is: higher-timeframe bias, lower-timeframe stop raid, market-structure shift, then FVG / order-block entry inside a killzone.
- The examples emphasize that the “right” target is a function of the larger structure: daily, weekly, and monthly levels can each serve as the external reference for the next leg.
- This source is the cleanest direct explainer of the IRL/ERL oscillation and the order-block entry stack.
