# ICT 2024 Mentorship October NFP NQ October 04, 2024

## Summary

- ICT frames Non-Farm Payrolls as a carnival ride: you do not know the number, you do not know the direction, and you should not try to predict it.
- The practical rule is to wait at least 15 minutes after the 8:30 release before taking the market seriously on a lower timeframe.
- On NFP weeks, the days leading into Friday can already be unreliable, so the real lesson is discipline: avoid forcing trades before the release if you are not yet fully developed.
- The tape-reading workflow is to mark the nearby buy-side and sell-side pools, then watch which side gets damaged first once the report prints.
