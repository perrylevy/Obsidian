# The MMXM Trader's 2024 YT Mentorship - Episode 1

This mentorship opener lays out a higher-time-frame checklist for selecting a setup: start with the monthly/weekly/daily context, locate premium or discount, look for SMT, then lower the risk and wait for a lower-time-frame confirmation.

The useful part is the hierarchy. It is not a random entry tutorial; it is a decision-order lesson. First establish the narrative, then identify the right level, then use opening-price / stop-hunt / CISD logic to refine the entry.

This makes the clip a good bridge example for the model stack because it shows how higher-time-frame bias, SMT, and delivery confirmation fit together before the entry is chosen.
