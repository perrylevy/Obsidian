# Why Does Everything Change When the Money Is Real?

This clip argues that live trading changes everything because real money activates the emotional brain's relationship to power, safety, status, and loss.

The reusable rule is that the chart itself does not become more dangerous — your internal state does. The trader has to train for uncertainty, stop trying to control outcomes, and learn to stay functional when a loss is possible.

The clip also recommends separating self-worth from money, focusing on performance instead of identity, and becoming a better loser so small losses do not turn into catastrophic mistakes.
