# 2025 Lecture Series - NQ Review May 29, 2025

## Summary

- The weekly close centers on a bullish macro frame, with the weekly consequent encroachment and daily FVG / volume-imbalance pair as the higher-time-frame draw (@ 2:23, @ 3:39, @ 4:11).
- The market uses the daily FVG first, then revisits the same idea through an Asian-session first presented FVG at the open and again through a PM-session first presented FVG (@ 6:17, @ 6:49, @ 8:25).
- The clip leans on the new-day opening gap and the new-week opening gap as active reference levels for the session, while explicitly framing the opening range between 9:30 and 10:00 Eastern time as the relevant window (@ 4:54, @ 6:49, @ 7:11, @ 8:25).
- A bullish breaker / bullish shoulder-block idea appears after the sell-side move and Fed-risk context, reinforcing that the market can reuse the same range from multiple angles (@ 7:44, @ 8:25, @ 9:35).
- The practical takeaway is that the session can be organized from the higher-time-frame draw down into the opening range, then managed through the first-presented-FVG logic rather than by chasing random later gaps.
