# ICT Suspension Block & Review — September 30, 2025

First public, ICT-direct teaching of the **suspension block** — a previously private construct. This clip is doctrinally significant because it defines the suspension block *in ICT's own words* for the first time, which promotes it from deferred-niche back into main doctrine.

## Summary

- A **suspension block** is a PD array that **looks like** a fair value gap but is formed by a candle whose body is **suspended between a volume imbalance above and a volume imbalance below** (@ 10:54, @ 11:37). Prior wicks crossing the area do **not** invalidate it; the construction is the upper-VI / body / lower-VI sandwich (@ 11:20, @ 13:16).
- It is named "suspension" because the body is held in place between those two volume imbalances (@ 11:37).
- ICT explicitly separates this from classic FVG definitions: by the original three-candle no-overlap rule this zone would not qualify as an FVG, but the algorithm still treats it as a valid PD array (@ 10:57).
- Suspension blocks are framed as **extremely powerful** — bullish on up-close candles during bullish orderflow, bearish on down-close candles during bearish orderflow (@ 11:37, @ 12:00).
- Like FVGs, suspension blocks have an **inversion mode**: once price reverses through a bullish suspension block, it changes character and acts as a premium array on subsequent revisits (@ 14:51, @ 15:18).
- Bodies — not wicks — confirm the array is holding. ICT repeatedly points to bodies respecting the upper/lower quadrants and consequent encroachment as "technical science" (@ 15:18, @ 15:38).
- On the live NQ example, price traded down into the bullish suspension block multiple times, held consequent encroachment, and rallied to **24,819.25** — the buy-side target called out from the volume imbalance level on the 1-minute (@ 12:16, @ 14:13).

## Operator mechanics (from the NQ walkthrough)

1. Identify an up-close (bullish) or down-close (bearish) candle whose **body sits between an upper volume imbalance and a lower volume imbalance** on the 1-minute.
2. Ignore prior wicks that cross the zone — they do not invalidate the suspension block construction.
3. With narrative and orderflow aligned in the suspension-block's direction, treat it as a high-quality PD array for entry.
4. Grade quality the same way as any dealing range: wait for **bodies** to respect consequent encroachment or the upper/lower quadrant, not just wicks.
5. If price reverses cleanly through the suspension block, flip the read — subsequent revisits now act as an **inversion suspension block** (premium array in a bearish context, discount in a bullish reversal).
