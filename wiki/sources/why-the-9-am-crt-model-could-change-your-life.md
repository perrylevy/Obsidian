# Why the 9 AM CRT Model Could Change Your Life | CRT trading strategy | ICT Secrets

## Summary

- The model is a 9:00 a.m. execution frame built on the 8:00 a.m. hourly CRT range and a lower-timeframe purge window from 9:00 to 10:30 New York local time.
- Direction starts with daily bias and a higher-timeframe key level, often an order block, FVG, breaker block, or old high / low.
- After the 8:00 candle closes, mark its high / low; then on the 15-minute chart wait for a CRT purge / Turtle Soup inside the time window.
- The lower-timeframe confirmation is a market structure shift or change in state of delivery, after which the entry is taken from the new order block.
- The examples target the 15-minute CRT extreme first and then the 8:00 a.m. CRT extreme.
- The source also sharpens the distinction between the 30-minute opening range and the broader first-hour dealing range.
- This is a crisp, repeatable 9 AM session variant of candle-range theory and belongs in the timing stack, not as a standalone doctrine island.
