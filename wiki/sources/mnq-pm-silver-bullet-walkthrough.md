# MNQ Live Trade Walkthrough — PM Silver Bullet & Price Continuum Theory (Live Account)

## Summary

Doctrine statements made by the trader:
- The PM macro window "2:50 to 3:10" is named as one of the PM-session macros ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 3:01, @ 10:59).
- For a bullish FVG, the lower half should not be traded through; "for bullish we want to see the lower half of PD arrays cannot be traded to" ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 0:58).
- When drawing an FVG, any volume imbalance inside the range must be included in the gap ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 0:36).
- "Event Horizon" is defined as the midpoint between two adjacent New Day / New Week Opening Gaps ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 4:51).
- Higher-timeframe inefficiencies are "parent" — stronger / more important than lower-timeframe inefficiencies ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 15:22).
- Price Continuum Theory ("ICT teaches that a lot"): cycle timeframes; when LTF FVG is overlapped, check if a parent HTF FVG is holding at its midpoint ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 15:22).
- Low-resistance signatures: speed above highs, FVGs staying open, fluid delivery — "this is the condition you want to trade in" ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 13:42).
- A reclaimed bullish FVG: a bullish FVG initially failed as support, but if price re-trades above it later, it is expected to act as a normal bullish FVG on the next visit ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 22:25).
- Three consecutive down-closed candles acting as a basing structure are identified as a bullish order block ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 29:45).

Trade-specific observations (dated, MNQ-only):
- Long entry on MNQ inside a 1-minute bullish FVG sitting in the upper half of a 5-minute bullish FVG (with volume imbalance); draw on liquidity was buyside ~22,100 ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 0:08, @ 0:36).
- Stop "just beneath consequent encroachment" of a wick midpoint ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 0:08).
- Took ~90% off at -2 standard deviation ($1,000) and left 2 micros running to buyside ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 8:02, @ 11:27).
- Final P&L on the trade: target of $1,000 for the PM session achieved ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 30:59).

## Doctrine takeaways (vs trade-specific observations)

Doctrine (timeless — candidates for doctrine pages):
- PM macro window 2:50–3:10 NY ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 3:01, @ 10:59).
- Bullish FVG: lower half must not be traded through ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 0:58).
- FVG drawing rule: include any volume imbalance inside the gap range ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 0:36).
- Event Horizon = midpoint between two adjacent New Day / New Week Opening Gaps ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 4:51).
- HTF inefficiencies are "parent" to LTF inefficiencies ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 15:22).
- Price Continuum Theory: cycle timeframes; a parent HTF FVG's midpoint can hold price even when the LTF FVG is overlapped ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 15:22).
- Low-resistance signatures: speed through highs, FVGs staying open, fluid one-directional delivery ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 13:42).
- Reclaimed bullish FVG: a bullish FVG that initially failed as support can be re-used later if price climbs back above it ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 22:25).
- Bullish order block can be identified as a cluster of three consecutive down-close candles acting as basing ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 29:45).
