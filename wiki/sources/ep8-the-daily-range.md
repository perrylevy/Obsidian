# EP8: The Daily Range

## Summary

- Frames the day with a daily dealing range: 25 DRT / 75 DRT are treated as breakaway / terminus gap levels, with 50 DRT as the equilibrium or measuring level. That makes this a range-organization / daily-range framing example rather than a distinct doctrine family ([[sources/ep8-the-daily-range]] @ 2:42, @ 4:04).
- Treats the market as a buy program when plotting low-to-high and a sell program when plotting high-to-low; the DRT level used depends on the program state.
- Uses the opening price of a bullish order block, the propulsion block, and the bullish breaker as practical reaction points inside the range.
- Repeatedly emphasizes that premium / discount and the relation to equilibrium matter more than any single candle by itself.
- Notes the session structure on the intraday chart and explicitly treats the London lunch window as dead time.
- Uses SMT divergence between Euro and dollar structure as a validation clue at key levels.
- This is best read as a framing example of how the daily range logic can be organized into reusable levels, not as a separate doctrine family.
