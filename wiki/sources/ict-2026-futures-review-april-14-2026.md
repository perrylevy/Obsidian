# ICT 2026 Futures Review | April 14, 2026

## Summary

- ICT distinguishes the 9:30–10:00 opening range from the full 9:30–10:30 first-hour dealing range and treats the first hour as the meaningful session scaffold.
- The clip says the lunch macro can begin around the 10:30 hour, i.e. once the first-hour dealing range is complete, and it often looks for a retracement back inside the range from the 9:30 open.
- In a strong bullish morning, the lunch macro can target the first low formed after 10:00, making 10:30–11:00 a time window for pullback / retracement logic rather than a fresh morning thesis.
- ICT reinforces the consequent-encroachment checkpoint on the working wick / gap and says the gap takes precedence when deciding what price should respect.
- The clip also restates that once a candle trades back through its open and upper half, it can function as a bearish order block; bodies staying out of the upper half preserve the downside narrative.
