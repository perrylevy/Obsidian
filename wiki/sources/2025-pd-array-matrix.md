# ICT PD Array Matrix Revealed — October 18, 2025

Live stream by ICT (The Inner Circle Trader), October 18 2025. Covers the **PD Array Matrix**: the structural framework ICT uses to select which PD arrays are actionable at any given moment. Demonstrates on DXY (Dollar Index), Euro USD, Gold, and NQ/NASDAQ.

---

## Summary

- **The 20-day look-back is the primary data range.** Grade the highest high and lowest low of the last 20 trading days. This box is divided into four quadrants (lower extreme, lower–mid, upper–mid, upper extreme) with the midline as [[concepts/consequent-encroachment]] / equilibrium. For trending/parabolic markets (e.g. gold), extend to 40 or 60 days.
- **PD Array Matrix = quadrant grading applied to every significant candlestick wick and inefficiency inside that range.** The algorithm refers to the open, high, low, and close of every time-based candle; it grades each individually (close → high for a premium wick; low → open for a discount wick) and sub-divides into lower quadrant / equilibrium / upper quadrant / extreme. These graded levels are the rails the algorithm rides.
- **High-probability PD arrays must straddle a quadrant line.** A fair value gap (or suspension block) is "smart money" caliber only when some part of the gap range overlaps with / lays on top of a quadrant level. A gap floating entirely between quadrant lines is common-grade and 50/50.
- **Bullish order-flow rule:** when price is bullish, down-close candle bodies should stay at or above the lower quadrant of the relevant range; [[concepts/consequent-encroachment]] to upper quadrant is the "meat" of high-probability setups. Bearish order-flow is the mirror.
- **Order block definition clarified.** An order block is the opening price of the lowest down-close candle (or range of consecutive down-close candles) — it "orders the change in the state of delivery." It has nothing to do with buy/sell orders in the book.
- **Time must agree with price.** A valid PD array is not tradeable until it coincides with a macro window or session open. Both conditions — price at quadrant + correct time — must align.
- **Large-event ranges supersede the 20-day look-back.** When a single extreme daily candle dominates recent price action (e.g. the big sell-off Friday two-weeks prior), grade *that* candle's range first; the 20-day look-back becomes subordinate until price escapes that band.
