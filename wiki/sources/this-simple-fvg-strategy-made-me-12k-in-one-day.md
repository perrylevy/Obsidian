# This SIMPLE FVG Strategy Made Me $12K in ONE DAY

This clip is a derivative-teacher compilation built around one repeatable idea: sweep higher-time-frame liquidity, wait for the reaction into an IFVG / FVG structure, then hold toward the opposing liquidity target.

Across the examples, the recurring pattern is not a complicated model but a simple execution chain: a sweep or liquidation event provides the context, the market reacts with momentum, an inversion fair-value-gap confirms the turn, and the trade is managed toward the next visible liquidity pool. The clip also stresses that the highest-conviction versions are the ones where the reaction is immediate after the sweep, while delayed reactions are lower probability but still workable.

A second takeaway is discipline: the trader repeatedly argues that one pattern, mastered consistently, is enough. The model is intentionally stripped down to sweep → reaction → IFVG close-through → opposing liquidity, with occasional use of order blocks, breakers, and partials when they improve the trade.
