# The Harder You Try, the More Success Slip-Slides Away - WHY?

The clip argues that trading success is not produced by brute force, rationalism, or effort alone. Traders often bring a work-hard mentality into the market and then discover that the market exposes the limits of that approach.

The practical lesson is that trading is driven by emotional state, bias, and a probability-based mind. To become consistent, the trader has to manage the emotional brain, calm the nervous system, and stop trying to control outcomes by force.

The clip also emphasizes that a trader can know the rules intellectually and still fail to follow them when the emotional state takes over. The fix is not just more study; it is a different operating psychology.
