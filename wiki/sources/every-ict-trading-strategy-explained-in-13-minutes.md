# Every ICT Trading Strategy Explained in 13 Minutes!

## Summary

- The video is a rapid survey of common ICT-style strategies and frames them as small combinations of liquidity, structure, and FVG logic.
- Silver Bullet is presented as sweep + market structure shift + FVG entry, with the day's high or low getting raided first.
- Cameron's model is presented as draw on liquidity, stop raid, and FVG entry.
- IFVG is described as a failed FVG whose polarity flips after price closes through it; the midline matters as a validity check.
- Turtle Soup is framed as a liquidity raid followed by an FVG entry in the direction of the reversal.
- CRT, OTE, CISD, and Power of Three are also summarized as compact execution frameworks or pattern families.
