# 2025 Lecture Series - EurUsd & NQ Futures June 11, 2025

## Summary

- Opens with a weekly bias-building routine: before the Sunday open, ICT checks seasonal tendencies and the larger road map, then decides whether there is a directional lean or whether the market should be read without a bias.
- On NQ, the salient references are a daily imbalance / wick structure, consequent encroachment on the premium wick, and a higher-price draw that had been developing for weeks.
- The clip repeatedly warns that the CPI / PPI week is a high-volatility environment where aggressive trading is often low-quality and overpressing the edge is the wrong move.
- On EURUSD, ICT frames the expected path as bullish unless the inversion-FVG continuation fails; if it fails, the fallback is consolidation rather than forcing a trade.
- The practical lesson is to define what would make the thesis wrong, then stand aside when the tape stops matching the expected characteristics.
