# Trading FOMC Two Stage Delivery

## Summary

- The lecture frames FOMC as a two-stage delivery event: the first 2:00 p.m. leg can be the release impulse, and a later post-conference leg can become the continuation or second-stage delivery.
- The day is mapped with daily and 1-minute charts, then graded against relative equal highs, sell-side liquidity, a daily buy-side imbalance, and the first presented FVG after 9:30.
- ICT treats the early FOMC move as a high-sensitivity context where quarterly / daily / intraday grading matters more than generic pattern matching.
- The morning setup uses a 9:30 shift in market structure, a volume imbalance, and a first-presented FVG / consequent-encroachment sequence before the later 2:00 p.m. FOMC leg.
- The clip emphasizes surgical execution: take the directional piece that aligns with the HTF map, then avoid overstaying when FOMC volatility can reverse or extend in a second wave.
- This source is a clean FOMC delivery example and is best treated as a direct session/macro expansion of the timing page rather than a new standalone doctrine family.
