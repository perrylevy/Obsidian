# Most Traders Lose Because of THIS And Here's 5 ways to FIX It

The clip says most traders are not losing because they lack setups. They are losing because their mind hijacks execution: ego, dopamine, pressure to get back losses, and the urge to force a result.

The practical fix is structure. Know your bias, invalidation, and risk before entry; journal honestly; detach identity from wins and losses; and stay boring enough that execution becomes repeatable instead of emotional.

The clip's five rules are basically a discipline framework: expect nothing, keep risk boring, journal truthfully, detach from your win rate, and stay aligned with the model even when it feels unexciting.
