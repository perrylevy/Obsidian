# Timeframe Alignment: How To Align Timeframes For Expansion

## Summary

- The lesson reduces execution to three layers: higher-timeframe bias, middle-timeframe structure, and lower-timeframe entry.
- A lower-timeframe setup only matters when it is nested inside a higher-timeframe point of interest such as an FVG, swing high/low, or order block.
- The structure side must first align with bias through a change in the state of delivery or market structure shift; only then does the entry timeframe become actionable.
- The clip gives practical alignment pairs: weekly with 4H, daily with 1H, 4H with 15m, 1H with 5m, 30m with 3m, and 15m with 1m.
- Expansion is the reward for full alignment: when all timeframes point the same way and the point of interest is validated, the market can deliver quickly with fewer retracements.
- SMT is used as a confirmation tool in the examples, especially when the reversal or continuation is intended to be a larger directional move rather than a random bounce.
- This source is a clean mechanical explanation of the alignment stack and should anchor the concept page with explicit timeframe pairings.
