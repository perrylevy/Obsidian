# June 24, 2024 NQ Turtle Soup Short

## Summary

- The clip is a pre-open Turtle Soup short on NQ built from buy-side liquidity being taken and then failing to hold above the working array.
- The trade uses a rejection block and inversion fair value gap logic, with consequent encroachment as the midpoint reference and the opening-range gap as background structure.
- ICT wants heavy downside follow-through once the short is accepted; the point is not a weak poke, but acceleration away from the swept level.
- This is the premarket version of the same Turtle Soup logic: raid, reject, then expand in the opposite direction.
