# ICT Tutelage September 04, 2024

This tutelage clip is a dense morning-session walkthrough. ICT/Calub treats the session as a one-day working window: ignore what happened before the open and focus on how price behaves around the new day opening gap, the opening-range gap, and the first valid 1-minute FVG after 9:30. The clip also reinforces immediate rebalance and change-in-state-of-delivery language with a live reclaim / continuation sequence.

## Summary

- The working window is the morning session up to roughly 11:00–11:30 New York time; beyond that, the lesson is to stop overextending the study of the open.
- The daily chart is used to anchor the prior day's fair value gap as a premium array while the current session builds its own morning structure.
- The new day opening gap is treated as a live reference for the current day, and the opening-range gap is explicitly defined from prior settlement to the 9:30 open.
- The first valid FVG after 9:31 is the first-presented FVG for the session, and price can reclaim it after the initial sweep.
- Once the first FVG is reclaimed, ICT treats it as an inversion-style frame that can carry price toward relative equal highs and the next draw on liquidity.
- Immediate rebalance appears when an expansion leg returns back to the prior candle's opening price / reference before continuing lower or higher.
- The opening price of the bearish order-block candle is called out as the change in state of delivery, linking order-block logic directly to the execution marker.
- The opening-range gap can be read in quadrants; the midpoint / consequent encroachment and upper quadrant are live reference levels during the morning reprice.

## Practical takeaways

- Work only the morning session and avoid dragging the lesson into the rest of the day.
- Keep the NDOG and ORG labels straight; both are live session references, but they anchor different scopes.
- Use the first 9:31+ FVG as the initial morning anchor, especially after a large-range or post-holiday day.
- Treat a clean reclaim of that first FVG as a valid inversion-style continuation signal.
- When the opening-price / CISD marker is obvious, let it frame the order-block entry instead of overcomplicating the setup.
- The opening-range midpoint and upper quadrant are not decorative; they are active management levels inside the morning sequence.
