# Top Down Trade Review September 01, 2024

## Summary

- The review walks top-down from weekly to daily to 15-minute execution and uses the prior week's wick as the governing discount/premium structure.
- ICT says the weekly opening price defines the new week opening gap on a one-minute chart; the weekly chart itself is only the bias scaffold.
- Because the prior week closed above the large wick, price is expected to stay focused on the upper portion of that weekly range, with the lower portion treated as discount and the more favorable buy area.
- On the daily chart, Friday opens lower in the weekly inefficiency, rebalances the prior close / volume imbalance, then trades back up through a bullish order block whose opening price acts as the delivery-change line.
- The same session logic repeats on the 15-minute: old daily lows matter, Tuesday's low is the most salient prior low after looking back three days, and low-resistance liquidity run targets are the easiest objectives.
- The clip repeatedly insists that the market is not driven by trendlines or breakout lore; it is re-pricing through specific PD arrays, imbalance, and liquidity pools.
