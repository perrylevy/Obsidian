# ICT General Market Commentary | November 14, 2025

## Summary

- ICT opens by saying he will walk the dollar index, FX majors, gold, crude, and major averages, then bridge the futures read to CFD markets and stock-trading questions.
- The through-line is anticipation, not reaction: he tells traders to annotate their charts, identify the next draw on liquidity, and use the higher-time-frame context before they try to scalp the lower time frame.
- The dollar index is described with inversion-FVG language that he says he had already anticipated before it formed; later, a reclaim of the opening price and a close back through a small imbalance validate the buy-program / smart-money-reversal transition.
- He repeatedly warns that the current climate has gap risk and volatility risk, so short-term trading is less attractive than stepping back, reducing frequency, and respecting the empty/inefficient price zones.
- The source is a bridge commentary: it reinforces the market-maker buy/sell model, draw-on-liquidity, liquidity-void, and smart-money-reversal framework rather than introducing a new standalone doctrine family.
