## Summary

- The review starts from the opening-range-gap scaffold and layers in premium wick / consequent-encroachment levels, showing how the morning and afternoon can be read from the same session map ([[sources/ict-nq-futures-market-review-october-1-2025]] @ 0:44, @ 1:34, @ 2:51, @ 4:49).
- A 9:31 first-presented FVG is explicitly called out as part of the same opening-day structure, with discount sensitivity, inversion FVGs, and the opening gap all supporting the same directional read ([[sources/ict-nq-futures-market-review-october-1-2025]] @ 5:09, @ 5:45, @ 6:17).
- The clip emphasizes that the market should be judged by bodies, wicks, and quadrant levels rather than crude support/resistance lines; the wick CE and inversion behavior matter more than a naive line-draw ([[sources/ict-nq-futures-market-review-october-1-2025]] @ 4:20, @ 7:48, @ 8:14, @ 10:28).
- Relative equal highs are treated as the obvious buyside draw later in the day, with the review framing the market as likely to run that smooth high once the earlier supports are respected and the sellside has already been engineered ([[sources/ict-nq-futures-market-review-october-1-2025]] @ 12:58, @ 13:08).
- The clip is a clean bridge example: open-range gap, first-presented FVG, inversion FVG, relative equal highs, and premium/discount all operate as one integrated map.
