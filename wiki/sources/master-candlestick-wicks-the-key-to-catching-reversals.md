# Master Candlestick Wicks - The Key To Catching Reversals

## Summary

- The lesson is a clean wick-science primer: the important read is whether the upper or lower half of a wick is respected, because that half tells you whether price is likely to continue or reverse.
- A respected wick often prints an open-low-high-close or open-high-low-close structure that confirms the bias, while a disrespected wick suggests the market will likely reach for the opposing extreme.
- The clip repeatedly moves from daily to hourly to 5-minute examples, showing that the same wick rule works fractally across timeframes.
- The main value is that it teaches the trader to treat a wick midpoint as a decision line, not just as decorative candle noise.
