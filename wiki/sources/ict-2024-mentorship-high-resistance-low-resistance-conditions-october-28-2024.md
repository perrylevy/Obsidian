# ICT 2024 Mentorship High Resistance & Low Resistance Conditions October 28, 2024

## Summary

- The lecture frames market conditions as a choice between low-resistance and high-resistance delivery: low-resistance sessions are cleaner, faster, and easier to trade; high-resistance sessions are slower, more obstructed, and demand smaller expectations.
- ICT explicitly distinguishes the premarket window from the 9:30 opening session: on Mondays without major news, the premarket can be the lower-resistance opportunity while the AM open is more likely to be high-resistance or even punishing.
- The clip repeatedly emphasizes that Monday is not automatically a no-trade day; instead, the trader should use the economic calendar and the specific session climate to decide whether to trade premarket, wait for the open, or stand aside.
- The structure examples center on clustered new-week opening gaps, prior-week gaps, and the way price tends to reprice to the easiest nearby objective rather than the furthest one.
- The practical management lesson is to favor low-hanging-fruit objectives and lower leverage when the market is in a high-resistance environment, because overreaching for the maximal target is exactly what causes frustration and poor exits.
