# ICT Daily Profile - Seek & Destroy

## Summary

- The clip defines a seek-and-destroy day as an indecision profile: the daily candle opens and closes around the same area and the market fails to sustain a clean directional move.
- The practical read is session-based: London often takes both sides of the Asia range, and New York then spends the rest of the day oscillating back into the range instead of expanding cleanly.
- When London already creates most of the day’s range, New York becomes lower probability and is often better avoided.
- If the profile is tradable, the method is to fade back into the range from outside it, using premium/discount and equilibrium as the guide.
- This source is mainly a daily-profile filter rather than an entry model.
