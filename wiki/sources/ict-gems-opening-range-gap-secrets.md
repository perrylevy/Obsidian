# ICT Gems - Opening Range Gap Secrets

## Summary

- The clip treats the opening-range gap as a real liquidity void, not just a decorative visual.
- The midpoint / consequent encroachment of the gap is framed as the highest-probability reaction level, especially when the market has not shown enough strength to fill the gap completely.
- A large bearish gap that fails to close is read as strong directional intent: bodies matter more than wicks, and the market often only wants to rebalance to the midpoint or upper quadrant before resuming.
- Relative equal highs / lows and the first fair-value-gap response are used as the practical context around the gap.
- The main message is to measure the gap, judge the bodies, and let the market tell you whether it is expanding or pausing to rebalance.
