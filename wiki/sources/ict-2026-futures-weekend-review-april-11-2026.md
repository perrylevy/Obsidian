# ICT 2026 Futures Weekend Review | April 11, 2026

## Summary

- ICT opens by clarifying when he uses the continuous contract versus the front-month contract: the continuous chart is for clean historical context and old-price comparison, while the front month is the live delivery contract with the actionable intraday levels.
- Large gaps between the prior day's settlement and the 9:30 RTH open can stay open for a while; ICT explicitly says there is no fixed time limit on when price returns to those levels.
- The opening-range gap is the first-hour bias anchor: ICT says the half-gap / consequent encroachment is the first draw, and that the opening gap being above or below price determines whether the initial look is buy side or sell side.
- The clip states that if you do not know the half-gap level during the first hour of trading, you are guessing; the first 60 minutes from 9:30 to 10:30 are the first hour's dealing range.
- The transcript also uses breaker and suspension-block examples to show that body acceptance inside the right half of the structure matters more than wick noise or chart-toggle confusion.
