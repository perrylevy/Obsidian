# 2025 Lecture Series - ICT Venom Example May 12, 2025

## Summary

- The clip opens with a bullish higher-time-frame bias: as long as the market stays above the daily fair gap and does not close below it, ICT wants to remain bullish and keep targeting the prior swing high.
- Because the day opens on a premium gap and the market is already expanded, the lesson is not to chase the open; the cleaner path is to wait for discount, then look for a sellside raid and a deferred long.
- The named Venom model is a bullish opening-session setup: after a single-candle run below sellside liquidity, ICT wants a buyer at the opening price or lower, with the selloff acting as the bait and the reversal candle leaving the setup behind it.
- The 1-minute first presented FVG for Monday becomes the weekly anchor once 9:31+ prints, and ICT keeps it extended through the week as the recurring guide for the opening frame.
- The clip also frames the move as a time-distortion day: a lot of sideways candles wear traders out, but the structural clue is still the same — run the stop pool, validate the bullish displacement, then use the first presentable array or inversion FVG for continuation.
