# ICT 2024 Mentorship How To Manage Missed Entries September 17, 2024

## Summary

- ICT frames a missed entry as a recoverable execution problem, not a reason to abandon the session: if the move is still active, you can still work with lower timeframes or the next relevant macro window instead of freezing.
- The teaching stays anchored to the opening-range gap and first-presented-FVG style morning map, but it broadens the toolset to 1-minute, 15-second, 5-minute, and even hourly analogies so the trader can re-enter the move at a smaller scale.
- He emphasizes that the point is not prediction or perfect timing; the point is to stay tied to the current session structure and avoid emotional paralysis after you miss the first trigger.
- The clip also reinforces that no-bias / first-hour logic remains valid even after a miss: the answer is to keep reading the session, not to force a trade because you feel left behind.
