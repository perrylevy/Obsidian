# New York Reversal Explained | Daily Profile Strategy

This source defines the New York reversal as a daily-profile setup where London fails to fully reach a higher-time-frame level, leaving New York to complete the run and then reverse.

The practical idea is that the reversal is not just a local chart pattern. It is a session-structure read: London sets the stage, New York engages the level, and CISD confirms that the market has shifted delivery.

It is best treated as a daily-profile filter rather than a standalone entry model. The important question is whether New York is finishing a move that London left incomplete and whether the market is reversing from a meaningful higher-time-frame level.
