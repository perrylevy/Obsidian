# ICT 2026 Lecture: Trading ATH In The ICT PD Array Matrix — April 20, 2026

**Video**: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r-ztz7kReYc) | **Duration**: 01:02:14 | **Channel**: The Inner Circle Trader

## Summary

This lecture teaches how to trade at all-time highs (ATH) when price enters "no man's land" with no previous structure above. The solution is the **PD Array Matrix** combined with **continuous contract toggling** to bring price back into a tradeable range. ICT walks through a live Monday, April 20, 2026 MNQ session using premium wicks, TGIF levels, opening-range gap consequent encroachment, inversion FVGs, and the "bolo" defensive stop concept.

### Core themes

1. **Continuous contract toggling** — switch from monthly contract to continuous contract to see price in relation to previous ATHs.
2. **Premium wick grading** — find the longest wick on the daily chart, grade it to its consequent encroachment; that midpoint becomes a key decision level.
3. **TGIF (Thank God It's Friday)** — a 20–30% weekly-range retracement that typically forms Thursday/Friday on an up-close bullish weekly candle.
4. **Opening-range gap consequent encroachment** — the midpoint of the Friday-to-Monday gap is the test level; bodies staying in the lower half = bearish.
5. **Inversion FVG** — when price trades outside a gap then retraces back into it; entry at midpoint, confirmation from body behavior.
6. **Bolo** — a defensive stop level, not an entry point. If price trades to the bolo, you're wrong.
7. **Partial-profit discipline** — take partials at each sell-side liquidity pool; don't pyramid re-entries too close to partial exits.

## Key sections

### The ATH problem and continuous contract solution

When price reaches all-time highs, there is no previous structure above to reference. Retail traders expect a return to "previous highs," but this is ineffective for ICT methodology.

**Solution**: Toggle **continuous contracts ON**:
- Monthly contracts (e.g., "June 2026") show price far from previous highs.
- Continuous contracts (no month in title) bring price back into proximity with previous ATHs.
- This reveals the true relationship between current price and prior price action.

**Example**: MNQ daily chart — October 30th, 2025 high at **26,864.5** becomes visible and tradeable only on the continuous contract.

### Premium wick and consequent encroachment

**Premium wick**: A wick that extends above the candlestick body. When price is *below* the wick, that wick is "premium" relative to current market price. The opposite = discount wick.

**Grading the wick**:
1. Find the longest wick vs. surrounding candles.
2. Mark the high and the close of that candle.
3. The midpoint = **consequent encroachment**.

**October 30th, 2025 example**:
- High: **26,864.5**
- Consequent encroachment: **26,745** (purple level)
- This level is part of Friday's daily range and the current trading range.

### TGIF framework

**TGIF** = a specific price action pattern on Fridays (or as early as Thursday 1 PM ET).

**Setup requirements**:
- Up-close bullish weekly candle.
- Look for retracement of **20–30% of weekly range** (up to 40% in some cases).
- Measured from weekly high downward.

**How to calculate**:
1. Fibonacci tool: anchor to weekly low, drag to weekly high.
2. Change setting from 50 to **2** (= 20% level).
3. Monitor Thursday 1 PM ET through Friday close.
4. If not triggered by Tuesday, abandon (likely longer-term move).

**This week's MNQ TGIF level**: **26,489.25** (green level).

### Friday's opening range gap (RTH OG)

- Friday 4:14 PM ET settlement → Monday 9:30 AM ET RTH open.
- **Gap type**: Discount gap (opened lower near ATH).
- **Consequent encroachment**: Deep blue bold dashed line = the test level.

**Interpretation**:
- Discount gap + bodies closing in lower half = **bearish bias**.
- No body at/above consequent encroachment = **rejection of higher prices**.
- Indicates potential for full gap closure or lower.

### Inversion fair value gap

**Definition**: When price trades **outside** a fair value gap, then **comes back into it**.
- Color-coded differently on ICT charts.
- Entry zone: **midpoint to low of the gap**.
- Anything inside the gap is valid for entries.

**Monday's execution**:
- Inversion FVG identified in electronic trading hours.
- Price tested it multiple times.
- Each test showed rejection (bodies blunted, no upside follow-through).
- Shorted at consequent encroachment and subsequent tests.

### The test — laboratory experiment approach

**Question**: Can price get above consequent encroachment of the wick / gap?
- **YES** (body at or above level) = probably wrong, market going higher.
- **NO** (rejection, blunted bodies) = market likely going lower.

**Monday's observation**:
- Price tested RTH ORG consequent encroachment.
- Left NO body near it.
- Immediate displacement lower = bearish confirmation.
- Shorted at consequent encroachment of the wick.

### Sell-side liquidity pools

- Areas where buyers previously entered = now sell-side liquidity.
- ICT placed multiple limit orders below these pools.
- As each pool was hit, partial profits were taken.
- Final target: Friday RTH ORG consequent encroachment (**26,745**).

### The bolo — defensive stop level

> "The bolo is NOT an entry point — it's a defensive level."

- Stop loss placed **outside** the bolo.
- If price trades to the bolo = **you're wrong**, don't re-enter.
- Allows trader to step away from chart without worry.

### Position management rules

- **Don't pyramid** entries too close to partial exits.
- If partial taken at level X, don't re-enter at X+20 handles.
- Wait for **deeper retracement** (20–30 handles) before adding.
- Take partials at each significant liquidity pool.
