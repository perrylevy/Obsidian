# My Forever Model: The Strategy That Has Made Me Over Half A Million (INSANE VALUE)

The clip argues for a single high-probability execution model built from manipulation, inversion FVG, change in state of delivery, optional SMT, and an opposing liquidity target.

The practical lesson is that the model works because it has logic: first manipulate against the intended move, then invert through inefficiency, then confirm with delivery change, and finally target the liquidity pool the market is actually drawing toward.

The clip also stresses that the setup becomes stronger when the higher-time-frame context already points in the same direction and when the trader can wait for the pullback instead of forcing the first touch.
