# ICT 2024 Mentorship Limit Orders & Volatility Pinball Drills October 01, 2024

## Summary

- ICT presents the session as a practice drill for desensitizing the trader to entries, exits, and stop-outs; the goal is skill-building, not proving a trade model on that day.
- He argues that limit orders are the proper execution tool for actual trading, while market orders are mainly for demonstration or emergency exits.
- The drill uses repeated small executions, tiny timeframes, and journaling so the trader learns to read price action and get comfortable with short selling, partials, and quick invalidation.
- The clip also keeps returning to body-versus-wick logic, volume imbalance, and a strict stop placement rule outside the creating candle when a VI or FVG is being used.
