# Market Structure Shift - ICT Concepts

## Summary

- The clip defines displacement as aggressive full-bodied delivery, often visible as a fair value gap.
- A market structure shift is the confirming break that tells you the prior directional delivery has ended; the sweep alone is not the trade.
- The highest-probability version waits for price to sweep liquidity, react, and then break structure after the confirming move.
- Once structure shifts, the trader can look for the entry inside the new leg via a fair value gap or order block instead of entering on the sweep itself.
- The source frames MSS as a confirmation event that often aims at the opposite-side liquidity pool after the break.
