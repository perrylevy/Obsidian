# The Confirmation Model

This clip presents a repeatable confirmation checklist for getting aligned with a trade before entry.

The structure is: find a higher-time-frame fair value gap and a clear draw, wait for a liquidity sweep inside OTE, confirm SMT and internal liquidity behavior, then look for an IFVG and a change in state of delivery before taking the trade toward opposing liquidity.

The practical takeaway is that the model is not about predicting every tick. It is about requiring enough confirmation that the entry feels mechanical, the stop is obvious, and the target is the next clear liquidity pool.
