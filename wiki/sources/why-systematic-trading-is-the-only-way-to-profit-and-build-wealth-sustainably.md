# Why Systematic Trading is the Only Way to Profit and Build Wealth Sustainably

This clip argues that profitability comes from a system followed with absolute discipline, not from intelligence, effort, or a vague sense of market feel.

The reusable rule is that a real trading system has four parts: objective entry rules, pre-defined exit rules, fixed position sizing, and trade-management rules. If you deviate from any of them, you are no longer trading the system; you are gambling.

The clip also recommends building trust through backtesting, forward testing, and pre-trade checklists, then journaling rule adherence so success is measured by execution quality rather than only P&L.
