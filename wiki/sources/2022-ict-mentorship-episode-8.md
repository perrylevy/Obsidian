# 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 8 - Applying Institutional Order Flow To Forex Markets

**Video**: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7rbV8aWkcqY) | **Duration**: 13:32 | **Channel**: The Inner Circle Trader | **Published**: February 10, 2022

## Summary

This episode applies the ICT institutional order flow framework to **forex markets**, demonstrating how the same concepts (market structure, fair value gaps, liquidity, killzones) work across asset classes. It features a live **Euro/Yen (EUR/JPY)** example with a complete top-down analysis from daily chart → 15-minute → 5-minute → 3-minute → 1-minute. The episode reinforces the canonical killzone boundaries (London 2:00–5:00 AM NY, NY 7:00–10:00 AM NY), emphasizes TradingView New York local time calibration, and introduces **component currency analysis** for exotic crosses.

### Core themes

1. **Forex application of ICT concepts** — the same institutional order flow framework (MSS, FVG, liquidity, killzones) applies to forex pairs, not just futures.
2. **Killzone boundaries** — London session 2:00–5:00 AM NY, NY session 7:00–10:00 AM NY; Asia and London close trades are possible but not preferred.
3. **Top-down analysis process** — daily chart for bias → 15-minute for structure → 5-minute/3-minute/1-minute for entry refinement.
4. **Component currency analysis** — for exotic crosses like EUR/JPY, analyze each component separately (6E Euro futures for EUR bias, Yen futures for JPY bias) to confirm directional thesis.
5. **Fibonacci retracement for targets** — anchor to previous swings on the daily chart; standard deviation negative one setting for target projection.
6. **New York local time mandatory** — all charts must be set to NY local time, not the trader's local time.

## Key sections

### Data source & setup

- **Forex data feed**: forex.com only (via TradingView).
- **Time frame focus**: Daily charts and lower timeframes (5-min, 3-min, 1-min).
- **Critical requirement**: Always set TradingView to **New York local time** (not your local time).

### Market structure analysis

The episode demonstrates identifying trading opportunities through:
- **Relative equal lows** — marked price levels.
- **Swing highs and lows** — two points of market structure moving upside.
- **Fair value gaps** — price levels between swings.
- **Buy side liquidity** — old highs that act as potential draw points.

> "It's likely to draw to it just like you know playing horseshoes — you play the game horseshoes, not every single time you throw a horseshoe is it going to go on that post but it's still fun to play right"

### Trading sessions & killzones

**New York Session (ICT Killzone)**:
- **Operating hours**: 7:00 AM – 10:00 AM New York local time.
- **Setup formation**: NY session trades form during this window.
- **Strategy**: Hunt for setups providing fair value gap trades.

**London Session**:
- **Operating hours**: 2:00 AM – 5:00 AM New York local time.
- **Strategy**: Look for setups providing fair value gap trades during this bracket.

**Note**: Asia and London close trades are possible but not preferred; focus on NY and London sessions.

### Practical application: Euro/Yen example

**Daily chart analysis**:
- Market trades down through relative equal lows, then rallies higher.
- Creates two points of market structure moving upside.
- Fair value gap identified between swings.
- February 10, 2022 candle showed consolidation followed by another run into highs.

**15-minute breakdown**:
- Rectangle marked to identify price structure relative to NY session.
- Midnight NY time shows initial rally.
- NY session entry point identified.

**5-minute to 1-minute progression**:
1. Identify swing low taken out.
2. Identify swing high taken out.
3. Check for fair value gaps in price leg.
4. Trace down to 3-minute chart for confirmation.
5. Validate swing low and swing high conditions.

**Target calculation**:
- Use **Fibonacci retracement** anchored to previous swings.
- Example result: 133.153 level (matching actual high of 133.15).
- Standard deviation: negative one setting.
- Reference: Full Fibonacci setup shown in "OTE Primer" video.

### Pair analysis framework

**Understanding exotic crosses**:
Analyze component currencies separately:
- **Euro**: Strong (going higher).
- **Yen**: Weak (going lower).
- **Result**: Euro/Yen should go higher.

**Process**:
1. Check 6E (Euro futures) front month continuous.
2. Determine directional bias for each component.
3. Apply time of day + pattern recognition.
4. Execute trade.

### Key insights

**Why the instructor dislikes Yen pairs**:
- Tend to have double returns to specific levels.
- Less predictable movement patterns.
- Other forex markets provide better trading opportunities.

**Entry strategy**:
- Fair value gaps provide entry points.
- Entries can "stab down" into gaps — be mindful.
- Aim for entries on the identified fair value gap level.

**Expansion moves without range**:
When no established range exists:
- Use previous swing lows/highs as reference.
- Apply Fibonacci retracement to prior swings.
- Identify consolidation and retracement patterns.
- Project targets based on historical structure.
