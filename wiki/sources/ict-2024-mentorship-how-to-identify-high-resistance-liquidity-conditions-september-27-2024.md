# ICT 2024 Mentorship How To Identify High Resistance Liquidity Conditions September 27, 2024

## Summary

- The clip makes the 9:31 rule explicit: the 9:30 candle is visible and useful for context, but the actual first fair value gap for the model begins with the next candle at 9:31.
- It treats the opening-range gap as the real morning frame, measured from the prior 4:14 p.m. settlement to the current 9:30 open, and then asks whether price is staying in the correct half of that gap.
- Friday context matters: after a week of upside, relative equal lows become the likely sellside draw, and the session can trade as an exhaustion-gap / TGIF retracement day.
- The lecture emphasizes that the path inside the gap should be read with bodies, not just wicks: wicks may test the area, but bodies above or below the key zone tell the stronger story.
- It repeats the broader resistance-liquidity lesson from the October 28 lecture: the trader should use the time-based model, the opening gap, and the nearby liquidity structure to decide whether the morning is tradable or whether the setup is still too noisy.
