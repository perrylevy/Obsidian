# 2025 Lecture Series - NQ NFP Algorithmic Price Delivery | 04/04/2025

## Summary

- ICT treats the 9:30 open as the start of a clean opening-range frame, then immediately uses the first presented 1-minute FVG as the morning’s working array.
- The first meaningful pulse is the 9:50–10:10 macro, while the 10:00 NFP release acts as a volatility driver that should be absorbed into the existing opening narrative instead of forcing a fresh bias.
- Once the opening array validates, ICT reframes the same area as an inversion FVG and uses consequent encroachment / body acceptance to judge whether the move is still alive.
- The clip adds a second late-morning cadence: after the open validates, 10:50–11:10 becomes a continuation / target window rather than a new morning thesis.
- The same session logic repeats in the PM: 1:30–2:00 has its own opening-range FVG, and 3:15–3:45 is the later delivery window.
- Gray pool, immediate rebalance, and bullish order block refinement are entry techniques inside the same opening-range framework; the wiki canonical term stays “bullish order block.”
