# ICT 2026 Futures Review — April 24, 2026

**Video**: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2OVAvl4H3IY) | **Duration**: 49:32 | **Channel**: The Inner Circle Trader

## Summary

This review covers a live NQ/MNQ market read from Friday, April 24, 2026, including a detailed walkthrough of a Wednesday–Thursday short trade executed at all-time highs, a Friday morning gap analysis, and extended commentary on weekend geopolitical risk and trading psychology.

### Core themes

1. **Three Drives pattern at all-time highs** — ICT demonstrates how a classic three-drive structure (also called "three Indians" in Street Smarts literature) can stage smart-money shorts even at record prices, trapping breakout and bull-flag traders.
2. **Consequent encroachment as the decision line** — the midpoint of the opening-range gap and of the daily structure is used to judge whether price has accepted or rejected a level.
3. **Partial-profit discipline** — every new lower low triggered a partial exit; the final cover was in the lower 90% of the daily range.
4. **Weekend gap risk** — heavy military equipment and carrier deployments to the Middle East, plus a ceasefire interpreted as regrouping, created elevated black-swan risk for the Sunday 6 PM futures open.
5. **Account-preservation psychology** — trade smaller size, lower leverage, and reduced frequency in volatile conditions; the "big payout" trap destroys funded accounts.

## Key sections

### Opening setup (9:30 AM ET)

- Premium gap at the cash open.
- Consequent encroachment of the opening-range gap near **27,080**.
- Relative equal lows formed smoothly beneath that level.
- Pre-posted level **27,063** (tweeted in advance) — the high of the opening gap's consequent encroachment.
- Price was trading in the upper octant of the opening structure, suggesting limited upside and a likely draw lower.

### Three Drives pattern — Wednesday & Thursday short

ICT describes a classic **three drives pattern** forming at all-time highs:

1. **High #1** — taken above buy-side liquidity. Market extremely overbought.
2. **High #2** — formed as price pulled back slightly, baiting breakout traders.
3. **High #3** — final drive that trapped bull-flag and opening-range-breakout traders.

> "When you see these types of things when you see a high above buy side liquidity when it's extremely overbought... the likelihood of drawing down into this area here builds the likelihood of them staging in short positions."

**Why the pattern works:**
- Breakout traders are caught long.
- Bull-flag traders repeatedly buy pullbacks that never sustain.
- Previous day's high acts as resistance.
- Fair value gaps below provide the draw target.

**Execution:**
- Shorted at all-time highs on Wednesday and again on Thursday.
- Addressed viewer pushback ("you can't short ATH") by showing the pattern works regardless of ATH status.
- Target: **26,805** (hit successfully).

### Order-flow confirmation

- Body stayed in the lower half after touching the midpoint — a sign of weakness.
- Consequent encroachment respected; bodies stayed outside the midpoint by the low.
- Deep retracement was weathered with a wide stop, trusting the higher-timeframe weekly profile.

### Position management — partial profits

- Took partials on every new lower low.
- Avoided sharing the same candlestick space as previous candles — a visual cue to reduce risk.
- Final exit: lower 90% of the daily range.
- Missed an additional 100+ handles on the full run, but the partial-exit discipline kept the trade profitable.

> "There's never been a partial profit that's been unprofitable. It's never happened in history. No matter who did it, no matter what style of trading you do, it's always been profitable."

### Friday morning observations

- Large 7 AM push made the morning session feel lethargic — not interested in chasing rallies.
- New-day opening gap not yet tested.
- Relative equal lows from Thursday afternoon still in play; potential revisit Monday if not hit Friday.
- Key level to watch: **27,063** (opening gap high consequent encroachment).

### Geopolitical & weekend risk

- Heavy artillery and equipment being sent to the Middle East.
- Additional aircraft carrier deployment expected.
- Ceasefire characterized as a regrouping period, not genuine peace.
- Anticipated escalation over the weekend.

> "I believe we're going to see that type of event... where we have a market at a really nice healthy close on a Friday and then something terrible is going to happen and we're going to open up significantly lower as a result of it."

**Market implications:**
- Potential for significant Sunday 6 PM gap opening lower.
- All-time highs without strong conviction rally = suspicious.
- Stocks lack fundamental justification for current levels.

### Trading psychology & account preservation

**Current market conditions:**
- Higher risk environment requires lower leverage.
- Reduce trading frequency to offset increased volatility.
- Avoid oversizing positions chasing "lucky" big wins.
- Funded account failures often result from chasing large payouts.

> "That is a trap right now... you're landing in Venus fly traps and you're getting devoured, but you're putting yourself there because you're trying to chase something instead of saying, 'Let me just try to get one micro once or twice.'"

**Key principles:**
- Don't chase breakouts or bull flags.
- Use previous day highs/lows as an endless supply of setups.
- Context matters more than confirmation.
- Tape reading reveals order-flow intentions.
- Trade smaller size = better longevity.
- Consistent micro trades > occasional large losses.
