# Self Sabotage: You're Afraid of the Life Trading Will Give You.

The clip frames self-sabotage as a response to responsibility: traders may unconsciously break their own rules because success would require a heavier identity, more accountability, and a different life.

The practical lesson is that destructive behavior often comes from fear of what winning demands, not from a lack of desire to win. If the trader still identifies with the losing, low-responsibility version of self, the account tends to revert back to that baseline.

The clip also ties account blowups, post-payout overtrading, and hesitation together as manifestations of the same inner conflict between the trader's stated goals and the identity they are actually protecting.
