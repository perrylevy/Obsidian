## Summary

Episode 19 compresses three linked ideas into one execution lesson: order blocks are change-in-state-of-delivery levels, the Judas swing is the engineered counter-move that traps traders ahead of the true move, and Power of Three describes the larger accumulation → manipulation → distribution sequence that the market tends to repeat across timeframes.

## Core themes

1. **Order blocks as delivery-change levels** — not generic supply/demand zones.
2. **Judas swing as the trap move** — the move opposite to the expected daily direction.
3. **Power of Three across timeframes** — midnight open, 8:30 open, and the daily range all participate in the same scaffold.
4. **Candle bodies matter more than wicks** — the bodies reveal the underlying narrative.
5. **Liquidity targeting** — the algorithm seeks resting buy stops above highs and sell stops below lows.
6. **Execution discipline** — do not pick tops and bottoms; wait for the delivery sequence to reveal itself.

## Key doctrine notes

### Order blocks

The episode frames an order block as a price reference that the algorithm returns to after displacement. It is a bookmark in delivery, not just a visually convenient zone.

### Judas swing

The Judas swing is the counter-move against the daily expectation. The episode also notes that multiple Judas swings can exist at different timeframes, so the concept is fractal rather than single-use.

### Power of Three

The episode presents Power of Three as the three-phase backdrop that explains why the morning move can be a trap before the true directional expansion.
