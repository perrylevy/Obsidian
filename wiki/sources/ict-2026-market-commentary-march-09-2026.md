# ICT 2026 Market Commentary | March 09, 2026

## Summary

- ICT treats the 9:30 open as the anchor, and because the open prints above prior settlement the session starts with a premium opening-range gap rather than a neutral one.
- The morning is still a tape-read problem, not a prediction problem: ICT calls it a 50/50 open until price validates the thesis, and he uses the 10:00–11:00 window as the confirmation / management block.
- The first presented FVG is not a random later gap. In this clip ICT anchors the working morning FVG to the 9:44 sequence and then reads the 7-minute-after-10 candle as the sellside discriminator.
- The same commentary explicitly states that a propulsion block is an order block, and that the minimum requirement is that price has not breached mean threshold inside the composite range.
- The session also reinforces the broader 9:30–11:00 playbook: open, validate, then let the later AM tape decide whether sellside stays open or the bullish structure continues.
