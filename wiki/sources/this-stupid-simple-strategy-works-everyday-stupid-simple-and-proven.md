# This Stupid Simple Strategy Works Everyday (Stupid Simple And Proven)

The clip's core rule is a three-step alignment model: first wait for price to tap a higher-time-frame FVG, then wait for a 5-minute FVG to open, then trade only when price gives an inversion out of that lower-timeframe gap.

It is presented as a low-R, high-win-rate approach. The emphasis is not on complex pattern stacking; it is on one clean HTF key level, one lower-timeframe confirmation gap, and one liquidity target.

The clip also adds a practical management rule: use the swing that manipulated into the 5-minute gap as the stop reference, move to breakeven at the internal high / low, and if the broader draw remains intact after a stop-out, a re-entry can still be valid.
