# 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 14 - MSS + FVG Long Execution Example

**Video**: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NUdu1n-ML98) | **Duration**: 4:38 | **Channel**: The Inner Circle Trader | **Published**: February 28, 2022

## Summary

A short execution demo showing ICT applying the prior lesson in real time: a bullish market structure shift is already in place, price is expected to revisit a fair value gap from Friday's close, and the long is taken with buyside liquidity above the short-term highs as the destination. The clip is less about new doctrine and more about showing how MSS + FVG + liquidity expectation combine into a live trade plan.

### Core themes

1. **Bullish market structure shift** — the setup begins with an upside MSS already established.
2. **Fair value gap entry** — the limit buy is placed into the gap from Friday's close.
3. **Buyside target** — price is expected to run above the prior short-term high / new equity high area.
4. **Retail resistance contrast** — ICT explicitly notes that retail traders will see the level as resistance and short into it.
5. **Trade management** — the clip emphasizes watching candles and gap closure, not just the P&L number.

---

## Key sections

### Setup and expectation

ICT opens by stating that the market has already produced a **market structure shift to the upside** and that he is looking for a **gap fill from Friday's close**. The planned long is therefore not a chase; it is a retest of the inefficiency left behind by the prior displacement.

He frames the target as a push above the nearby highs, with the more ambitious objective around the 14,220 area if the move expands cleanly.

### Entry logic

The limit order is placed into the fair value gap while he watches whether price keeps expanding toward the gap rather than stalling. He repeatedly emphasizes that he is watching the candles and the gap closure, not the dollar amount under open profit.

The clip also shows the typical ICT patience move: price can pause and consolidate near the gap before expanding, so the trader has to let the market do its work.

### Retail resistance framing

ICT notes that retail traders will look at the nearby level and assume **resistance**, because price previously moved down from there. His frame is the opposite: the area is a buy-side objective and a place where a long should be expected to run through rather than fail.

### Outcome

The limit order is filled and the trade is managed as a live example of how the MSS + FVG framework is supposed to operate in practice.
