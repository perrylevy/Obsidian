# ICT 2026 Smart Money Concepts Lecture | February 23, 2026

## Summary

- ICT frames the tape as a high-resistance liquidity run when the market is staying inside the same prior-candle range; on a Monday, that compression can keep the low deferred until the market earns a close below the relevant consequent encroachment.
- Consequent encroachment is treated as the practical continuation check: a close below CE in a discount wick supports continuation lower, while failure to close below CE keeps the market in the high-resistance state.
- The new week opening gap remains part of the live Monday scaffold, and ICT explicitly describes the 9:30 open as a 30-minute opening range that can probe buy-side and then sell-side liquidity before the real move emerges.
- The clip also restates the direct connection between order-block opening price and change in state of delivery, using the live Monday chart as the example.
- Later examples reinforce the body-vs-wick rule inside FVG/CE work: wicks may probe through the midpoint, but bodies failing to reclaim the upper half preserve the directional thesis.
