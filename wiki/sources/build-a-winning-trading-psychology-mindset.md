# Build a Winning Trading Psychology Mindset

## Summary

- The core distinction is not money-making vs not money-making; it is performance vs outcome. Winning in trading means performing well, not controlling the payoff.
- The clip argues that the trader must become a superb loser: small losses accepted cleanly, no revenge behavior, and no attempt to force certainty out of uncertainty.
- The mind that succeeds in business often fails in trading because trading requires patience, probabilistic thinking, and emotional regulation rather than a control-oriented success script.
- The "cougar vs lion" metaphor is used to show that the trading brain must be patient, ambush-oriented, and comfortable waiting for the setup instead of chasing action.
