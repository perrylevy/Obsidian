# 2025 Lecture Series - NQ Heavy Manipulation Review

## Summary

- The clip argues that the day is heavily manually intervened in: price chops around, rips through levels, and becomes much harder to read than a clean run (@ 8:16, @ 15:16).
- The daily premium wick, daily volume imbalance, and first presented FVG are still the framework, but the tape is noisy enough that risk management and stop discipline become the dominant concern (@ 1:02, @ 4:31, @ 11:29).
- A 10:50–11:10 macro and a bearish order block / institutional orderflow entry drill are used for the short setup, with the move later described as a turtle-soup false breakout around sell-side liquidity (@ 11:57, @ 12:30, @ 12:49, @ 13:09).
- The review repeatedly notes that the market can sweep, reaccumulate, and then move away from the level once the internal structure is validated or invalidated (@ 16:31, @ 17:54, @ 18:28).
- Late-session continuation then reuses the afternoon first presented FVG and the daily volume imbalance as targets (@ 18:45, @ 19:29, @ 19:55).
- This review is best treated as a noisy-market framing example: it shows how core doctrine survives in heavy-manipulation conditions, but with tighter risk discipline and less trust in the clean textbook read.
