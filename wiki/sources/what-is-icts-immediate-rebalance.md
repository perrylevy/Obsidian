# What Is ICT's Immediate Rebalance?

## Summary

- ICT describes the **immediate rebalance** as one of his PD arrays: a rapid repricing back to a specific reference level, rather than a deferred revisit to an FVG later in time.
- The worked NQ example uses the May 2023 hourly chart: a 10 a.m. candle reprices back to the low of the 8 a.m. candle and the weekly gap low, which ICT calls an immediate rebalance.
- The key distinction is that this is **not** a normal FVG reaction. An FVG can remain a deferred draw; an immediate rebalance is read as the market already having booked the level and now snapping to it.
- ICT emphasizes that this signature can produce a sudden one-sided delivery leg and says not to take partials on that leg; the target behavior is sharp and complete rather than slow and incremental.
- He also distinguishes the concept from a liquidity void: the important point is whether the market has already offered the required repricing, not merely whether a chart shows a blank area.
- This source is a direct doctrinal statement of immediate rebalance and should anchor the new concept page.
