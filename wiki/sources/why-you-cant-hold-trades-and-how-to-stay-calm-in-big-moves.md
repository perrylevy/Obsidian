# Why You Can’t Hold Trades and How to Stay Calm in Big Moves

This clip is a trading-psychology lesson about why traders cut winners too early and how to stay calm while a position is working.

The reusable rule is that holding a trade is a separate skill from entering one. To hold well, you need a pre-planned stop and target, trust your edge, and stop letting the anxious part of your mind rewrite the trade in the middle of the move.

The clip recommends simple execution supports: reduce lower-timeframe noise, use alerts instead of staring, consider partials if they make it easier to stay in the trade, and rehearse patience before the market opens.
