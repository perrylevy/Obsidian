## Summary

Episode 17 translates the 2022 mentorship model into a concrete EURUSD-style forex application. ## Core themes

1. **Institutional price levels** — ICT describes four practical round-number bands: big-figure 00s, mid-figure 50s, 80-20 levels, and smaller rounded levels that act as targets and rejection zones.
2. **New York Open Kill Zone** — the execution window is centered on the New York open; if 10:00 news is pending, the window can extend to 11:30.
3. **Opening-price logic** — the 8:30 open is read against the midnight open as part of the bias / Power of Three framework.
4. **EURUSD daily application** — the episode walks through a daily-chart forex example using market structure shift, midnight opening price, and the 8:30 opening price as anchors.
5. **Intermediate-term high framing** — the forum summary notes that an ITH is characterized by lower short-term highs on both sides, giving it a distinctive institutional-supply shape.

## Key doctrine notes

### Institutional levels

The episode treats round numbers as professional reference points rather than arbitrary whole numbers. The useful operational point is not just that price may react at those levels, but that exits should often be staged a few pips before the level to reduce slippage and avoid being tagged on the exact number.

### Opening-price and PO3 logic

The forum summary captures the key conditional logic:

- if the 8:30 opening price is lower than the midnight opening price and the market is bearish, the opening-price relationship supports the bearish Power of Three framing,
- the opposite logic applies in bullish conditions.

This is a practical extension of the PO3 model: the session open, the midnight open, and the post-open expansion together define the directional map.

### Daily-chart forex example

The episode applies the framework to EURUSD on the daily chart, using:

- market structure shift,
- midnight opening price,
- 8:30 opening price,
- and the relationship between the daily open and institutional levels.

The forum summary also mentions a short trade entry, P/L projection, and an analysis of missing structure shifts, which reinforces the idea that the daily chart is the primary map even when the execution is intraday.
