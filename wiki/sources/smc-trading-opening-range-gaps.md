# 2025 Lecture Series - SMC Trading Opening Range Gaps

## Summary

- The lecture distinguishes the 9:30–10:00 RTH opening range gap from ETH new-day / new-week opening gaps and insists the chart be on regular trading hours and New York time for the ORG rule to apply.
- Premium opening range gaps are measured from the prior 4:14 p.m. settlement to the 9:30 a.m. open; the gap can be graded by quadrants and consequent encroachment, and very large gaps often do not fully close immediately.
- A gap over roughly 120 handles is treated as a likely continuation / partial-fill candidate rather than a clean full reversion; 75–120 handles is the gray area where the trader leans on other context.
- The lecture explicitly links opening-range gap handling to premarket liquidity, economic calendar context, and the question of whether the market should even be traded before the 9:30 opening range completes.
- It also reinforces the workflow discipline of using the opening range as a structured frame rather than overloading the chart with unnecessary tools or assumptions.
