# How To Use The Economic Calendar For Trading

## Summary

- The clip turns the economic calendar into a weekly risk filter: avoid the day before high-impact news, avoid the release itself, and then trade the post-news aftermath if the profile is clean.
- It sorts events by impact and applies different restraint rules for high-impact, medium-impact, and low-impact news, with high-impact events like FOMC and NFP getting the strictest treatment.
- The weekly profile matters as much as the event itself: accumulation early in the week, manipulation around the event, and distribution / continuation after the news can all be read as part of one sequence.
- The examples use standard-deviation projections, order-blocks, fair-value-gap reactivity, and liquidity targets to show how calendar awareness becomes an execution filter rather than a prediction tool.
