# Storytellers — Daily High To Low, June 21, 2025

ICT-direct NQ (September contract) walkthrough framing a daily-high-to-low read **before the 9:30 open** using a prior daily wick and its midpoint / consequent encroachment. Useful for the wiki's intraday NQ workflow because it names the flagship setups as the expected expressions of the pre-set frame.

## Summary

- The pre-session read was posted **before 5:00 a.m. NY** on X — the framing was produced with no delayed data available (@ 1:01, @ 4:31).
- The chosen anchor was a specific daily wick; the **midpoint / consequent encroachment of that wick down to its close** was called out as "the heart of where the setups would form" for the session (@ 3:34).
- ICT explicitly names the flagship setups that operate inside that anchored zone: **Silver Bullet, Market Maker Buy Model, Market Maker Sell Model, Unicorn setup** (@ 3:34–3:56). This clip is the cleanest source for treating CE of a daily wick as the intraday setup pocket across all four models.
- Context rule: on a Friday going into the weekend — especially with Middle East military risk in the background — do not expect fresh buying to push to new highs; anticipate a daily-high-to-low read on NQ (@ 2:47, @ 3:06).
- Wednesday's daily high carried forward into Friday because Thursday was a holiday. That high was read as a **retail breakout trap**, and the plan was to wait for the 9:30 open before acting (@ 11:29, @ 12:03).
- Teaching aside: learning this approach is pattern-recognition earned by back-testing and journaling; live signal services teach codependency, not bias construction (@ 5:01, @ 5:25, @ 5:44).

## Key quotes

- **@ 3:34** — "The midpoint or consequent encroachment of it to its close, that was going to be the heart of where the setups that you as students would be able to find like a silver bullet, a market maker buy model entry, a market maker sell model entry, a unicorn setup."
- **@ 2:47** — "Don't expect… new buying to send us to new highs."
- **@ 11:29** — "That high is Wednesday's daily high… running above that high, stopping out anyone that is short and tricking individuals thinking that it's a breakout."
- **@ 12:03** — "I am not interested in doing anything until 9:30 open."

## Operator use

1. Before the session, identify the most relevant **daily wick** relative to the live draw on liquidity.
2. Mark **consequent encroachment** (midpoint) of that wick down to its close (for a short setup) or up to its close (for a long setup).
3. Treat that zone as the **pocket** where the day's Silver Bullet, MM Buy/Sell, or Unicorn setup is likely to express.
4. Wait for the 9:30 open; do not pre-position based on pre-market probes of the prior daily high/low.
5. Read any run of Wednesday-or-prior daily high with Thursday closed as a likely retail trap before the real program starts.
