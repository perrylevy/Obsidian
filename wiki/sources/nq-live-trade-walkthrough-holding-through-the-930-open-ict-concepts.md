# NQ Live Trade Walkthrough - Holding Through the 9:30 Open!

## Summary

- The trade is framed by a bullish daily context, but the intraday path starts with a fast lower probe that is treated like a Judas swing rather than a thesis break.
- Initial risk sits beneath the consequent encroachment / midpoint of the first presented IFVG after 8:30, so the opening structure has a clear invalidation line.
- The trader wants partials before the 9:30 open and explicitly goes risk-free before the bell because the opening auction can bring whiplash.
- Once price closes back above CE / the IFVG midpoint, the trade is treated as supported again and the remaining runner can be managed toward buy-side liquidity around 19,000.
- The script also treats the sequence of consecutive up-close candles as a bullish breaker-style draw-through after the sell-side raid.
- This is a strong AM-session execution example of open-risk management, CE-based de-risking, and liquidity-draw continuation, not a new standalone model.
