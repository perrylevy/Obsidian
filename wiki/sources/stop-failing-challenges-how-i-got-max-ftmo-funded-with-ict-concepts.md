# Stop Failing Challenges: How I Got Max FTMO Funded w/ ICT Concepts

This clip frames challenge success as a function of following one repeatable sequence rather than trying to brute-force a payout through size or constant activity.

The named lesson is a sequence-style framework for bias and framing a trade, with the broader message that funded-account success comes from obeying a process under pressure.

The practical takeaway is that challenge failure is often a discipline / psychology failure before it is a chart-reading failure: keep the rules tight, wait for the right conditions, and do not let stress pull you into forcing trades.
