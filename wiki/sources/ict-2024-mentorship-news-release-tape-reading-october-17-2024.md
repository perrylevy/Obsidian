# ICT 2024 Mentorship News Release Tape Reading October 17, 2024

## Summary

- The clip treats 8:30 news as a liquidity smoke screen: retail sales, unemployment claims, and Philly Fed are on the docket, but the raw data itself is not the tradable edge.
- ICT explicitly says the market can use the release to run either direction first, then flip into the opposite side at the opening bell.
- The correct workflow is to map nearby buy-side and sell-side pools before the release, let the first impulse print, and only then read what structure remains.
- The tape-reading lesson is that price, not the report, tells you whether the day is headed for continuation or reversal.
