# $4,000 In 7 Minutes: Trading My ICT 30m PO3 Model

## Summary

- The trade is framed as a 30-minute Power of Three setup nested inside a larger weekly / daily PO3 read.
- The weekly backdrop was still bullish, but the daily PO3 was bearish because price had not yet performed the manipulation below the daily open and the 2-day open.
- The practical downside targets were the 2-day open, the 25% mark of the range, and the daily open — i.e. low-hanging-fruit draws on liquidity rather than deep range projections.
- Entry validation came from higher-time-frame narrative plus lower-time-frame SMT, a previous-day-high divergence across ES / Q / YM, and a one-minute order block with 15-second CISD confirmation.
- The trade is a clean example of how a sell-model-style morning can still sit inside a larger bullish weekly framework once the manipulation leg has done its job.
