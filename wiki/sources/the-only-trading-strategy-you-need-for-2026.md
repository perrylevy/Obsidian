# The Only Trading Strategy You Need For 2026

This clip presents a simple fractal trading model: establish a daily bias from the prior day's closure, identify a point of interest on the higher time frame, then drop to an aligned lower time frame and wait for a continuation order block after the change in state of delivery confirms the swing.

The core instruction is to trade only when the time frames line up. If the daily bias is bullish, the hourly should show a bullish continuation or reversal closure into a fair-value-gap, and the lower time frame should print a continuation structure that lets the trader enter with a clean stop beyond the protected swing and a target toward the next draw on liquidity.

A major refinement is that messy consolidations should be treated as stand-aside conditions. If the market cannot form a clean continuation and instead chops around, the trader should avoid forcing a trade and wait for a clearer swing, a clearer point of interest, or a cleaner break of the range.
