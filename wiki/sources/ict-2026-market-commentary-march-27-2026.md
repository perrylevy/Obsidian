# ICT 2026 Market Commentary | March 27, 2026

## Summary

- ICT starts the morning by watching the opening range and the opening-range gap, using the 9:30 print as the live anchor instead of abstract support/resistance.
- The first usable 1-minute FVG after 9:30 is treated as the first presented FVG, and the opening-range gap / consequent encroachment become the practical morning map.
- The clip shows a bullish suspension-block read on the daily chart: when bearish, price should stay in the lower half and keep candlestick bodies out of the upper half to preserve the downside thesis.
- Friday context matters. Even with a bearish lean, ICT is reluctant to force the downside if the open has not validated the clean break / retest sequence.
- Later in the session, the same opening frame is reused: if price can reclaim above the local level, it can return into the first presented FVG and the liquidity inside the opening-range gap becomes the target logic.
