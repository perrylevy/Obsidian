# Best ICT Turtle Soup Trading Strategy That Works! (Insane Winrate)

## Summary

- The strategy marks the Asian session high and low after the 2:00 a.m. New York close, then uses the first side taken as a bias hint for the rest of the day ([[sources/best-ict-turtle-soup-trading-strategy-that-works-insane-winrate]] @ 2:25, @ 3:13, @ 3:42).
- If the Asian low is swept first, the day is initially framed bearish; the model still wants to see the Asian high taken as well before taking the short setup ([[sources/best-ict-turtle-soup-trading-strategy-that-works-insane-winrate]] @ 3:58, @ 4:30).
- Valid entries come after acceptance back into the range, a market-structure shift, or a double-sweep retest on the 15-minute chart ([[sources/best-ict-turtle-soup-trading-strategy-that-works-insane-winrate]] @ 6:14, @ 6:46, @ 10:04, @ 10:28).
