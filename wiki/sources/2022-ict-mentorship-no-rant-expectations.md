# 2022 ICT Mentorship [No Rant] - Expectations

## Summary

- This clip is an orientation / expectation-setting piece for the 2022 ICT Mentorship.
- ICT says the mentorship is predominantly about futures index trading, with Nasdaq as the main study market and ES / YM as useful companions.
- He emphasizes comparing live executions against YouTube claims and understanding what price is likely to do before it does it.
- The mindset he wants is framework-first: learn the rules, learn how to practice, and stop chasing tiny handle counts as if that alone were the objective.
- The clip is less a specific setup lesson and more a reminder that a trading model must be built on a clear framework.
