# 2025 Lecture Series - Making Money With SMC Concepts

## Summary

- This lecture series is framed as a simple live-trading model for a new student: keep live risk small, settle into one market, and let a handful of repeatable ICT ideas do the work.
- ICT names five central tenets: live-risk sizing, time-based delivery / macros, liquidity pools, price inefficiencies, and simple entry/target logic.
- The session treats the regular-trading-hours opening gap as a primary frame of reference, then uses quadrants, consequent encroachment, and standard deviations to judge where price is likely to deliver.
- The opening range is the first 30 minutes of trading (9:30–10:00 ET) and the PM session repeats the same pattern inside 1:30–2:00 ET.
- The lecture strongly emphasizes journaling: record the times, PD arrays, macro windows, opening-range-gap behavior, and completed runs so the recurring signatures can be studied later.
- It is a clean bridge source between the opening-range gap, first-presented-FVG, lunch macro, and the price-delivery-continuum workflow.
