# How to Trade This EASY 2025 Model - Chermane's Model - NQ Trade Breakdown - ICT CONCEPTS

## Summary

- The trade breakdown uses midnight open and 8:30 open as the main opening-price checklist.
- If price is above both, the model treats the market as deep premium and favors shorts.
- The entry is framed as an IFVG / consequent-encroachment failure once buy-side liquidity has been taken and breakout traders are trapped.
- The model keeps the execution simple: enter on the reversal logic, take a partial, and leave a runner back toward the midnight open.
- This source is a clean example of opening-price anchors combined with IFVG failure logic.
