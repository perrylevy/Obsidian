# ICT 2026 Smart Money Concepts Lecture | January 13, 2026

## Summary

- ICT opens by saying he is going to teach time distortion: when a market is staying inside a range and not really trying to go anywhere, the market is effectively waiting for a higher-time-frame fulfillment, so the cure is to step up to the larger chart and let the larger objective explain the compression.
- On CPI morning, he warns that the 8:30 release can rip positions apart and explicitly recommends standing down unless the trader already knows how to handle that kind of volatility.
- The lecture extends first-presented-FVG logic into the premarket lead-in: the 7:00–9:30 window is treated as the build-up to the regular open, and the 9:30 print becomes the handoff into the 30-minute opening-range probe.
- ICT also repeats the FVG stop rule and the liquidity logic around the open: candle-one defines the stop, the open can probe buy-side and sell-side liquidity, and the real move may only become obvious after the opening range resolves.
- The 10:00–11:00 block is framed as the first profit-taking / offboard window after the open, which makes the source especially relevant to the user’s 9:30–11:00 execution window.
