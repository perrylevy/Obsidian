# Top Trading Psychologist Reveals the Fastest Way to End Self-Sabotage

This clip frames self-sabotage as a coping response rather than a character flaw: the trader is usually trying to escape an uncomfortable state such as anxiety after a loss or drawdown.

The practical rule is to look for the trigger, name the state, and ask what hidden benefit the behavior is providing in the moment. Once that motive is visible, the trader can replace the old response with a different coping system instead of beating themselves up and repeating the loop.

A second reusable rule is that overtrading and overrisking usually show up when the trader wants to get back to break-even or escape the discomfort of being in the negative. The fix is not shame; it is awareness, accountability, and a pre-planned alternative routine.
