# 2025 Lecture Series - ICT Gauntlet & NQ AM Session May 07, 2025 Review

## Summary

- The session is framed as a pure liquidity-and-time-of-day read: ICT says the 7:00–9:30 ET window is the main pool map, and he explicitly argues you do not need NWOG/NDOG scaffolding to make the trade readable ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-ict-gauntlet-nq-am-session-may-07-2025-review]] @ 6:23, @ 7:40, @ 8:06, @ 9:07).
- The 9:30 open trades down through sellside liquidity first, then attempts to rebound toward the nearest opening buy-side objective; the first relative equal highs off the open are treated as the low-hanging-fruit target ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-ict-gauntlet-nq-am-session-may-07-2025-review]] @ 6:58, @ 7:24, @ 9:38, @ 11:30, @ 11:40).
- ICT uses a 15-second chart for execution refinement once the one-minute narrative is established; the stop goes just below the open raid low and the entry is taken as the rebound starts to validate ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-ict-gauntlet-nq-am-session-may-07-2025-review]] @ 10:48, @ 10:54, @ 11:40, @ 22:18, @ 22:52).
- The clip names a very specific internal FVG inside the bullish breaker run as the "gauntlet" and treats it as a particularly meaningful sellside inefficiency in the opening leg ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-ict-gauntlet-nq-am-session-may-07-2025-review]] @ 14:34, @ 15:10, @ 15:57, @ 17:11).
- The 9:50–10:10 ET macro is presented as the window where the morning structure becomes obvious on the 15-second chart; ICT highlights the 7-minute-after-10 candle and the upper-quadrant / volume-imbalance relationship as the signature check ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-ict-gauntlet-nq-am-session-may-07-2025-review]] @ 18:01, @ 18:31, @ 21:14).
- This clip is useful as a direct ICT example of a "liquidity first, macro second" AM session rather than a session that depends on a more elaborate opening-gap framework.
