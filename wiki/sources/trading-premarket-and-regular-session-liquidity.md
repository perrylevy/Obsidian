# Trading Premarket and Regular Session Liquidity

## Summary

- The lesson grades **premium and discount wicks** on higher-time-frame candles, then uses those quadrants as a map for how the premarket and regular session should be delivered.
- The premarket 7:00 a.m. session is treated as a live liquidity-building period: a buyside pool forms above equal highs, then 8:30 news provides the volatility injection that can flip the day back down into sell-side objectives.
- ICT emphasizes that the setup is not just about the premarket open; the regular session reuses the same logic with opening-range liquidity, inversion FVGs, and the first clean buy-side / sell-side references after 9:30.
- The 9:30 open produces a fake-out first, then the market drives into the inversion FVG and reclaims the prior bearish FVG, taking sell-side before it later rallies through the remaining buy-side pools.
- The afternoon pass uses the lunch macro and a post-10:00 sell-side reference to show that the same delivery logic continues after the open: time window first, liquidity pool second, array third.
- The clip also walks through volume imbalance, measuring-gap, and an institutional-orderflow-entry-drill style entry inside the bearish FVG, showing how a session can be traded mechanically once the level map is known.
