# ICT 2026 Market Commentary | March 25, 2026

## Summary

- ICT frames the day as a low-confidence, high-resistance environment: both directions can be justified, so the burden of proof is on price before any trade idea is worth risk.
- For ES/MNQ, the 7:00–9:30 ET pre-open is treated as the liquidity map; the 9:30 open, opening-range-gap, and first presented FVG define the session once regular trading hours begin.
- In a rangebound / conflicted tape, ICT prefers nearby inefficiencies just above the old lows or below the old highs instead of forcing a breakout narrative.
- Bodies are the confirmation tool. Wicks can probe the level, but the body close determines whether the opening gap / IFVG / reclaimed FVG logic is actually valid.
- The practical takeaway is regime control: when the opening tape is messy, lower leverage and wait for the market to validate the morning frame before committing.
