# How To Fix A Losing Trade NQ Live Trade Walkthrough ICT SMART MONEY CONCEPTS

## Summary

- The trader starts the day with a loss, then uses smaller risk and stricter scaling to recover without forcing the market to give back the whole morning.
- The trade is framed around a daily bullish order block, relative equal highs, a new-day opening gap, and an inversion fair value gap whose consequent encroachment becomes the pyramid-entry zone.
- Because the morning is messy and the market is spending too much time inside a balanced price range, the trader leans on partials, reduced risk, and patience rather than trying to force a full-size continuation.
- The clip is a practical lesson in damage control: one good trade, sized and managed properly, can erase a loss if the trader respects delivery speed and refuses to overstay.
