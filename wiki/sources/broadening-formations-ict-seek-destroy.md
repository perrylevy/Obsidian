# Broadening Formations - ICT Seek & Destroy

## Summary

- A broadening formation is an expanding range: price keeps taking one side of the range, then the other, so the structure widens instead of compressing ([[sources/broadening-formations-ict-seek-destroy]] @ 0:36, @ 0:58).
- The practical drawing method is to connect the swept highs and lows, then extend those lines as new range extremes appear ([[sources/broadening-formations-ict-seek-destroy]] @ 1:21, @ 1:49, @ 2:14).
- TTrades uses broadening formations as edge-of-range points of interest and as targets, especially when paired with [[concepts/smt|SMT]] or [[models/power-of-three|PO3]] logic ([[sources/broadening-formations-ict-seek-destroy]] @ 5:25, @ 6:48, @ 7:07, @ 9:56).
- On higher timeframes the formation often behaves like an outside bar that has taken both sides of the range ([[sources/broadening-formations-ict-seek-destroy]] @ 8:39, @ 9:01).
