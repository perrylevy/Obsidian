# ICT Forex & Futures Market Review October 4, 2025

## Summary

- ICT frames forex as a comparatively low-opportunity, high-resistance environment in this review; the more important lesson is the reusable structure, not the specific pair.
- The review repeatedly grades wicks, volume imbalances, and suspension blocks with consequent encroachment rather than treating them as loose support/resistance.
- For NASDAQ futures, Thursday's opening-range gap is carried into Friday as a live frame: the gap high, low, upper quadrant, lower quadrant, and midpoint remain actionable until price resolves the range.
- The clip shows the same logic on a CFD chart by using the prior day's 4:14 p.m. close and the 9:30 a.m. open as the anchors that recreate the regular-trading-hours opening-range gap.
- Once the larger gap is in play, the first-presented FVG, inversion FVG, and suspension block all become part of the same delivery map.
