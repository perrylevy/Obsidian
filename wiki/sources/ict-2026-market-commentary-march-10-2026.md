# ICT 2026 Market Commentary | March 10, 2026

## Summary

- Uses the 9:30 open as a live opening-range-gap / premium-discount test rather than a static annotation.
- Frames the morning as a carry-forward exercise: prior-week opening-range-gap levels remain active even after the chart has moved away from them.
- Treats the 7-minute-after-10 candle as a sellside litmus test; if the sellside draw is still real, that candle stays unfilled, and if it fills, the narrative flips.
- Repeatedly emphasizes body acceptance over wick noise around the opening-range-gap and the prior-week ORG octant levels.
- Extends the same logic into the premarket/RTH boundary by distinguishing when premarket references can be used versus when 9:30 resets the narrative.
