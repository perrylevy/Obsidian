# 2022 ICT Mentorship Episode 10 - Implementing Economic Calendar Events With The Open

**Video**: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S9ORTYmXwdE) | **Duration**: 1:06:46 | **Channel**: The Inner Circle Trader | **Published**: February 17, 2022

## Summary

This episode combines **economic calendar awareness** with the **daily bias** and **Power of 3** frameworks. It covers how to use forexfactory.com for news impact assessment, the 8:30 AM ET news embargo as a key volatility trigger, the **opening range** concept for premium/discount array placement, and the **15-minute chart as the "bellwether"** for intraday trading. The episode also includes critical risk management guidance: minimum account sizes for NQ volatility, why discount broker margins are dangerous, and a 2-week paper trading demonstration.

### Core themes

1. **Economic calendar awareness** — use forexfactory.com; red events = high impact, orange = medium, yellow = low; 8:30 AM ET is the critical news embargo time.
2. **Daily bias determination** — the #1 most requested topic; study price action to identify whether price is drawing to old high (bullish) or old low (bearish).
3. **Opening range concept** — for bearish days, the range from open to high defines where premium arrays form; subtract this range from the open to find the optimal short entry zone.
4. **Power of 3 daily pattern** — bearish: open → rally (accumulation of shorts) → sell-off (manipulation) → close near low (distribution); bullish reverses the sequence.
5. **15-minute chart as bellwether** — if forced to trade only one timeframe, ICT chooses 15-minute; it provides panoramic view for swing, day, and scalp trading.
6. **Timeframe drilldown for FVG** — strip down from 5m → 4m → 3m → 2m → 1m; stop at first timeframe where FVG is found.
7. **Risk management** — minimum $15,000 for current NQ volatility; exchange margin (~$22,000) is the professional standard; discount broker margins are dangerous.
8. **Paper trading discipline** — 2-week paper account demonstration showing consistent execution before going live.

## Key sections

### Economic calendar fundamentals

**Why it matters**:
- Essential for all traders, especially those considering live trading.
- Prevents unexpected volatility surprises.
- Most economic events impact stock indices significantly.

**Recommended resource**:
- **forexfactory.com** — ICT's preferred economic calendar.
- Color coding system:
  - **Yellow** = Low impact events
  - **Orange** = Medium impact events
  - **Red** = High impact news drivers

**Critical time reference: 8:30 AM ET**:
- News embargo lifts at this time.
- Multiple news events enter the marketplace simultaneously.
- Some reports used as "smokescreen" catalysts; others create predictable price buildup/breakdown patterns.

### Daily bias & Power Three framework

**The core teaching**:
Daily bias determination is the **#1 most requested question** ICT receives from mentorship students.

> "I'm never going to be able to do it any easier than this... you just have to study price action and you'll see it for yourself after a while"

**Power of 3 components: Accumulation → Manipulation → Distribution**:

**Bearish daily pattern**:
1. **Open** — starting price.
2. **Rally** (accumulation of shorts) — price moves above opening.
3. **Sell-off** (manipulation) — initial rally is a "sucker play."
4. **Close near low** (distribution) — smart money exits short positions.

**Bullish daily pattern** (reversed):
- Open near low → Rally → Close near high.
- Accumulation of longs at the low.
- Distribution of longs near the close.

### Opening range concept

**Definition**: The range from the opening price to the high of the day.

**Application for bearish days**:
- Take the range (High - Open).
- Subtract that range from the opening price.
- This creates the **Opening Range** — where optimal short entries form.
- All premium arrays reside within this range.

> "Every single one of them will reside and form in the range between the open and its high"

**Key insight on candle patterns**:
- Not every candle in a sustained move will follow the pattern.
- **Bearish bias**: Expect mostly down-close candles (not all).
- **Bullish bias**: Expect mostly up-close candles (not all).
- One counter-trend candle doesn't invalidate the bias.

### Identifying daily bias: swing structures

**Swing low definition**:
- Three candles: lower low in middle, higher lows on both sides.
- **Do NOT need Williams Fractals** (too many candles, miss the move).

**Bias determination process**:
1. Identify swing low or swing high.
2. Assess proper context.
3. Hunt for the setup.
4. Apply Power Three pattern expectations.

**Fair value gap (FVG)**:
- Imbalance between two candles.
- Market tends to rebalance these gaps.
- Can be used as retracement targets.

### Intraday trading framework

**Optimal timeframe: 15-minute chart**:
> "If I was held to a decision of what time frame would you be forced to trade with if you had to pick just one... the 15-minute time frame"

**Why 15-minute**:
- Can swing trade, short-term trade, day trade, or scalp.
- Provides full panoramic view of price action.
- Serves as "bell weather chart" for all intraday trading.

**Top-down analysis approach**:
When seeking fair value gaps, strip down timeframes in this order:
1. 5-minute chart
2. 4-minute chart (if no FVG on 5-min)
3. 3-minute chart (if no FVG on 4-min)
4. 2-minute chart (if no FVG on 3-min)
5. 1-minute chart (if no FVG on 2-min)

**Rule**: Stop at first timeframe where FVG is found; don't go lower.

**Paint roller analogy**:
- Large candles with extended ranges = inefficient pricing.
- Market returns to "paint" the range evenly.
- Creates rebalancing opportunities.

### Risk management & account requirements

**Minimum account size**:
- **Absolute minimum**: $10,000 (not recommended).
- **Fair recommendation**: $15,000+ for current NASDAQ volatility.
- **Professional standard**: ~$22,000 (exchange initial margin requirement).

**Why discount broker margins are dangerous**:

**Real-world example**:
- NASDAQ moved 100 points in a single 1-minute candle (recently observed).
- 8 mini contracts × 50-point slippage = $8,000 loss in one trade.
- Possible to lose entire account in single unexpected move.

> "If you're looking at that and you're trying to hold let's say 16 minis or 8 minis and it moves $2,000 against you in one minute folks your stop would not have been respected"

**Margin increase = warning signal**:
- Exchange raising margins indicates **massive moves coming**.
- Current NASDAQ mini contract margin: ~$22,000.
- Respect the volatility; don't fight it.
