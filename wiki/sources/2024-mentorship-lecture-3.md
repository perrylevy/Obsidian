# ICT 2024 Mentorship — Lecture #3 (August 7, 2024)

Lecture 3 shifts the 2024 mentorship from general framing into a more explicit time + price execution map. ICT uses the 6:00 p.m. NY restart, the first 7:00–9:00 AM checkpoints, and the cluster of new-day / new-week opening gaps as the main liquidity scaffold for bias.

## Summary

- The **new day opening gap** is the active session reference for the next trading day, not a one-time gap-fill curiosity. ICT says it can stay meaningful for multiple trading days and should be logged with its high, low, and midpoint.
- If the calendar is light on medium/high-impact news, the morning often behaves like a **rangy false-breakout / pullback environment**, so the first move is not automatically the trade.
- The **new day opening gap cluster** becomes a larger draw on liquidity when several gaps sit above or below current price. ICT treats that clustering as a “black hole” that can dominate the direction of the day.
- **Consequent encroachment** is the midpoint of the gap. In this lecture it is the practical divider between the upper and lower quadrants of the NDOG, especially when the gap is small enough to eyeball.
- The morning liquidity map starts after **7:00 NY**. If relative equal highs appear after 7:00 while the NDOG sits below, the more likely sequence is a liquidity sweep up first and then a move toward the gap / imbalance.
- Price action is framed as **engineering liquidity**, not retail buying/selling pressure. The lecture repeatedly pushes the idea that the algorithm is referencing time-stamped inefficiencies.

## Practical takeaways

- Mark the 6:00 p.m. opening print and maintain only the most relevant recent NDOGs / NWOGs.
- Track the midpoint / CE of each gap and treat it as a decision line.
- Do not chase the first liquidity-looking move at 7:00, 8:00, or 9:00; wait for the gap draw and the session's liquidity sweep behavior to line up.
- If several NDOGs are clustered in one direction, expect price to gravitate there even before the morning structure fully forms.
