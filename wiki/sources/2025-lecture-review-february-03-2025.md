# 2025 Lecture Review February 03, 2025

## Summary

- This Monday review frames the morning as a gap-confluence trade rather than a generic scalp: the new week opening gap and the regular-trading-hours opening range gap are overlaid into one working scaffold.
- The new week opening gap is defined off Friday settlement to the Sunday evening open, and the lecture grades that gap with Fibonacci quadrants plus consequent encroachment.
- The opening range gap is the Friday settlement to 9:30 a.m. RTH gap, and it is graded the same way; the practical insight is that the lower CE / lower quadrant on one gap can line up with the upper quadrant / CE on the other.
- The clip explicitly treats the NWOG and ORG overlap as a confluence zone, especially when Sunday opens below Friday settlement and the week begins in a discounted state.
- Execution follows a simple path: early downside into sell-side liquidity, reclaim of the first 1-minute FVG around the 9:31 candle, flip into an inversion FVG, entry near the midpoint / consequent encroachment, and target back toward the NWOG CE.
- The lecture also emphasizes a breakaway-gap rule: once price leaves the inversion FVG cleanly, it should not revisit it; that supports stop placement after confirmation.
- Because volatility is elevated, the lecture recommends reducing size / leverage instead of forcing full-risk execution.
