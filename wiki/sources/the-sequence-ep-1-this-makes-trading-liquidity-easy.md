# The Sequence Ep.1: This Makes Trading Liquidity Easy

## Summary

- The sequence is a top-down narrative tool for month, week, day, and session.
- It is explicitly framed as accumulation, manipulation, and distribution.
- The method starts from a monthly or weekly target / key level, then uses a lower timeframe stop run, candle closure, and FVG / OB continuation entry to position inside distribution.
- The practical value is that you do not need to guess the exact turning point; you follow the sequence and wait for the market to move through the higher-time-frame narrative.
- SMT and prior session highs/lows add probability, and the final entry is usually on the lower timeframe after the higher-time-frame closure has already confirmed the bias.
