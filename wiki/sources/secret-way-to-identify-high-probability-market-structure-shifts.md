# Secret Way To Identify High Probability Market Structure Shifts..

## Summary

- The video separates a simple liquidity sweep from a true market structure shift.
- A sweep alone is not enough; the useful confirmation comes when price sweeps liquidity, reacts, then breaks structure after filling an FVG.
- The highest-probability version is the one where a prior higher low / lower high fails, price taps back into fair value, and then a break of structure confirms the reversal.
- The practical entry can be an FVG or order block after the confirming break, not the sweep itself.

## Doctrine takeaway

The clip's real rule is that a high-probability MSS has a three-part shape:

1. sweep a meaningful liquidity level,
2. get a reaction that trades back into a fair value gap or similar imbalance,
3. break structure in the new direction.

That makes the MSS a confirmation event, while the sweep is only the setup for confirmation.
