# Never Miss Another Trade

The clip frames a single candle as containing three trade opportunities: the wick, the continuation, and the cap at the end of the range.

The practical lesson is fractal. The same sequence appears on weekly and daily candles, with the low being engineered first, the expansion trade in the middle, and the final small scalp at the opposing premium / discount edge.

The clip also emphasizes that the best version of the model is the one the trader sees most clearly and can repeat without forcing it. On the higher time frame, an SMT setup can strengthen the draw, but the core idea is still the candle's open-low-high-close anatomy.
