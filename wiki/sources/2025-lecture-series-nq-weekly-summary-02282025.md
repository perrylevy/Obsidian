# 2025 Lecture Series - NQ Weekly Summary 02/28/2025

## Summary

- The weekly summary argues that NQ was held in a large holding pattern until one side of the range was taken, after which volatility expanded toward the next draw (@ 2:07, @ 3:39, @ 5:28).
- The daily gap / volume-imbalance hybrid is treated as a balanced-price-range style structure that can be repriced and then left behind once buy-side is taken, with consequent encroachment marking the efficient midpoint boundary (@ 3:39, @ 5:52, @ 6:30).
- Once the buy-side objective was satisfied, the market was expected to work lower into the next inefficiencies and sell-side pools, with the week unfolding as a sequence of inside-day compression and release (@ 7:37, @ 8:56, @ 9:57, @ 10:51).
- The clip repeatedly stresses journaling and hand-drawn notes as the way to track the price-delivery continuum from weekly chart to intraday execution, especially when a weekly holding pattern later resolves into inversion behavior (@ 11:08, @ 12:06, @ 27:28, @ 35:32, @ 43:18).
- This source is useful as a weekly framing layer: it ties the higher-time-frame draw, balanced-price-range style repricing, CE, and the later inversion of the same structure into one repeatable weekly narrative.
