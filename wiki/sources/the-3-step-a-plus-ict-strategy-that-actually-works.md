# The 3-Step A+ ICT Strategy (that actually works)

This clip frames trade selection as a simple three-part problem: pick the obvious target, understand the liquidity structure inside the range, and only then wait for a clean timing / entry condition.

The strongest practical lesson is that external range liquidity is the preferred target. If the market is too range-bound, too ambiguous, or too evenly balanced to make the target obvious, the correct choice is often to stand aside rather than force a counter-trend attempt into internal liquidity.

The clip also adds a calendar discipline layer: major events like NFP, FOMC, and CPI can make the weekly path noisy or deceptive, so the trader should avoid pressing hard ahead of those releases unless the setup is unusually clear.
