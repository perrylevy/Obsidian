# ICT 2026 Smart Money Concepts Lecture | February 11, 2026

## Summary

- ICT treats the 9:30 open as the start of the morning work, but the practical opening-range window remains the first 30 minutes and the first valid 1-minute FVG is still the session's working array.
- The clip tightens the default trigger window to 9:31–10:00 ET for the first presented FVG, while acknowledging that the 9:30 candle can matter in narrow edge cases if it genuinely creates a valid FVG.
- On the NFP Wednesday open, ICT watches the opening-range gap, half-gap / consequent-encroachment behavior, and early sellside sweeps as one morning scaffold rather than separate ideas.
- When the market is compressing in high-resistance liquidity, the important question is whether it can close through consequent encroachment; failed reclaim behavior leaves a rejection block and signals weakness.
- The clip also frames a fast return to a nearby reference as an immediate rebalance and uses body-vs-wick behavior to judge whether the downside thesis is still intact.
