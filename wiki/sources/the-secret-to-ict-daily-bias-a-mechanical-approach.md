# The Secret To ICT Daily Bias: A Mechanical Approach

## Summary

- The lesson builds higher-timeframe bias by segmenting price into quarters and reading the previous quarter's market profile before deciding what the next quarter is likely to do.
- The core cycle is mechanical: accumulation tends to precede expansion, expansion can become retracement, and the prior quarters' behavior helps forecast which profile is likely next.
- The model repeatedly uses premium / discount, quarterly opens, FVGs, SMT, and liquidity lookbacks to decide whether the next quarter should bias bullish or bearish.
- For execution, the clip uses daily / weekly / 4H / 1H / 5m nesting and 20/40/60-day lookbacks to identify likely draw-on-liquidity targets and continuation zones.
- The source is less about a new trigger and more about the top-down daily bias workflow that tells you which side of the market should be favored before any lower-timeframe entry is considered.
- This is a strong derivative teacher summary for mechanical bias construction, especially when the user wants a repeatable top-down filter rather than discretionary chart reading.
