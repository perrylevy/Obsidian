# ICT - The First Presented FVG In The Opening Range

## Summary

- The clip restates the real opening range as the first 30-minute RTH window from 9:30 to 10:00 a.m. New York time.
- The first fair-value-gap that forms inside that window is the working array, but only after the opening sequence has produced the right displacement and liquidity context.
- The opening-range gap and the first presented FVG are treated as one operational frame: the gap gives the opening scaffold, the FVG gives the executable location, and consequent encroachment gives the reaction line.
- When buy-side has already been taken and the gap forms after displacement, the imbalance can be read as a bearish suspension-block-style opening and later as an inversion FVG.
- The clip is a clean bridge between opening-range structure and first-presented-FVG execution.
