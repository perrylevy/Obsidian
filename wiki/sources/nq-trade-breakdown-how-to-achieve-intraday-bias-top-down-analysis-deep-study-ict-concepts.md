# NQ Trade Breakdown - How to Achieve Intraday Bias - Top-Down Analysis Deep Study - ICT CONCEPTS

## Summary

- The clip builds weekly and intraday bias from a daily inversion fair value gap and then drills down to a 15-minute entry.
- Midnight open and 8:30 open are used together as a deep premium / discount filter for deciding whether shorts or longs are favored.
- The model wants support in the upper half of the daily IFVG when bullish, and it avoids trading into the lower half of that structure.
- A good long setup is a clean FVG in the upper half of the IFVG with sell-side already swept and no immediate willingness to trade to midpoint.
- This source is an especially clear example of opening-price anchors being used as a bias filter, not just as a target.
