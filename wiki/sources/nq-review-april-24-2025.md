# 2025 Lecture Series - NQ Review April 24, 2025

## Summary

- The market dipped into a gap below the previous day's low, then worked higher into relative equal highs and a 60-minute fair value gap (@ 0:45, @ 0:58, @ 1:14).
- That 60-minute FVG was treated as an inversion fair value gap after price traded through it, pushing toward minor buyside liquidity (@ 1:41).
- A 15-second order block long was used during the pre-session, with the entry taken close to consequent encroachment (@ 2:35, @ 2:55).
- The review emphasizes that pre-open context matters: if you were not already positioned before the bell, there was little left to do (@ 3:44).
- Daily/HTF imbalance, equal highs, and inversion behavior are the key framing tools.
- This review is best read as an example of how the same core doctrines can be arranged around pre-open context and a fast pre-session entry, not as a unique standalone model.
