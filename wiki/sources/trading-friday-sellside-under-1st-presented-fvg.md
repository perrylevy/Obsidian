# Trading Friday Sellside Under 1st Presented FVG

## Summary

- The clip is a Friday short built from a reclaimed FVG / first-presented FVG context with nearby sell-side liquidity as the target.
- ICT emphasizes that the move should show decisive lower delivery: if the bodies stay inside the gap or spend too much time around the quadrant, the read is weak.
- The setup is conservative because Friday at the end of a bullish week tends to favor low-hanging sell-side objectives rather than heroic downside targets.
- The first-presented FVG is used as the working array; if it behaves like an immediate rebalance, the market should quickly consume the discount wick and press toward the next liquidity pool.
- The clip also shows that the best trade management is simple: keep the stop where it belongs until the lower quadrant is truly broken, then let the lower delivery do the work.
- This source is a clean Friday sellside execution example and a good companion to the broader first-presented-FVG and liquidity pages.
