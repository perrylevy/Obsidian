# How to Stop Giving It All Back

## Summary

- The clip argues that "giving it all back" is usually not a market problem; it is an emotional state problem that shows up after a period of good performance.
- It identifies three common sabotage pathways: euphoria / overconfidence, scarcity thinking, and the merger of net worth with self-worth.
- The practical answer is emotional intelligence: notice the state, interrupt the loop, and stop letting a good session turn into a loss spiral.
- The lesson is less about a specific setup and more about the trader's relationship to winning, money, and identity.
