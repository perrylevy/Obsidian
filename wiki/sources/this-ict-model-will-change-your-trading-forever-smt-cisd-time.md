# This ICT Model Will Change Your Trading Forever (SMT + CISD + Time)

## Summary

- The model is an open-based SMT framework: wait until after 9:30, then look for one asset to take the high while the other fails, and only trade the asset that fits the higher-time-frame narrative.
- Pre-open SMTs are explicitly treated as unreliable; the real confirmation is the regular-session version after the market is officially open.
- The key trigger is a change in the state of delivery: close below the candle that made the high, then use the next candle / retest / breaker / IFVG as the execution path.
- The trader treats the breaker entry as the highest-probability but lowest-RR version of the sequence, while SMT, IFVG, and CSD are earlier or more aggressive variants.
- The lesson also emphasizes filtering by daily bias and news timing: if news is coming at 10:00, wait; if the daily is bearish, favor the bearish leg and avoid the premarket fakeout.
- This source is useful as a compact derivative model for opening-session execution with SMT + CSD confirmation.
