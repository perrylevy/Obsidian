# AM Silver Bullet Strategy on NQ

## Summary

- The AM Silver Bullet is a time-based reversal model restricted to the 10:00–11:00 a.m. New York window.
- It uses the 9:00 a.m. hourly candle as the session frame: mark the high and low once 10:00 hits, then only consider trades after one side of that range is taken.
- Entries require confirmation such as displacement back into the range, breaker blocks, order blocks, or fair value gaps.
- If price does not sweep the 9:00 a.m. range during the window, no trade is taken.
