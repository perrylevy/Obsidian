# ICT 2026 Market Commentary | March 08, 2026

## Summary

- ICT frames the 9:30 a.m. New York open as the live regular-trading-hours anchor and repeatedly separates that RTH structure from the overnight electronic session.
- The opening-range gap is read in context: a 9:30 open above the prior settlement is a premium gap, while the same opening-range framework can also be carried forward as a discount opening-range gap on the prior-day reference.
- The first valid 1-minute FVG inside the opening window becomes the morning's working array; later FVGs are secondary unless the first one is validated, reclaimed, or broken.
- Body behavior matters more than wick noise. ICT repeatedly points to bodies respecting the opening-range gap and the higher-order level as the signal that the move is still following the narrative.
- The clip is a strong live example of the 9:30–11:00 ET execution window because it combines opening-range-gap logic, first-presented-FVG selection, and RTH-vs-ETH separation in one morning review.
