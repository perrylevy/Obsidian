# 2025 Lecture Series - SMC New York NWOG With Forex 01/27/2025

## Summary

- ICT frames GBP/USD as a live New Week Opening Gap example: Friday close to Sunday 5:00 p.m. ET open becomes the weekly reference gap, not just a visual artifact.
- The setup is traded inside the New York session, roughly 7:00–10:00 NY local time, with the 10:00 Silver Bullet window as the clean execution pocket.
- Price rallies into the NWOG, then a 5-minute inversion FVG is read on a 1-minute chart; body acceptance in the lower half / around CE is the key confirmation.
- The source repeatedly distinguishes wick probes from body acceptance: wicks can raid above the gap, but the bodies need to stay in the lower half or near consequent encroachment for the IFVG to remain valid.
- The remaining portion of the NWOG is treated as the live imbalance, while the already-worked half is the balanced price range.
- Once the IFVG proves itself, the move resolves lower and the market delivers into the next liquidity draw.
- This clip is best treated as a direct Forex confirmation of the NWOG + IFVG + CE + Silver Bullet stack, not as a new standalone model.
