# 2025 Lecture Series - NQ Review CPI No Trade May 13, 2025

## Summary

- The CPI open is treated as a news-driven day where the early move must be filtered rather than blindly traded.
- The initial thesis is to let CPI take out sellside / establish the opening impulse, then use the first presented FVG and the 10:00 NY macro as the first meaningful post-news entry frame.
- A daily fair value gap on the higher time frame becomes the next draw-on-liquidity objective once the open has delivered the CPI impulse.
- The afternoon follow-through repeats the same logic: the 3:15–3:45 macro can use an inversion FVG / volume-imbalance combination to target the sellside liquidity pool and the short-term low.
- The source is explicit that the first presented FVG, breakaway gap, and measuring gap sequence are part of the same delivery chain.
- Overall, this is a no-trade-before-news lesson: wait for the release, let the tape show its hand, then trade the post-news structure rather than the pre-news noise.
