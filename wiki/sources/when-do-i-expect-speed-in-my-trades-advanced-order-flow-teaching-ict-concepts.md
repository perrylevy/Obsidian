# When Do I Expect SPEED in My Trades? Advanced Order Flow Teaching ICT CONCEPTS

## Summary

- The core rule is not "enter faster"; it is to expect immediate directional speed once the market has already established your draw and then retraces into the relevant PD array.
- If there are liquidity pools or intermediate highs/lows before the main target, the market should rip through them instead of stalling or reversing early.
- A short-term retracement into a fair value gap, order block, or new-day opening gap should still lead to speed through the intermediate swing if the larger draw is intact.
- A breakaway gap should stay open. If the tape trades into it and fails to respond immediately, the gap is no longer behaving like launch fuel.
- The same logic applies on the short side in the final-hour examples: once sellside is the focus, the tape should accelerate through the target-side low rather than hover around it.
