## Summary

- The weekly frame is bullish, with the weekly wick consequent encroachment, 21,941.75 draw, and 22,103 continuation level acting as the big-picture roadmap (@ 0:54, @ 1:26, @ 2:46).
- The opening range is treated as the 9:30 open through the 9:31 first presented FVG, and the PM session gets its own first presented FVG later in the day (@ 8:29, @ 9:42, @ 12:46, @ 13:16, @ 17:15).
- The clip emphasizes that RTH and ETH need to be blended rather than isolated, with the new day opening gap and the new week opening gap both used as live reference levels (@ 12:01, @ 14:47, @ 18:14, @ 30:40).
