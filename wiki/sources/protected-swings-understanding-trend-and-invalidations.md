# Protected Swings – Understanding Trend and Invalidations

This clip defines a protected swing as the high or low that should hold if the trend is going to continue.

The important refinement is the candle-cluster rule: once price closes through the series of candles that formed the low or high, that swing becomes protected. The clip also shows the same idea through SMT, where the correlated asset can help confirm that the swing should now hold.

In practice this is an invalidation map. Once the protected swing is established, you can use it to decide whether the trend remains intact and whether a continuation entry still makes sense.
