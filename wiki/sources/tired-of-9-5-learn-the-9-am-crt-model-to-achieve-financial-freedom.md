# Tired of 9-5? Learn the 9 AM CRT Model to Achieve Financial Freedom | Trading strategy

## Summary

- The clip expands the 9 AM CRT model into a full morning workflow: identify draw on liquidity, mark the 8:00 a.m. CRT reference, then wait for the 15-minute purge and confirmation.
- It explicitly separates the 30-minute opening range from the broader first-hour dealing range and uses the hour's midpoint as a balancing reference.
- The entry logic is built on 15-minute CRT behavior, a sweep of the 8:00 range, and then a lower-timeframe confirmation such as MSS / CISD before using the order block.
- It reinforces the idea that CRT is a session execution model, not just a candle-reading trick.
