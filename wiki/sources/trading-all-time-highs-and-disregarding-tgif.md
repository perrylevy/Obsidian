# Trading All Time Highs and Disregarding TGIF

## Summary

- The main rule is that TGIF logic is unreliable at all-time highs: a bullish weekly backdrop can keep extending, so Friday does not automatically mean reversal.
- ICT grades the premium wick from Thursday, the first-presented FVG, reclaimed bearish FVG behavior, and nearby sell-side liquidity to decide where the market is most likely to react.
- The clip repeatedly stresses that at ATHs you should not try to pick the top; instead, use the wicks, liquidity pools, and intraday FVGs to locate low-hanging objectives.
- The afternoon rally shows how a bullish market can keep delivering even after reaching obvious objectives, which is why target selection has to be conservative when fading a primary uptrend.
- The dollar and cable side comments reinforce the same idea: daily premium/discount, inversion FVG behavior, and session timing matter more than the calendar label "Friday."
- This is a direct Friday/ATH framing clip and should be preserved as a practical caution against overusing TGIF as a reversal heuristic.
