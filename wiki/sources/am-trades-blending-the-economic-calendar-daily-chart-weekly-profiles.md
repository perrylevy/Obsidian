# AM Trades | Blending The Economic Calendar, Daily Chart, & Weekly Profiles

## Summary

- The clip builds a weekly trade narrative from three filters: economic-calendar risk, the daily chart's current phase of price delivery, and the weekly profile that says whether the week is likely to trend, reverse, or stay congested.
- Monday is treated as low-quality / sacrificial data; the real decision points come from Tuesday through Friday after the calendar tells you which days may expand.
- High-impact events like CPI, FOMC press conference, and NFP matter because they can reshape the weekly range, while medium-impact events are treated as caution windows inside the daily range.
- The main lesson is that the trader should not consider entries until the weekly narrative is already framed.
