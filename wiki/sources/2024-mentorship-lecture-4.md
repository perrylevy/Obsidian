# ICT 2024 Mentorship — Lecture #4 (August 8, 2024)

Lecture 4 turns the August mentorship into a more explicit 8:30 NY execution drill. ICT uses the morning employment-data window, the stacked NDOG/NWOG levels, and the 50% midpoint / quadrant logic to show how price can be expected to spool toward the more loaded side of the chart even before the 9:30 open.

## Summary

- The **8:30 a.m. NY print** matters even when the news is not a major CPI/NFP release. ICT says the algorithm still starts to spool at 8:30, and a medium/high-impact release can simply amplify the move.
- He treats the market as sitting between reference levels until the 8:30 event resolves, with the important question being which side has the stacked inefficiencies above or below.
- The **new day opening gap** and **new week opening gap** remain the primary directional filters. The algorithm is expected to gravitate toward the cluster side, not because of buy/sell pressure, but because those are the reference points.
- **Consequent encroachment** again acts as the midpoint, while larger gaps justify quadrant tracking and smaller gaps can be eyeballed.
- A bullish run can still be framed with a **breaker + inversion FVG** combination, but the source warns against treating a bare gap fill as a clean buy signal.
- The lecture repeatedly argues for a cleaner chart and more notebook-driven annotation, so the trader watches price instead of worshipping lines.

## Practical takeaways

- Treat 8:30 NY as a real timing checkpoint, even without a big news catalyst.
- Look for stacked NDOG/NWOGs on one side of price; that side becomes the more likely draw.
- Use CE and quadrants only when the gap is large enough to justify them.
- Don’t confuse a gap revisit with a valid long by default; wait for the breaker / IFVG / displacement context.
