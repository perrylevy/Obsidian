# Your Results Match Your Identity, Not Your Strategy

This clip argues that results are constrained less by the chosen strategy and more by the trader's identity, emotional defaults, and tolerance for disciplined repetition.

The core frame is the thermostat principle: if your internal set point is still fear, impatience, or emotional reactivity, profits tend to snap back toward that older identity. Strategy can create opportunity, but identity decides whether you can keep the gains.

The practical takeaway is to normalize boring discipline, stop chasing quick-money validation, and repeatedly act like the trader you are trying to become until the behavior becomes automatic.
