# 2025 Lecture Series - SMC Midnight Opening Range

## Summary

- The lecture treats the midnight opening range as the first 30 minutes from 00:00 to 00:30 New York time and says the opening price at midnight is a separate anchor from the range itself.
- The range high, range low, and midpoint / standard-deviation projections are used as a rough map for where the session may pivot, especially during London and the New York morning.
- ICT emphasizes that the setup works in futures and Forex and that the chart must be keyed to New York local time if the algorithmic reference is going to be meaningful.
- The lecture uses the midnight opening range as part of a broader daily-high / daily-low workflow: measure the range, project it, and then look for delivery into the projected levels, liquidity pools, and first displacement.
- It also reinforces the idea that the range gives the trader a structured boundary before the main New York session begins rather than a perfect prediction.
