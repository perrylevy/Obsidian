# Why You're Failing with ICT Concepts

The clip's main point is that most traders fail not because they lack ICT concepts, but because they try to trade too many PD arrays at once.

The practical fix is to pick one array that is easiest to see, make the entry and stop rules repeatable, and use highs and lows or liquidity as the context around that chosen array. In the example, CISD / change in state of delivery is the preferred trigger, with order blocks and opposing candles as the execution frame.

The clip also frames turtle soup, inversion, breaker blocks, and fair value gaps as different confirmation rungs in the same reversal family. The point is not to use all of them simultaneously. Use the one that gives you the clearest, most repeatable data.
