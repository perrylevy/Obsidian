# ICT Opening Range Theory - 1st Presented FVG Logic

## Summary

- The lecture re-centers the opening range on the 30-minute window from 1:30 to 2:00 a.m. ET for London and treats the first 30 minutes as the key session frame.
- The first valid FVG inside that opening range is the executable array, but only after displacement and liquidity context make it meaningful.
- When the gap forms after buy-side has already been taken, it can be classified as a bearish suspension-block-style imbalance and then as an inversion FVG once price closes through it.
- The opening-range gap and the first presented FVG are blended into one operational frame: the gap gives bias, the FVG gives entry, and consequent encroachment gives the reaction line.
- The clip is a clean doctrinal bridge between opening-range structure and first-presented-FVG execution.
