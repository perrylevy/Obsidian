# 2025 Lecture Series - SMC Forex Review 02/04/2025

## Summary

- The dollar index weekly chart is read through wick consequent encroachment, inversion FVG logic, and a daily bearish propulsion block that acts as the active draw.
- The review keeps stressing that wicks may overshoot, but bodies must tell the real story; if bodies reclaim the wrong half, the inversion thesis weakens.
