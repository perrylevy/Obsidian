# Why Most Traders Lack Consistency (And How You Can Be Different)

This clip argues that consistency comes from process, patience, and system fidelity — not from hunting more trades.

The practical rule is to wait for the market to offer your setup, trade only when your system aligns, and treat no-trade days as part of the job. If the market is not offering the conditions you expect, doing nothing is a valid and often superior decision.

The clip also emphasizes that break-even traders usually need small process fixes, not a wholesale strategy rewrite. Journal the outlier days, remove recurring weak conditions, and stay with one system long enough to build real experience and intuition.
