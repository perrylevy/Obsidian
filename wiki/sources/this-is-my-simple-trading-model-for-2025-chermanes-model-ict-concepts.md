# This is My SIMPLE Trading Model for 2025 - Chermane's Model - ICT CONCEPTS

This clip describes a simple New York model: wait for the opening range to play out, then only take shorts above the midnight open and longs below it.

The useful part is the time filter. The model is explicit about waiting until after 10:00 a.m. New York time and using the midnight opening price as the directional filter, which makes the setup easier to frame mechanically.

It is a clean derivative example of an opening-price / time-window model and belongs in the model stack as a simple execution family rather than as a broad doctrine page.
