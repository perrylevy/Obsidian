# From Vision To Execution

This lecture frames trading as a two-step process: first cast forth a vision of where price is likely to draw, then wait for the execution window where time and price meet that draw.

A practical rule comes out of the turtle-soup example: when the market sweeps a short-term high or low and then displaces, the first presented FVG after that sweep becomes the standout array. ICT says to project that level forward and let the market prove whether time will agree with the price idea.

The other recurring rule is restraint. If the draw is not clear, do nothing. The lecture repeatedly ties that restraint to experience, specialization, and knowing when the session is too thin or unruly to force a trade.
