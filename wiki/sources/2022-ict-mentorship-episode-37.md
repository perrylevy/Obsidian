## Summary

- ICT frames the day off the daily chart, starting from a fair value gap / breaker context and treating the short-term high as the next buyside draw.
- The market is expected to keep bullish delivery until the short-term high is taken out; the intraweek structure keeps pointing to the same liquidity target.
- Non-farm payroll weeks are explicitly treated as lower-quality trading conditions, with a strong warning to stop trading by Wednesday New York session and avoid forcing entries into Friday news.
- Twitter commentary is used as a teaching tool, not a signal service: the point is to study liquidity targets in real time before they happen.
- The journaling lesson is to annotate the order block, the FVG, and the destination factually, then rehearse the setup as if you had seen it live.

## Key sections

### Daily chart read
- Start from the fair value gap / breaker context.
- Treat the short-term high as the immediate draw on liquidity.
- Stay aligned with bullish delivery until that high is run.

### NFP week caution
- Friday NFP is not a day to casually speculate.
- The Thursday / Friday session can get choppy and sporadic.
- If the week already delivered the intended objective, stand down rather than force trades.

### Real-time study, not signals
- Twitter is presented as a teaching medium.
- The exercise is to locate the fair value gap / liquidity target before the market reaches it.
- It is explicitly not a trade entry or exit service.

### Journaling workflow
- Mark the order block as the down-closed candle prior to displacement.
- Record the FVG and the target side of liquidity.
- Write the sequence in positive, fact-oriented language so the chart becomes a training record.
