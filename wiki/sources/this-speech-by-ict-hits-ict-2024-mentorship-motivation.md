# This Speech By ICT Hits! [ICT 2024 Mentorship Motivation]

The clip is a motivation-heavy ICT speech about building a trading life through patience, repetition, and self-belief rather than chasing action.

Its strongest practical message is that progress comes from narrowing to one model, accepting that the work is boring, and trusting the process long enough to build confidence from repeated execution.

The speech also warns against overtrading, social distraction, and constantly comparing yourself to other traders. The trader should stay in their lane, study the charts, and let a single method compound over time.
