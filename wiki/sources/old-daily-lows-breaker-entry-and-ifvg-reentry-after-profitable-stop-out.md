# Old Daily Lows -Breaker Entry and -IFVG Reentry After Profitable Stop Out

This clip packages a re-entry idea around old daily lows: the market breaks, an IFVG setup offers a second chance, and the profitable stop-out becomes part of the continuation logic rather than the end of the trade.

The important part is the sequence. Once the old low has been violated or reclaimed, the broken level can be treated as a breaker and the IFVG retest can become the re-entry mechanism.

This is a useful bridge example for the breaker-block and IFVG cluster because it shows how a stop-out can still leave a valid continuation path if the higher-time-frame draw is still intact.
