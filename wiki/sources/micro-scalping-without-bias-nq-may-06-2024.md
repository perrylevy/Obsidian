# Micro-Scalping Without Bias - NQ May 06, 2024

## Summary

- ICT frames the trade as a very small-range scalp taken without forcing a directional bias first: the market is allowed to show its hand, then the trader uses the available imbalance.
- The working structure is a premium / discount read around prior settlement, a nearby fair value gap, and short-term volume imbalance that can act as support or resistance inside the micro range.
- The trader adds into the move, trims risk with partials, and manages the stop under the down-close candle or the next logical invalidation line rather than trying to micromanage every tick.
- A remaining bearish FVG can later be treated as an inversion level, while a fresh open imbalance can be treated as a breakaway gap / measuring-gap style continuation cue.
- The clip is a clean example of scalping without a pre-committed thesis: read the tiny range, use the imbalance, and respect the liquidity path.
