# Trade Continuations Using Order Blocks

This clip shows how to join a move after the reversal is already confirmed. If you miss the first turn, you are not left behind; you can wait for a continuation entry instead.

The reusable rule is to let the reversal sequence form first, then use a retracement into a fair value gap, a swept level, or a fresh order block as the continuation pocket. Enter on the close through the confirming candles, place the stop beyond the invalidation, and aim for the next liquidity pool or the other side of the range.

The clip also emphasizes that SMT can replace the need for a perfect sweep in the active market, and that higher-time-frame alignment matters. The point is not to chase; it is to wait for new structure and then ride the second or third stage of the move.
