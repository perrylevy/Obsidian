# NQ Trade Breakdown - How To Pick The Right First Presented FVG

## Summary

- The source frames the day as a top-down long bias: daily FVG target higher, then a 15-minute retracement into the daily FVG / order-block region, then an RTH open that keeps selling into buy-side cleanups.
- The 9:30 candle is treated as the first presented FVG when the market opens beneath sell-side liquidity and the 9:30 candle itself forms the gap; that 9:30 gap is preferred over a later 9:31 gap.
- The same clip distinguishes between a tier 1 and tier 2 long framework using the 8:30 and midnight opening prices: beneath both opening prices is the deep-discount / tier 2 pocket, while between them is the more transitional tier 1 zone.
- The preferred entry is not always the first touch of the obvious gap; in a bullish setup, the cleaner entry may come from a deeper discount pocket or a bullish order-block / breaker refinement inside the same frame.
- If the first presented FVG is cleanly left open, that breakaway-gap behavior is treated as bullish and the market is expected to run toward buy-side liquidity and the midnight open.
- This is a useful refinement example for first-presented FVG selection and opening-price filters.
