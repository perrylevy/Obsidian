# How Do I Engage Markets When I Don't Have An Initial Bias?

## Summary

- The open is treated as an information-gathering phase, not a prediction phase: if the bias is unclear, wait for the first hour to define the working range.
- The practical workflow is to mark the 9:30 open high / low, split it at 50%, and let the upper or lower half tell you which side matters first.
- The source repeatedly points to the suspension block, the new-week opening gap, and the consequent encroachment of the opening frame as the useful higher-order references.
- If price stays below the consequential midpoint of the suspension block, the market may just continue to meander inside the range; if it reclaims the level and flips, the inversion fair value gap becomes a live continuation frame.
- The daily and hourly views are used together to confirm whether the move is truly leaving the range or merely rotating inside it.
- The explicit lesson is that waiting is not inaction; it is the correct response when you do not yet have a valid initial bias.
