## Summary

- The trade is framed around the 10:50–11:10 NY macro, with buy-side liquidity as the target and the upper half of the working range as the place price should stay (@ 0:32, @ 0:47).
- An inversion fair value gap is used as the setup trigger; the first candle back into the gap after the impulse is treated as an immediate rebalance (@ 1:38, @ 1:58).
- The review stresses that the first hour established a dealing range and that the market should remain away from the lower half of the IFVG if the long is valid (@ 4:34, @ 5:10).
- The 10:00 hour news and the high of the post-10:00 structure became the practical target for the lunch macro run (@ 7:42).
- The clip also frames the 1-hour range as a working range and treats the midpoint / re-test logic as central to execution.
- This is another framing example: the macro timing and IFVG logic are presented as a reusable way to organize existing doctrine, not as a unique standalone rule.
