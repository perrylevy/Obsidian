# ICT 2026 1st Hour Dealing Range | March 28, 2026

## Summary

- ICT explicitly distinguishes the 30-minute opening range from the full 9:30–10:30 first-hour dealing range.
- The opening range is the first 30 minutes from 9:30 to 10:00; the first-hour dealing range extends that scaffold to 10:30 and becomes the box the market is allowed to build and test.
- In the trending-day version, a shallow break below the first-hour range can be a test rather than a true failure; the close below a wick inside the range is the acceptance clue, and the first presented FVG after the break becomes the next short array.
- ICT uses the midpoint / equilibrium of the first-hour range as a practical checkpoint, and he shows how to project the range lower as a standard-deviation style guide once the box is established.
- The clip also frames 10:30–11:30 as a sweet spot where lunch-macro retracement can begin once the first-hour range is complete.
