# NQ Trade Breakdown - Reclaimed IFVG Entry With Trump Volatility (Live Account)

## Summary

- The trade uses a daily discount-wick / upper-half bullish frame: price stays bullish while the upper half remains intact and CE acts as the decision line.
- The 15-minute FVG in that upper-half daily scaffold is the key support array, and the entry is a reclaimed IFVG rather than a blind first-touch FVG.
- The 9:30 open behaves like a Judas-swing-style trap lower under bullish bias, which sets up the reclaim instead of invalidating the thesis.
- After the gap is reclaimed, price is bought on the retrace; the logic is the same IFVG continuation / reclaim family already in the wiki, just shown in a live NQ example.
- The target is buy-side liquidity / relative-equal-high style objective, with the session's opening chop treated as news and timing distortion rather than a reason to overtrade.
- Trump volatility delays the clean move and creates extra chop, so patience and selective timing matter more than constant participation.
- This is best treated as a reinforcing example of the existing IFVG + Judas swing + AM-window stack, not a new standalone model.
