# Internal & External Liquidity Model - ICT Concepts

## Summary

- Defines **internal liquidity (IRL)** as a Fair Value Gap — a three-candle pattern where wick of candle 1 and wick of candle 3 do not overlap, signalling aggressive displacement (@ 0:37).
- Defines **external liquidity (ERL)** as swing highs and swing lows — i.e. buyside and sellside liquidity (@ 0:58, @ 1:17).
- Core relationship: price oscillates **external → internal → external**. When price sweeps ERL but cannot displace through it, it is likely to retrace into IRL (FVG), respect the FVG, then deliver back to ERL (@ 1:28).
- The model: pick a higher time frame draw (IRL or ERL), drop down a time frame, then on the lower time frame require a [[deferred/timing/killzones]] + [[concepts/liquidity-sweep]] + [[concepts/market-structure-shift]] + [[concepts/fair-value-gap]] entry, targeting **2R** (@ 1:56).
- Intraday preference is **daily / hourly / 5-minute** nesting; higher time frame swing version is **monthly / daily / hourly** (@ 12:26, @ 15:47).
- Pairs well with discount/premium, SMT, standard deviation projections, mean threshold, and consequent encroachment (@ 0:17 description, @ 8:06, @ 10:04).
- Example 3 introduces **low resistance liquidity** — lined-up previous day lows as a draw (@ 8:06).
- Example 4 uses previous month's low taken out, then **equal lows** formed and run as ERL on the daily (@ 10:47).
- Example 5 frames a **market maker buy model** on the 15-minute, entering a fair value gap near discount that lines up with **breaker block** bodies (@ 14:23, @ 14:38).
