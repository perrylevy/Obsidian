# 2025 Lecture Series - Forex & NQ Review 02/05/2025

## Summary

- ICT frames the FX environment as a high-resistance liquidity-run condition: the tape is fuzzy, overlapping, and prone to overshoot, so tight stops are a bad fit.
- For Forex, the lesson is that you cannot look at one pair in isolation; EURUSD, GBPUSD, and the dollar index need to be read together as a correlated triad.
- The NASDAQ example uses a discount opening-range gap: because the market opens below the prior regular-trading-hours settlement, the 9:30 open becomes the opening-range-gap low and the midpoint becomes the first reaction checkpoint.
- The session then behaves like a delivery sequence: initial rally into the gap, sell-side raid, then the higher-probability upside continuation once the lower-side liquidity has been cleaned up.
- ICT explicitly ties the execution to precise price levels, not loose zones, and shows the entry nested under first-presented FVG / gap logic with relative equal highs as the later upside draw.
