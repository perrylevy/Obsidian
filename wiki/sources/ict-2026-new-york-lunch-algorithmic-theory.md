# ICT 2026 New York Lunch Algorithmic Theory | March 11, 2026

## Summary

- ICT frames New York lunch as a distinct midday window and keeps the trader away from classic support/resistance language; the useful object is the imbalance that forms immediately before the liquidity raid.
- The core rule is to carry forward the inefficiency right before the lunch sweep into the next day; if price trades back into that zone, it can become the short-side reference, and the reverse applies for longs.
- The clip ties that lunch logic to yesterday's regular-trading-hours opening-range gap and its consequent encroachment, turning the gap midpoint into the practical decision level.
- It also uses "event horizon" more broadly as the midpoint between two liquidity pools, not only as an opening-gap midpoint.
- The lesson reinforces first-presented-FVG logic by pointing to the first fair-value gap immediately before the liquidity take as the array to track forward.
