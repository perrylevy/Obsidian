# This Very Simple FVG Scalping Strategy is all you need to be Profitable in 2026

## Summary

- The clip reduces the setup to three basics: recognize a swing high/low, identify an FVG, and wait for price to rebalance the inefficiency before entering.
- The default entry is 100% of the FVG; if the gap is large, 50% is acceptable, but the speaker warns that the deeper fill can be missed entirely.
- Stops are placed under the first candle that created the FVG, or under the swing high/low when the structure demands a safer stop.
- The model is explicitly session-aware: London is the preferred volatile window, the first 15 minutes of the NY open are avoided, and the speaker usually waits for 9:45 or 9:50 before engaging.
- The clip repeatedly says to trade only when price is expanding with a clear angle; flat, slow, or consolidative delivery is treated as a no-trade environment.
- On the NY examples, the 9:30 open can create the initial bias, but the practical target is often a prior gap / NOG and the clean FVG that took the swing high or low.
- SMT divergence is used as an aggressive refinement when one correlated asset has already tagged the same FVG or liquidity pool.
