# ICT 2026 Entries & Drills | April 15, 2026

## Summary

- ICT frames the session as a deliberate drill day: the chart is messy, the tape is fast, and the point is to practice reading price action rather than force live-money execution.
- The opening read uses the 9:30 opening price, relative equal highs/lows, FVG / IFVG structure, and consequent encroachment to decide whether the market can cleanly expand or is still too sloppy to trust.
- The practical drill format is explicit: use a 15-second chart on top of the one-minute context, keep the target small, and treat 10–15 handles as a reasonable participation objective for repetition.
- ICT repeatedly says the goal is outcome-indifference: use demo/paper, record the stopout or completion, and log how the market felt instead of trying to be right.
- Cross-market comparison matters too: ES and MNQ can decouple intraday, and that divergence can help confirm whether the current push is continuation, stalling, or late-day manipulation.
- The source is not a new model; it is a high-leverage execution lesson about turning a bad open into a practice lab.
