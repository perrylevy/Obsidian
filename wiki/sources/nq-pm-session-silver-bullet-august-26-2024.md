# NQ PM Session Silver Bullet - August 26, 2024

## Summary

- The PM Silver Bullet is a 2:00–3:00 p.m. New York local-time model.
- ICT frames it as a recurring 60-minute opportunity window that can be traded with time-and-price discipline.
- The setup depends on draw on liquidity, a confirming FVG / order-block-type structure, and a target that offers sufficient framework.
- The lecture emphasizes that the window repeats every trading day, but the trade is only valid when the pattern actually forms.
