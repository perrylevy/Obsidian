# 2025 Lecture Series - Trading In High Resistance Liquidity Run Conditions With My Lunch Macro

## Summary

- The clip describes a high-resistance day: the market stays tight, keeps stopping the trader out, and refuses to deliver clean continuation without patience and smaller size.
- The lunch macro is framed as a target window after the first hour. Once the 10:00 range is in place, the 11:30–1:30 pulse can become the next objective rather than a random intraday wiggle.
- The trade is managed through inversion fair value gaps, order blocks, and partials because volatility is fast and the day is not giving easy follow-through.
- The main lesson is adaptive execution: when the day is stubborn, the trader must reduce aggression, protect open profit, and let the macro window do the work.
