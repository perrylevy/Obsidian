# 2025 Lecture Series - SMC Opening Range Gaps

## Summary

- The lecture draws a hard line between regular-trading-hours opening-range gaps and electronic-hours new-day / new-week opening gaps.
- The opening range gap is measured from prior 4:14 p.m. settlement to the new 9:30 a.m. RTH open; that distinction matters because RTH is the only valid frame for the opening-range-gap rules in this clip.
- Large premium or discount gaps are measured with a Fibonacci from settlement to open, then graded by quadrants and consequent encroachment.
- The lecture repeatedly says that extremely large gaps often do not fully fill immediately; they can leave a liquidity void and only rebalance partially.
- The practical takeaway is to read the gap size first, then decide whether the market is likely to continue, partially retrace, or rebook to the midpoint / upper quadrant.
