# Holding Trades

Holding trades is the execution skill of staying with a winning position through normal retracements without panic-closing early.

The practical lesson is that holding is not just about conviction in the entry. It is about having a pre-committed exit plan, trusting your edge, and not letting anxiety rewrite the trade after you are already in profit.

## Core idea

A trader can have a valid entry and still lose the trade's full potential by failing to endure the middle part of the move. The clip frames this as a nervous-system problem more than a chart-reading problem.

The patient-trading clip adds a complementary rule: patience is not only about holding once in profit; it is also about waiting calmly before entry and not forcing trades out of boredom.

## Practical tools

Common supports for holding trades include:

- setting the stop and target before entry,
- avoiding mid-trade plan changes,
- using partials when necessary to reduce emotional pressure,
- zooming out the chart when the lower timeframe becomes too noisy,
- and using alerts so you do not feel forced to babysit every candle.
