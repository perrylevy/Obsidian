# Event Horizon

Event Horizon is the midpoint between two adjacent New Day Opening Gaps or New Week Opening Gaps. In the MNQ walkthrough, it is explicitly defined as the midpoint between those opening gaps and used as a meaningful reference level ([[sources/mnq-pm-silver-bullet-walkthrough]] @ 4:51). Lecture 16 of the 2024 mentorship extends that same midpoint idea to a live NQ opening-gap stack, where the midpoint between adjacent NDOG / NWOG structures is treated as a black-hole draw when the gaps are close together ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-16]] @ 30:05, @ 31:21, @ 31:39).

## Core idea

The event horizon is the midpoint that sits between two gap structures. It functions as a reference line where price behavior can be interpreted in relation to both gaps at once.

The 03/11/2026 lunch algorithmic-theory clip uses the same midpoint logic between two liquidity pools, showing that "event horizon" can also be read as a temporary draw between live liquidity pools rather than only as an opening-gap midpoint ([[sources/ict-2026-new-york-lunch-algorithmic-theory]] @ 15:02, @ 15:51).

The 02/13/2025 Telegram execution uses the same language in a more literal intraday sense: the Asian-range low becomes the event-horizon style level the trader is working around when the opening-range structure and PPI move are mapped out ([[sources/nq-live-execution-using-analysis-i-shared-in-telegram-02132025]] @ 18:29).

## Why it matters

This concept matters because it gives a named midpoint to the opening-gap family:

- it links New Day and New Week opening gaps,
- it gives the market a shared reference between those structures,
- and it appears in the PM price-continuum walkthrough as part of the active delivery map.

## Practical use

Use the event horizon when:

- two adjacent opening gaps are in play,
- you want the midpoint reference between them,
- or you need a simple level to anchor the delivery between the two gaps.
