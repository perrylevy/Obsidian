# Institutional Orderflow Entry Drill

Institutional orderflow entry drill is the signature where a bullish FVG is reclaimed, but price fails to trade back down to its consequent encroachment / midpoint. In the continuity lecture, that failure to revisit CE is the setup’s tell ([[sources/2025-price-delivery-continuum]] @ 04:38, @ 06:36, [[concepts/consequent-encroachment]]).

## Core idea

The pattern is not just “an FVG filled.” It is:

- a bullish FVG on the chart,
- price reclaiming it,
- and then price not getting back down to CE on the next pass.

That makes the midpoint the key decision line.

## Why it matters

This is a higher-timeframe bias confluence concept, not a generic pattern-trade.

It belongs inside the broader price-delivery framework because the market is being evaluated by how it respects or fails to respect the active delivery midpoint.

## Practical use

Use it when:

- a bullish FVG has already been reclaimed,
- the midpoint becomes the level to watch,
- and continuation lower would require a clean trade back through CE that never comes.

Lecture 12 of the 2024 mentorship adds the broader trade-management framing: when higher-timeframe bias is bullish, the institutional orderflow entry drill can appear at or below the 9:30 opening price, and the trader should think of it as a low-risk buy that may be held far beyond the morning session if the daily candle is expected to close near its high ([[sources/2024-mentorship-lecture-12]]).

The 08/27/2024 premarket NQ clip uses a similar patience rule from the short side: wait for the delivery shift, then add only if the price returns to the opening / imbalance area instead of forcing the move early ([[sources/premarket-nq-ob-to-discount-ndog-august-27-2024]]).
