# V-Shape Reversal

A **V-shape reversal** is a sharp probe-and-reject response that turns quickly after a sweep or failed continuation. In the IFVG family, it is the ideal visual companion to a valid inversion: price probes a level, rejects it cleanly, and expands in the new direction rather than chopping sideways.

## Core idea

The useful version is not just any bounce. It is a quick shift that shows:

- a sweep or push into a key level,
- a decisive rejection,
- and a clean expansion leg away from the level.

That shape helps distinguish a valid inversion from consolidation masquerading as a reversal.
