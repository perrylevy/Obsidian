# Patience

Patience is the ability to wait for the market to offer a real opportunity instead of forcing action out of boredom, urgency, or a need to feel busy.

The practical lesson is that a patient trader treats waiting as part of the job. They stay watchful, accept that they are not in control of outcome, and do not mistake inactivity for laziness when the setup is not there.

## Core idea

In trading, patience is an active skill. It means resisting the urge to jump, resisting the urge to make things happen, and staying centered until the market shows what it is willing to give.

The clip frames boredom as the main danger signal: boredom can trigger novelty-seeking, overconfidence, and action for the sake of action. Patience is what prevents that spiral.

The same lesson shows up in the "Stop Giving It All Back" and "Millionaire Traders Build Unshakeable Trading Discipline" clips: patience is not passive waiting, it is the discipline to stop chasing stimulation and to let structure, observation, and rules do the work ([[sources/how-to-stop-giving-it-all-back]], [[sources/how-millionaire-traders-build-unshakeable-trading-discipline]]).

The "This Speech By ICT Hits!" motivation clip makes the same point in a more expansive way: patience is what lets a trader sit still, study, and let one model compound instead of chasing other people's activity or trying to force a trade every day ([[sources/this-speech-by-ict-hits-ict-2024-mentorship-motivation]]).

## What helps

Useful supports include:

- keeping the playbook narrow,
- defining what a valid setup looks like in advance,
- treating waiting as normal rather than as wasted time,
- and accepting that not every session needs a trade.
