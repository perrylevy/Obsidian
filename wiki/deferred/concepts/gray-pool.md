# Gray Pool

A source-specific nickname for a lower **discount fill** inside a broader bullish context.

## Meaning

In the May 1 NQ review, ICT uses "gray pool" to point at a lower pocket inside discount where price can still offer a cleaner long than the first visible FVG touch ([[sources/2025-lecture-series-nq-may-01-2025-review]] @ 11:59, @ 12:28, @ 13:00).

It behaves like a refined entry pocket:
- below the working premium/discount midpoint,
- inside the same broader bullish frame,
- and often cleaner than chasing the first obvious inversion level.

## Use

If the doctrinal anchor is an [[concepts/inversion-fair-value-gap|IFVG]] or bullish order-block structure, the actual fill may come from the lower gray pool instead of the first touch of the higher array.

Treat this as a practical label for a deeper discount fill, not as a separate canonical ICT doctrine.
