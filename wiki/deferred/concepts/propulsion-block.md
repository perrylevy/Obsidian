# Propulsion Block

A **propulsion block** is a candle body that acts as a directional support/resistance reference after displacement, often treated as a sensitivity zone inside a larger dealing-range or order-block framework.

## ICT-direct support

In the 03/09/2026 market commentary, ICT explicitly calls the follow-on body a propulsion block and says it is an order block. He then adds that the setup is only valid if price has not breached mean threshold of the composite order-block range; once the opening price is breached, the structure is validated and retracement should stay in the proper half of the gap ([[sources/ict-2026-market-commentary-march-09-2026]] @ 26:33, @ 26:47, @ 27:13, @ 27:33, @ 31:35, @ 32:42, @ 32:55).

## EP8 framing

EP8 independently reaches a similar continuation idea: a down-closed candle formed after the original order-block reaction can act as a bullish propulsion block, and its body is graded internally so the **upper quadrant** becomes the sensitive reaction area for bullish continuation ([[sources/ep8-the-daily-range]] @ 4:54).

## Practical use

- It is not the original order block; it is the follow-on body that helps propel the move.
- Traders watch the body and its opening price as a refined reaction zone once the initial order-block logic has already been confirmed.
- In the EP8 examples, price repeatedly respects the propulsion-block body before continuing higher ([[sources/ep8-the-daily-range]] @ 4:54, @ 9:05, @ 19:06).
