# Venom Model

A bullish opening-session deferred-entry model. The May 12, 2025 review describes it as a way to let the market run sellside liquidity below the opening low, then buy at the opening price or lower once the market proves it has turned back up.

The model sits close to [[models/first-presentation-model]] and [[models/turtle-soup]]:
- it uses the opening-session context,
- it waits for a stop-run / false-break move lower,
- and it wants the bullish displacement to leave a usable array behind it.

## Working rule

- Start with a bullish higher-time-frame bias and a discount objective.
- Wait for a single-candle or quick run below sellside liquidity.
- Require the reversal to leave a clear "cibby first and bissy leaving it" structure in the source's language: a bearish push first, then the bullish leaving candle.
- Enter at the opening price or lower; if price is above that threshold, the setup is no longer the Venom entry.
- Use the first presented FVG / inversion FVG logic to frame the continuation after the raid.
- Treat the move as a deferred entry, not a breakout chase.

## Why it exists

The point is to avoid buying the premium open or forcing the first move. The market may spend a long time in sideways distortion, but the Venom model only needs a clean sellside raid, a validated turn, and a disciplined entry price.

## Relationship to other pages

- A variant of [[models/first-presentation-model]] when the opening session is choppy and the first valid buy is deferred.
- Closely related to [[models/turtle-soup]] because the setup uses a sweep / false-break structure.
- Supported by [[timing/first-presented-fvg]] because the first valid 1-minute gap still anchors the session once 9:31+ prints.
- Often framed with [[concepts/inversion-fair-value-gap]] and [[concepts/volume-imbalance]] once the reversal starts to prove itself.
